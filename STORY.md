# Story spine for the Step-1 manuscript (binding for all writers)

## The program
Construct the one-loop (later two-loop) quantum BV structure of 4d N=4 SYM with ghosts for the whole superconformal algebra psl(4|4), inside Costello's formalism [C07, CG2], Euclidean, index-free Weyl spinors [ESW], with a UV boundary epsilon only. Two kinds of output:
(i) the quantized equivariant interaction (the action side: counterterms, QME);
(ii) for every local operator O, the quantum observable O[infty] and its BV transformation along every generator of psl(4|4): along the dilatation this is the anomalous dimension; along Q and S it is the quantum-corrected supersymmetry; along K it is the primary condition.

## Why it is subtle (the three points the user stressed)
1. Operators. The insertion of a composite operator diverges. Costello's construction asks for a family O(eps) with lim_{eps->0} partial_delta W(P(eps,infty), I - I^CT[eps] + delta O(eps)) existing. Ideally O(eps) could be any functional. At scale infinity, however, K_infty = 0 and P(0,infty) is a contracting homotopy for Q on decaying sections: away from finitely many zero-mode classes, every Q-closed functional is Q-exact. A general functional O(eps) therefore carries no cohomological information. The content of an operator is that O(eps) is LOCAL: O(eps) = O - partial_delta I^CT[eps] with local coefficients in span{eps^{-1}, log eps}. Locality plus existence of the eps -> 0 limit fix the singular part uniquely; the finite part is the scheme. Every statement "Q O[infty] = Y[infty]" is meaningful because Y is required to be local, and the anomalous dimension is a class in the cohomology of LOCAL functionals.
2. Counterterms. Theorem B fixes the singular part I^CT[eps] uniquely. The regulator (cutoff eps on every edge) breaks gauge invariance and superconformal symmetry: I^CT[eps] contains eps^{-1} mass terms that are not Q-closed. The QME, transported to the UV boundary, then forces FINITE local counterterms. Their choice is constrained by the QME but not unique: this is the renormalization condition, and it is part of the definition of the quantum theory and of every operator.
3. UV boundary only. No IR scale appears. Every singular or anomalous coefficient is computed from edges pinned at t = eps: L_eps = (Q^GF (x) 1) K_eps edges (from eps d/deps P(eps,infty) = -eps L_eps: singular coefficients, scaling) and K_eps edges (from Q P(eps,infty) = K_eps: QME defects, BV transformations).
