1087 [local-c4ce10f2-6054-4d7d-8f8b-18f516191e7d] paragraph  | <p>Let's consider the circuits that violate the Laman condition with exactly one fewer edge. These graphs have an important property: if we short (take the limit in which the resistances in a subgraph go to zero together) a Laman subgraph of it, it will then be a Laman graph. 1088 [local-0f3869e2-383d-4993-b3da-60015c5e6fce] paragraph  | <p>We cannot do the full integral of <a href="#eq-sc-kernel">(B.92)</a> on these graphs, since the dimension of the space of \(y\) (satisfying the current conservation law) is less than the dimension of the resistor ratio \(\mathbb{RP}^{|\Gamma_1|-1}_+\) by one. 1090 [ch7-closed-current-degree] equation  | |\Gamma_1|=2|\Gamma_0|-4,\qquad 2h_1(\Gamma)=2(|\Gamma_1|-|\Gamma_0|+1)=|\Gamma_1|-2,\qquad d^2y_e=dy_{e,+\dot+}\wedge dy_{e,+\dot-}.

1091 [ch7-closed-current-proof] paragraph  | <p><em>Proof.</em> Let \(T\) be a spanning tree, as in <a href="#eq-sc-kernel">(B.92)</a>. Differentiating Kirchhoff’s current law at fixed injected currents gives</p>

1092 [ch7-closed-current-kcl] equation  | \sum_{\substack{e(0)=v\\\mathrm{outgoing\ edges}}}dy_{e,+\dot a}-\sum_{\substack{e(1)=v\\\mathrm{incoming\ edges}}}dy_{e,+\dot a}=0.

1094 [ch7-closed-current-repeat] equation  | dy_{e,+\dot a}\wedge\bigwedge_{e^{\prime}\notin T}(-d^2y_{e^{\prime}})=0\qquad(e\in\Gamma_1).
