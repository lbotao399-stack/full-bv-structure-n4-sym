1. Sign rule and normalisation (fixed by gates, no free constant)
 Per configuration, on top of the raw chain T = L_1 S h_1 ... L_n S h_n S L_0 S and the intrinsic pair sign (-1)^{|Xa|+|Xb|}:
     (-1)^{n(<sigma) + |S1(x)| (n(<s) + [s<sigma])} (-1)^{[sigma = L_0]},   GLOBAL = +1,
 n(<j) the number of odd outputs before position j along the chain from X_a, |S1(x)| = 0 (odd x), 1 (even x).  This is
 Def qs-defect (ii) relative to Lemma c10-sign; (-1)^{[sigma=L_0]} removes the factor that S L_0 S produces when the line at
 X_b is a special line (it joins slots of opposite parity).  Evidence:
  (a) E0-partC: with the special line restricted to the lines not adjacent to the S block, the even rule reproduces the
      Part-C engine (rule R2 x vertex-vertex sign -1) exactly;
  (b) G-reg (bosonic): along Delta, all placements, on the scalar bilinears: Upsilon^0_Delta = 40/3 O (traceless),
      beta = -8 (trace), position part 2 x^mu (-1/3) d_mu O: the first row of Thm c9-D1 and Thm c9-P1 exactly;
  (c) G-reg (fermionic, pair by pair, off shell): Upsilon^0_Delta = 4 lc + C^(2),0_Delta and Upsilon_{P_b} = C^(2)_{P_b} for
      (A_perp, lam_+), (lam_+, lam_+), (lam_+, lam_-), (A_perp, lam_-) with odd outputs (n(<sigma), n(<s) active);
  (d) GS: reversal X_a <-> X_b along xi gives one uniform ratio +-1 per pair type; without (-1)^{[sigma=L_0]} it fails;
  (e) G-R1: s Q_1(O) = -L_1^(0)({S^(1)(xi), O}) exactly on the (c, lam) sector of the bilinears (odd x vs arity zero).

3. Conventions recap
 Euclidean R^4, weight e^{S/hbar}, per-edge Schwinger cutoff t_e >= eps, special edge t in [0, eps] with numerator -|l+q|^2
 Khat (= +|l+q|^2 Kn of cq_core), T = infinity (generic momenta), no dimensional regularisation, no IR regulator.
 Engine directions M = 0..3 (V), 4..9 (W_R^perp); lam_+ := S_+ (x) W = +1 eigenspace of rho(e0 e1 e2 e3) on Sigma in this
 engine (as in the two-letter and Part-C engines A); xi in S_+ (x) W generic: sum of the integer basis vectors with
 coefficients (1,2,-1,1,0,1,-2,1).  Positions: z^mu -> -i d/dr_mu, z^mu z^nu -> -d^2/dr_mu dr_nu, r injected at the S block
 and absorbed by omega, exact Lagrange interpolation (the eps^0 coefficient is a polynomial in r).
 Two-component reading: Rem qs-twocomp; the engine's 16-component basis is related to it by the spinor map of
 step2/composite_qsk/revise.nosync/ferm_map (not used numerically here); the twist letters are built basis-free from the
 Cartan weights (qa_twist.py docstring).
