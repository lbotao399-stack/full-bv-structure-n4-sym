1. Objects and method
 (a) Special-edge composite chain (Def def:cc-sp).  Letter a - [lines and blocks] - letter b with k vertices of I (cubic
     blocks with one output, quartic blocks with two outputs, as in the two-letter engine), the internal vertex of S^(1)(x)
     inserted as a two-leg block between two lines P (the composite line K_x = the two segments A-A and A*|_V-c with the
     vertex between them), one special edge (line matrix +|l+q|^2 Kn, Kn = -Khat of r2_core: the field-antifield
     components of K_0; Schwinger time in [0, eps]) not adjacent to the internal vertex, all other lines P(eps, infinity).
     Chain matrix T = L_1 S h_1 ... L_n S h_n S L_0 S (two-letter engine convention), rows: slot of X_a, columns: slot of X_b.
     Internal vertex blocks (colour (t_in, t_out)):
        P_b : int(d_b A|_V, A*|_V):     H[A_M, A*_M] = i (k_j)_b, H[A*_M, A_M] = i (k_l)_b, M < 4
        s_0 : int 2(A|_V, A*|_V)
        s_mu: int 2(d_mu A|_V, A*|_V) with the injected momentum r = t e_mu at the vertex (absorbed by omega).
     Along Delta (Lemma lem:cc-kernels (iv), Prop prop:cc-position): position part = s_mu graph at r = 0 = 2 C_{P_mu};
     translation-invariant part C^(2),0_Delta = s_0 graph + sum_mu (-i) d/dr_mu (s_mu graph), the r-derivative taken
     exactly as the t^1 coefficient (all polynomials truncated at t^2; gate GE2 compares with exact finite differences).
 (b) Loop integral and finite part (Thm thm:cc-simplex).  E = k + 2 Schwinger times (the segments are separate edges),
     Gaussian integral at the centre c(alpha) = -sum alpha_e q_e, Phi = sum alpha_e q_e.q_e - c.c (bilinear dot: complex
     momenta by analytic continuation), F_0 = sum_j (Lap^j N)(c)/(j! 4^j) (-Phi)^{j+2-E}/(j+2-E)!, and
        16 pi^2 [eps^0] = int_{R_s} F_0 log(min_{f != s} alpha_f / alpha_s) d alpha,
        16 pi^2 [eps^-m] = (1/m) int_{R_s} [(min_{f != s} alpha_f)^m - alpha_s^m] F_m d alpha.
     Exact integration: R_s = union over the orders of the other edges of the simplices {alpha_s <= alpha_o0 <= ...}; in
     barycentric coordinates mu of such a simplex log(alpha_o0/alpha_s) = log(1 + c mu_1/mu_0), c = E/(E-1); for a monomial
     mu^b, int mu^b g(mu_0/(mu_0+mu_1)) = Dirichlet(b) x E_Beta(b_0+1,b_1+1)[g], with E[log x] = H_{b0} - H_{b0+b1+1} and
     int_0^1 x^n log(c + (1-c)x) dx by the recursion R_m = (1/d)(1/m - c R_{m-1}), R_0 = -(log c)/d, d = 1 - c.
     Hence every finite part lies in Q + Q log(E/(E-1)) per chain: basis {1, log2, log3, log5, log7} overall
     (E = 3: log(3/2), 4: log(4/3), 5: log(5/4)).  No log eps occurs (the two cutoff shapes have equal log coefficients).
 (c) Colour: the internal vertex and the special edge carry no ad factor; the colour word is ad_{Y_r}...ad_{Y_1} of the
     outputs, planar adjacent rule (-1)^r value/2 units u (Lemma c10-colour (iv)); for two-letter traces both orders
     (as ea_ops); graded cyclic moves and odd legs in cq_gops (sign of the permutation of the odd legs).
 (d) Families: evaluation on legs (outputs as plane waves), fits onto the two-field bases with exact sympy least squares
     and an exact residual check.  On shell: complex null Gaussian momenta; gaugino legs in the kernel of
     sum_mu q_mu sb[mu] within the right chirality with nonzero overlap with the letter; gluon legs e.q = 0.

2. Conventions fixed by gates (no free constant)
 - Global normalisation +1 (Z_x = chain value / vertex scales, units hbar/16pi^2): GC3 bilinears give at once
   C_{P_b} = -1/3 u d_b O and C^0_Delta = -8/3 u O (traceless and trace part, real and null momenta) -- two numbers, one
   normalisation, no fitted constant.
 - Koszul rule R2: the internal vertex and the special edge are odd relative to the odd outputs before them in the chain
   (sign (-1)^{#odd outputs before the vertex + #odd outputs before the special edge}), not relative to each other (the
   permutation rule including their mutual order makes the bilinear antisymmetric in the outputs).  Intrinsic pair sign
   (-1)^{|X_a|+|X_b|} as in ea_kern.kernel (delta-vertex Hessian).  With R2 + intrinsic sign the su(1|1) densities are
   universal on every ordered pair; with the opposite intrinsic sign the odd pairs (Z, psi) get +4/3 instead of -4/3.
 - Graded symmetry (gate GS): the chain from X_b with reversed outputs equals (ratio) the chain from X_a with exactly the
   ratio of the two-letter log kernels, for every tested pair, including odd outputs and mixed chiralities, PROVIDED the
   chains in which the special edge joins two vertices of I (not a letter) carry the direction sign
   (-1)^{[special edge before the internal vertex]} (convention VV_a).  The overall sign of these "vertex-vertex"
   configurations is not fixed by any gate run here: results carry C^(2) = (letter part) + kappa (vertex-vertex part),
   kappa = +1 (VV_a), -1 (VV_b), 0 (VV_0); GS holds for each kappa.  They occur only for pairs with a letter A|_V or c
   (the internal vertex must sit next to a letter when the special edge is between the two vertices of a k = 2 chain).
   F0, F1, F2 contain none; in F3 (gluons) the vertex-vertex part vanishes on shell (N = 0, 1), so all family results
   are kappa independent.  Off shell (tables) both parts are recorded separately.

3. Results (units u; exact)
 - Bilinears / planar scalars (F0): the (A_perp, A_perp) two-site density is, off shell and for all SO(6) components,
   C^0 = -4/3 (Y_i Y_j), C_{P_beta} = -1/6 i beta.(q_1 + q_2)(Y_i Y_j): no trace term, no exchange term.  With the
   one-body term of Lemma cc-onebody, Xi_Delta = 0 per letter at theta = -1/3: D_1 = 4 lc^natural on the scalar sector,
   su(2) spectra L <= 8 = Heisenberg (predictions P6).
 - Twist two (F1), N <= 4: sum C^(2),0_Delta = -2/3 (4+N) x identity on the two-field part (exact, rational, pairs (Z,Z)
   with two outputs plus (A_n, Z) with one output); C_{P_n} = -1/3 u d_n exactly; generic beta (N <= 2): = -1/3 u d_beta
   plus terms proportional to q_1^2, q_2^2 (free equations of motion); no q_1.q_2 or exchange structure.  D_1 at
   theta = -1/3 has eigenvalues -32 h(S), S even <= N (= -8u E_H, E_H = 4h(S)).
 - su(1|1) (F2): densities on shell: d0 = -4/3 and dP = -1/6 (q1+q2) on the same word, 0 on the exchanged word, for all
   ordered pairs (Z,Z), (Z,psi), (psi,Z), (psi,psi); lc^natural density -2(1 - Pi) (graded); Xi^0 density 0; graded
   cyclic spectra of D_1/(-8u), L <= 6, = harmonic action (ha_su11.json).
 - Fermion sl(2) (F2), N <= 3: log kernel eigenvalues -8 h(N+1) (validates the graded reading), sum C^0 = -2/3(4+N),
   C_{P_n} = -1/3 u d_n exactly, D_1 eigenvalues -32 h(n+1), n odd.
 - Gluon sl(2) (F3), N <= 1: lc^natural -12 (N = 0), sum C^0 = -2/3 (6+N), C_{P_n} = -1/3 u d_n, D_1 = -32 h(n+2); gauge
   Ward identity (one longitudinal leg) holds for the letter part and the vertex-vertex part separately.
 - Density universality on shell (general polarisations, letter vectors of definite chirality): for every ordered pair of
   A_perp, lambda_+, lambda_- (including the mixed chiralities lambda_+ lambda_-), the planar two-site density is
   d0 = -4/3 (X_a X_b) and dP_beta = -1/6 i beta.(q_1+q_2) (X_a X_b) on the same word and 0 on the exchanged word.
   (A first version of the test used random 16-component letter vectors; for lambda the plain dot product pairs S_+ (x) W
   with S_+ (x) W only, so such a vector is a mixture of a lambda_+ and a lambda_- letter and the "exchanged word" of one
   piece sits in the "same word" of the other: the test, not the kernel, was wrong.  Weight-basis checks of the 64
   bilinears tr(lambda_+ lambda_-) on shell give exactly -8/3 O.)
 - D_1 and P_1 with the free directions symbolic (results.json D1_P1_symbolic): on every family run,
   D_1 = 4 lc^natural - u sum_a (theta_{d_a} + 1/3) v.X_a and P_1 = -u sum_a (thetaP_{d_a} + 1/3) d_b X_a on cohomology
   (sector d_a of the letter X_a; kappa directions s-exact), i.e. Xi_Delta = -(1/3 + theta) u v. for equal theta
   (Theorem thm:cc-P7 (i)); theta = -1/3 gives D_1 = 4 lc^natural and the harmonic-action spectra, thetaP = -1/3 gives
   P_1 = 0 on cohomology.
