2. Finding on the sign of the Wess-Zumino identity (G-sign, G-WZ1)
 In the mode model (manuscript bracket of Def c1-bracket, Q = {S_free,-}) the derivation rule is
     Lambda{a,b} = -{Lambda a, b} - (-1)^{|a|} {a, Lambda b}      (checked: Q{dsds,T1} = -{Q dsds, T1}),
 the rule of the bracket {f,g}_C = Delta(fg) - Delta(f)g - (-1)^{|f|} f Delta(g), whose symmetry is
 {g,f} = (-1)^{|f||g|}{f,g}.  Lemma at-sformulas (ii) was derived with the standard odd-bracket rule
 Lambda{a,b} = {Lambda a,b} + (-1)^{|a|+1}{a,Lambda b} (bracket (-1)^{|f|}{f,g}_C).  With the bracket of Def c1-bracket,
 s^2 = 0 gives for degree-1 cochains (sG)^(1)(x) = Lambda G^(1)(x) + {G^(0),S(x)}, so the arity-one WZ identity reads
     Lambda Ob^(1)(x) = -{Ob^(0), S(x)}.
 The computed Ob^(1)(xi)|_2 satisfies exactly this (also directly: d/dtau of Lambda_tau Ob[I + tau S(xi)] = 0), and
 violates the printed sign.  Formula (i) of the Lemma (degree 0) and all solvability statements are unaffected.
