4. FINDINGS
 F1. Lemma at-sformulas (ii)/(iii) (Wess-Zumino) as printed, Lambda G^(1)(x) = {G^(0),S(x)}, has the wrong sign: the
     cochain bracket of Def c2-dce gives (sG)^(1)(x) = Lambda G^(1)(x) + {G^(0),S(x)} for a degree-1 cochain, and the
     computed Ob^(1)(xi)|_2 satisfies Q Ob1 = -{Ob0, S(xi)} exactly (Lambda{F0,T1} = -{Lambda F0,T1} checked).  The
     forcing argument of the proof assumed Lambda{a,b} = {Lambda a,b} + ...; with this bracket the first term has sign -1.
 F2. eq at-pole and Thm c9-quadratic (ii) are consistent only with the normalisation of section 2 (raw = -theory for
     even x); the theory's sentence "{F,S(Delta)} = -v.F" (Lemma c9-SDelta) corresponds to v.F := -F'[v.phi].
 F3. (A*_perp, A*_perp) vanishes identically (odd fields, symmetric pairings), so there is no degree -2 functional at
     weight 5 in the perp block; the independence of [S(P_b)_perp] in Thm at-theta holds trivially.
 F4. The raw (Delta,P_b) cycle sums carry log 2, log 3; they cancel in the class: [Ob2(Delta,P_b)|_2] = -(2/3)u[S(P_b)].
