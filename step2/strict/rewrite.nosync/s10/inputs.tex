\begin{remark}[The input of the planar scalar sector]
\label{rem:c10-inputs}
Proposition \ref{prop:c10-scalarsector} derives the sector from the bilinear values of Chapter 9. Its input is the table of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-D1}, the master formula on the bilinears, which determines the two coefficients $8$ of the composite triangle of Proposition \ref{prop:c9-triangle} (Remark \ref{rem:c9-twoeval}); the composite terms of the pairs enter through that table.
\end{remark}
