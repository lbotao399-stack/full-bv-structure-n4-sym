\begin{remark}[The data that complete the one-loop components]
\label{rem:c10-next}
The following data are given by the graphs of this chapter and are the next computations. The composite terms of the pairs beyond the planar scalar sector, first on the twist-two pairs $\big((\iota_nd)^kZ,(\iota_nd)^{k'}Z\big)$ and $(A|_V,Z)$ and on a pair of gaugino letters, are the special-edge cycles of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-D1} through the pair, with one special edge and the composite line or $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ on an edge at a letter; with them the condition of Corollary \ref{cor:c10-P7} becomes a computation on these families, carried out in Theorem \ref{thm:c11-families}. The coefficients of the three sector parts of $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ in $\hbar F_1^{(1)}(\Delta)$ are fixed to $-\tfrac13$ by the two-leg arity-two equations along $(\Delta,P_b)$ and $(\Delta,\xi)$ (Theorem \ref{thm:c9-theta2}); the cubic part of \eqref{eq:c9-arity1}, in which the bracket of $I$ with each sector part is nonzero while $\{I,S^{(1)}(\Delta)\}=0$, is then a consistency condition on three legs. The two-body kernels of $Q_1$, $S_1$ and $K_1$ are the defect cycles of Theorem \ref{thm:c11-classes}, with the active letters of Lemma \ref{lem:c10-active1} and the bound of Proposition \ref{prop:c10-bound1}; along $\xi\in\Sigma$ a two-body kernel has Weyl weight $w_{X_a}+w_{X_b}+\tfrac12$, as in the second-order operator on pairs of letters of [BK, (3.7)]. The identity \eqref{eq:c10-brst} with the images of antifield outputs and of antifield letters, on the pairs $(A|_V,\lambda)$, $(\lambda,c)$ with derivatives, $(c,A^*|_V)$ and the targets of $(A|_V,A|_V)$ with three outputs, extends Proposition \ref{prop:c10-checks}, and Proposition \ref{prop:c10-reduction} (ii) for antifield outputs, which couple the leg sets under $Q$, requires the same images. Field-strength families, the general fermion families and the identification of the basis with Beisert's oscillator states beyond two sites complete Corollary \ref{cor:c10-beisert}.
\end{remark}
