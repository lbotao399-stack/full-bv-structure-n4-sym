\begin{remark}[Method of computation]
\label{rem:c9-methodD}
Every number of this chapter along $\Delta$ and $P_b$ is a coefficient of $\varepsilon^0$ of defect cycles, evaluated in two independent ways: exactly, in the simplex form of Lemma \ref{lem:c10-special} and Theorem \ref{thm:c11-oddsimplex} over the region where the Schwinger coordinate of the special edge is the smallest, with the position realised by a momentum injected at the vertex $S^{(1)}(x)$; and in momentum space, with the special edge as the factor $e^{-\varepsilon|p|^2}-1$, Feynman parameters for the edges $P$ and the special Schwinger time integrated last. The cohomology classes of the obstructions of arity one and two are computed on complete bases of the quadratic local functionals with the given quantum numbers, fixed by representation counts, and the equations are solved by exact linear algebra. The second evaluation of Proposition \ref{prop:c9-structure} (iii), Remark \ref{rem:c9-Pi} and Remark \ref{rem:c9-twoeval} evaluates the composite cycles and the graphs with $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ on a leg directly as $\varepsilon\to0$ limits in a Feynman-parameter representation, with $E$ acting on Fourier components as $-(4+(p,\partial_p))$; it agrees with the defect cycles in every sector and placement class by placement class. The colour factors are reduced for a general simple $\mathfrak g$ and checked in the realisations $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{su}(2)$, $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{su}(3)$ and $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{gl}(3)$.
\end{remark}
