\begin{remark}[Two evaluations]
\label{rem:c9-twoeval}
The table of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-D1} is the master formula: the defect cycles of Lemma \ref{lem:c8-transfer}. A second evaluation, through the closed forms of Section 9.2, applies $Q$ to the arity-one part of $O[\infty]$ along $\Delta$ (Lemma \ref{lem:c7-image}); its graphs are the tree of $O$ with $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$, the graphs with $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ on a leg of the one-loop two-point function joined to $O$, the composite triangle, the renormalised composite two-point functional on a leg of $O$, and the trees with the vertices $-I^{CT}_{\mathfrak{psl}(4|4)}[\varepsilon]^{(1)}(\Delta)-\hbar F_1^{(1)}(\Delta)$. With \eqref{eq:c9-triangle} it gives \eqref{eq:c9-D1}. The two evaluations agree in detail on the terms beyond the scaling of Corollary \ref{cor:c9-CS}: in the first, the cycles through $O$ with a special edge give, beyond $4$ times the coefficient of $\log\varepsilon$ in $\partial_\delta I^{CT}[\varepsilon]$, the terms $-\tfrac43O-\tfrac13\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),O\}$, and the tree insertion into $O$ of the composite part $\tfrac13\int(dA|_{W_{\mathbb R}^\perp},dA|_{W_{\mathbb R}^\perp})$ of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-quadratic} (ii) gives $\tfrac83O$; in the second, the composite triangle gives $-2\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),O\}$, and the composite two-point functional on a leg of $O$ gives, by Lemma \ref{lem:c6-bvexact} and Remark \ref{rem:c9-Pi}, $\tfrac83O+\tfrac53\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),O\}-\tfrac43O$. Both sums are $\tfrac43O-\tfrac13\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),O\}$ units, and this agreement determines both coefficients $8$ in the value of the composite triangle.
\end{remark}
