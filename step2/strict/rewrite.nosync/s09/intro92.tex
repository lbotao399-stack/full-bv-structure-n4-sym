In the construction every component along $\Delta$ is the coefficient of $\varepsilon^0$ of the defect cycles of the defect vertex of Lemma \ref{lem:c8-transfer}, with $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ placed on the cycle at the special edge or as a line (Proposition \ref{prop:c9-structure} (ii), Theorem \ref{thm:c11-classes}). The closed forms of this section serve two purposes: they give the line placement, the composite kernel, and they give a second evaluation of the same flow, through $Q$ of the renormalised amplitudes, whose agreement with the defect cycles is a theorem of the construction (Proposition \ref{prop:c9-structure} (iii), Theorem \ref{thm:c10-D1}). Along $\Delta$ the generator $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ is quadratic (Example \ref{ex:c2-dil}), and the regulator moves under it through its two endpoints (Proposition \ref{prop:c7-dil} at $T=\infty$, Proposition \ref{prop:c9-ward} at finite $T$). The graphs with one vertex $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ therefore have closed forms at finite $\varepsilon$ and $T$. In this section and the next two, $v=2E$, so that $v.$ is the operator of Lemma \ref{lem:c7-weights} on all slots, and $P(\varepsilon,T)\star=\int_\varepsilon^TQ^{GF}e^{-tH}dt$ is the operator represented by $P(\varepsilon,T)$ (Definition \ref{def:c8-PT}). For an operator $X$ on $\mathscr E$ the kernel $(X\otimes1)K_0$ represents $X$, so $P(\varepsilon,T)=(P(\varepsilon,T)\star\otimes1)K_0$. The operator $\partial_\phi$ is read as in Definition \ref{def:c3-P}: on the product of a coordinate functional of the first factor and one of the second, in this order, it is the corresponding component of $\phi$. A linear operator $X$ on $\mathscr E$ acts on functionals as the derivation induced by $\ell\mapsto\ell\circ X$ on linear functionals $\ell$ [C07, §6.2], as $Q$ does, and $XF$ denotes this derivation applied to $F$. For a functional $F$ and an even $J$, the linearised flow of $F$ at $J$ is $e^{-W(P(\varepsilon,T),J)/\hbar}e^{\hbar\partial_{P(\varepsilon,T)}}\big(F\,e^{J/\hbar}\big)$; for even $F$ it is $\partial_\delta W(P(\varepsilon,T),J+\delta F)$ with $\delta$ a square-zero parameter of degree $0$, as in \eqref{eq:c7-flowcov}.
