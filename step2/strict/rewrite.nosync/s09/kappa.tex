\begin{theorem}[The multiple of the classical transformation]
\label{thm:c9-kappa}
Let the family of scalar bilinears consist of $\int\omega\,(\iota_{w_1}A,\iota_{w_2}A)$ for all compactly supported four-forms $\omega$ and $w_1,w_2\in W_{\mathbb R}^\perp$. Assume:
\begin{itemize}
\item (i) a one-loop quantisation through arity two along $\Delta$ and $P_b$: \eqref{eq:c8-qme} modulo $\hbar^2$ in its components of arity at most two along these generators, with $\hbar F_1^{(1)}(\Delta)$ of degree at most one in $x$ in the sector $(A|_{W_{\mathbb R}^\perp},A^*|_{W_{\mathbb R}^\perp})$;
\item (ii) local representatives through arity two along $(\Delta,P_b)$ (Definition \ref{def:c8-rep}), with $\psi=0$, on a space of local functionals stable under $\Lambda$ that contains the family.
\end{itemize}
Then $\theta=-\tfrac13$: on the part with two legs $A|_{W_{\mathbb R}^\perp}$ the coefficient of $\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),O\}$ in $D_1(O)$ vanishes. This is the operator-level form of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-theta2} (i): the value computed there from the action-level arity-two equation along $(\Delta,P_b)$ is reproduced by the arity-two component of $L\circ L=0$ on the scalar bilinears, a cross-check of that computation.
\end{theorem}
