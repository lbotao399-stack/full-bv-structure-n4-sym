\begin{remark}[The equations that fix the remaining numbers]
\label{rem:c9-next}
The numbers left free above enter the next equations of the one-loop structure. The coefficients $\beta_k$, $\kappa_k$ of Theorem \ref{thm:c9-F3} enter the part of $s(\hbar F_1)=\hbar\,\mathrm{Ob}$ with four legs and the three- and four-leg parts of the arity-one equations along $\Sigma$. The differences $\theta-\theta_V$ and $\theta_\lambda-\theta_V$ enter the cubic part of \eqref{eq:c9-arity1} through the brackets of the cubic part of $I$ with the sector parts of $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ (Proposition \ref{prop:c11-arity1} (iii)), and the two-leg arity-two equations along $(\Delta,P_b)$ and $(\Delta,\xi)$ fix all three to $-\tfrac13$ (Theorem \ref{thm:c9-theta2}). The uniform coefficients along $P_b$, $\Sigma$, $\Sigma^*$ and $K_b$ are related by the arity-two equations of Theorem \ref{thm:c11-oddar2} and are fixed up to the inner direction generated by $S^{(1)}(\Delta)$ and the outer direction $G_B$; the special conformal coefficients and the four second-order directions of $\hbar F_1^{(1)}(K_b)$ enter the arity-two equations along $(K_b,P_{b'})$ and $(\eta,\eta')$, at a finite endpoint $T$. The trace bilinear has the further outputs $(A|_V,A|_V)$ and $(c,A^*|_V)$, the rotations of $V$ carry the composite kernel $2\big(v.(P(\varepsilon,\infty)\star)^2\otimes1\big)K_0$ with $v$ a rotation and enter $M_1$, and the letters, with $(Q+\{I,-\})O\neq0$, carry composite loops through the vertex $\{I,O\}$; these are given by the same classes of graphs. Chapter 12 states the equations of every arity along every generator together (Lemma \ref{lem:c12-wz}, Theorem \ref{thm:c12-fix}).
\end{remark}
