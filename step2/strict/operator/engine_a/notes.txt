1. Method (route A)
 Master formula (Thm thm:op-master (iii)):  hbar L_1(O)^(1)(x) = sum_{a<b} +- Upsilon_x(X_a,X_b) - {hbar F_1^(1)(x), O}.
 Upsilon_x(X_a,X_b) is computed as the coefficient of eps^0 of the defect cycles of the pair: chains X_a - e_0 - V_1 - ...
 - V_n - e_n - X_b with vertices of I (cubic: one output, quartic: two A outputs), EXACTLY ONE term of S^(1)(x) (two-leg
 block at any gap, or the cubic T5 with one output), EXACTLY ONE special line K_eps - K_0 (numerator -|l+q|^2 Khat,
 Schwinger time in [0, eps]), all other lines P(eps, infinity) -- every placement enumerated (qa_core.structures/configs).
 Gaussian loop integral, F_m, and the exact simplex weights (Thm c11-oddsimplex):
   16 pi^2 [eps^0] = int_{alpha_s smallest} F_0 log(min_{f != s} alpha_f / alpha_s),
   16 pi^2 [eps^-m] = (1/m) int_{alpha_s smallest} [(min_{f != s} alpha_f)^m - alpha_s^m] F_m,
 exact Dirichlet-log integrals (values in Q(i) + Q(i) log(E/(E-1)) per chain).  Letter derivatives: polynomial factors on
 the letter momenta (d_mu -> i p_mu), directions d1 = (1,2,-1,1), d2 = (2,-1,1,3), d3 = (-1,1,2,1): n = 1: d1 on X_a;
 n = 2: d1 on X_a, d2 on X_b; n = 3: d1 d2 on X_a, d3 on X_b.  Data: seeded Gaussian-integer letters of definite
 chirality, integer momenta, recorded per row.
 Generators (fixed generic data, recorded in results.json): P_b, b = (1,-2,1,3); Delta (intrinsic part at x_O = 0:
 dv = 8, transport a = 2z); M_Om, v = Om x, Om = [[0,1,-2,1],[-1,0,1,2],[2,-1,0,-1],[-1,-2,1,0]] (om = Om^T: the
 antisymmetric part of d_m v_n; transport a = Om z); R in so(W_R^perp) (a generic 6x6 antisymmetric integer matrix on
 A|_perp, spin lift on lambda); xi in S_+ (x) W and xibar in S_- (x) W^* (engine lam_+ / lam_- bases, coefficients
 (1,2,-1,1,0,1,-2,1)); eta in S_+ (x) W^* and etabar in S_- (x) W (lams_+ / lams_-, coefficients (2,-1,1,0,1,1,-1,2)),
 intrinsic part s0 + sum_mu (-i d/dr_mu) S^(1)(rho(e_mu) eta); K_b intrinsic part: linear Taylor terms (om^(rho),
 dv^(rho) = -8 b_rho) with -i d/dr_rho, quadratic terms (a^(rho sigma)) with -d^2/dr_rho dr_sigma.
 Sign rule (no free constant): per configuration (-1)^{n(<sigma) + |S^(1)(x)| (n(<s) + [s<sigma])} (-1)^{[sigma = e_n]},
 the intrinsic pair sign (-1)^{|X_a|+|X_b|}, global +1 (qa_core, Def c11-defect (ii) relative to Lemma c10-sign; fixed in
 Part Q by G-reg and the graded-symmetry gate; re-checked here by G-reg, R7, the spectra and GS).
 Positions: z^mu -> -i d/dr_mu with r injected at the S vertex and absorbed by omega (exact as the t^1 coefficient of
 r = t e_mu, cq_gcore), z^mu z^nu -> -d/dr_nu [t^1 coefficient at r = j e_nu] (exact Lagrange interpolation in j,
 2h + 1 points, h = (D + 3)//2).

2. Finding on the finite special vertices (Def def:op-defect (iii), Thm thm:op-master (ii)-(iii))
 On every table row with D >= 2 the eps^0 coefficient of the defect cycles alone satisfies the pair form of Conj
 op-pair (Upsilon^0_Delta = 4 lc + C^0_Delta, Upsilon_P = C_P, Upsilon^0_M = C^0_M, Upsilon_R = 0), while the finite
 special vertex F_x = -sum_m ((-1)^m/m!) ({I, ct1_{x,m}}_{H^m} + {S^(1)(x), ct_m}_{H^m}) is nonzero there.  Closed form
 on a single output Y (weight w_Y, momentum q): {S^(1)(Delta), ct_m}_{H^m} = 2 (w_Y + D) |q|^{2m} ct_m (Euler, ct_m of
 degree D - 2m), {S^(1)(P_b), ct_m}_{H^m} = i (b.q) |q|^{2m} ct_m; e.g. (lam_+, lam_+) -> A_perp along Delta:
 ct_1 = -69 - 115 i, [eps^-1] of the defect cycles = 276 + 460 i = -4 ct_1, eps^0 = 4 lc exactly, F_Delta = 6|q|^2 ct_1.
 Mechanism: the brackets that define F_x are the counterterm partners of the class (Br) graphs whose special edge lies on
 a tree edge ADJACENT to the divergent cycle through the pair (from the action defect vertex (a): {I,I}^sigma, {S(x),I}^sigma);
 (special edge) x (cycle - its counterterm) -> 0, so the bare (Br) graphs have the nonvanishing limit
 sum_m ((-1)^m/m!) |p|^{2m} (pole coefficient) and cancel F_x exactly.  The statement "(Br) tends to 0" of the proof of
 Thm op-master (ii) holds for (Br) together with these partners; F_x must then not be added again in (iii).  This engine
 therefore uses Upsilon_x := [eps^0] D_x and reports F_x separately (rows 'F_x_single_output', 'conj_holds_with_F').
 Consequence for the theory note: Def op-defect (iv), eq c10-finitesp-type finite vertices in eq op-master and the
 "vacuum-pole terms" of G-pole should be revisited; the arity-zero analogue (the last term of eq op-master0) is affected
 in the same way.  Evidence: G-D1pair at D >= 2 (all rows), and the summed identity of Thm op-a on F0 / spectra.
 Pole identity in Ward form (computed, pair by pair): [eps^-m] of the placements other than the line = -4 m ct_m along
 Delta and 0 along P_b, M, R (the regulator variation of Thm op-ward applied to the poles).
