\begin{remark}[Physics notation: transfer endpoint and Wilsonian flow]
$T$ is the endpoint of the transfer of [C07], the Wilsonian scale, of length $T^{1/2}$, at which the effective interaction is taken, and $\varepsilon$ is the ultraviolet proper-time cutoff: the scalar factor of $P(\varepsilon,T)$ is the massless propagator $\frac1{4\pi^2|x-y|^2}$ restricted to the proper times between $\varepsilon$ and $T$, $\frac{e^{-|x-y|^2/4T}-e^{-|x-y|^2/4\varepsilon}}{4\pi^2|x-y|^2}$. $W(P(\varepsilon,T),J)$ is the Wilsonian effective interaction at scale $T$, the connected Feynman graphs with vertices from $J/\hbar$ and internal lines $\hbar P(\varepsilon,T)$, in which the modes of proper time between $\varepsilon$ and $T$ are integrated out. \end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: the equivariant quantum master equation]
$I_{\mathfrak{psl}(4|4)}[T]$ is the Wilsonian effective action at scale $T$ with the constant superconformal ghosts. \eqref{eq:c8-qme} is the regularised Slavnov--Taylor identity: its arity-zero part is that of BRST invariance, its arity-one part along $x$ the Ward identity of the generator $x$, its arity-two part the closure of the algebra. $F_{\mathfrak{psl}(4|4)}$ is a finite symmetry-restoring counterterm in the sense of algebraic renormalisation, and the functions $\varepsilon^{-m}\log^k\varepsilon$ are the power and logarithmic divergences of the proper-time cutoff.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: anomaly and renormalisation ambiguities]
$\mathrm{Ob}$ is the one-loop anomaly of the regularised Slavnov--Taylor and superconformal Ward identities; (i) is its Wess--Zumino consistency condition, (ii) states that it is removed by a finite counterterm exactly when it is $s$-exact, and (iii) identifies the finite renormalisation ambiguities with $H^0$ of $s$ on local cochains, the field redefinitions being trivial. In (iv) the coupling direction is a finite renormalisation of the coupling: through the pairing $(X,Y)=-\tfrac{4\hbar}{g^2}\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ the action $S$ is proportional to $g^{-2}$ at fixed $\mathfrak g$-valued fields, and $\mu S$ is $S$ with $g^{-2}\mapsto\mu g^{-2}$. $G_B$ is the relative normalisation of $Q$ and $\bar Q$ (Theorem \ref{thm:c8-invariants} (ii)).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: the hypercharge]
$B$ is the $\mathfrak u(1)$ outer automorphism of $\mathfrak{psu}(2,2|4)$, the hypercharge $U(1)_Y$: in $\mathfrak{gl}(4|4)$, with real form $\mathfrak u(2,2|4)$, it is the commutator with $\operatorname{diag}(0_4,1_4)$. It vanishes on $P$, $M$, $\bar M$, $\Delta$, $K$, $R$ and counts upper minus lower $SU(4)_R$ indices on the supercharges,
\[
B(Q^i_\alpha)=Q^i_\alpha,\qquad B(\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i})=-\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i},\qquad B(S^\alpha{}_i)=-S^\alpha{}_i,\qquad B(\bar S^{\dot\alpha i})=\bar S^{\dot\alpha i},
\]
so $Q^i_\alpha$ and $\bar S^{\dot\alpha i}$ (in the $\mathbf4$) have charge $+1$ and $\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i}$ and $S^\alpha{}_i$ (in the $\bar{\mathbf4}$) charge $-1$. In components $G_B^{(1)}(x)=S^{(1)}(Bx)$ is $S^{(1)}$ with $\xi_\alpha{}^i$, $\bar\eta^{\dot\alpha i}$ kept and $\bar\xi_{\dot\alpha i}$, $\eta^\alpha{}_i$ reversed in sign.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: the two finite ambiguities]
$e^{tB}$ multiplies $Q^i_\alpha$, $\bar S^{\dot\alpha i}$ by $e^t$ and $\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i}$, $S^\alpha{}_i$ by $e^{-t}$ and fixes the even part, an automorphism of the superconformal algebra; $G_B$ is the corresponding change of the couplings to the constant ghosts, with $G_B^{(0)}=0$, so the action $S$ itself is unchanged. Proposition \ref{prop:c8-B} states that this ambiguity, a relative rescaling of $Q$ and $\bar Q$, is independent of the finite renormalisation of the coupling.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: renormalised operators and their transformations]

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: large-distance behaviour and the endpoint T]
The endpoint $T$ is the Wilsonian scale of the transfer, and the one-loop components are independent of it (Theorem \ref{thm:c8-L} (ii)). The insertions of $S^{(1)}$ along $Q$, $S$, $\Delta$, $P$, $M$, $\bar M$, $R$ converge at large distance, so these components may be computed at $T=\infty$ (Proposition \ref{prop:c8-Tsafe}); along $\Delta$, $P$, $M$, $\bar M$ the insertions on a cycle are composite $A_m$--$c$ lines (end of Section 9.2). The special conformal $K_b$, whose Killing field $x^2b^m-2(b\cdot x)x^m$ grows quadratically, produces a large-distance logarithm, $\int_{|y|^2<T}|y|^{-4}d^4y$ growing as $\pi^2\log T$: the coefficient of $\log T$ of a graph with $S^{(1)}(K_b)$ on its cycle is $\tfrac3{8\pi^2}\hbar$ times the tree obtained by cutting the insertion out of the cycle and putting its two legs on the constant modes $A_m=b_mX_a$ and $c=X^a$, summed over a basis $X_a$ of $\mathfrak g$ and the dual basis $X^a$ for $(-,-)$. $K_1$ is defined with $\varepsilon\to0$ at fixed $T$ (Definition \ref{def:c8-K}), and Theorem \ref{thm:c8-K} states that the local operator $K_1(O)$ is the same at every $T$, while $O[T]$ keeps in its component along $K_b$ the term $\tfrac3{8\pi^2}\hbar\log T$ times this tree of the order-$\hbar^0$ part of $O[\infty]$, generated by the Wilsonian flow and with vanishing image under $Q$ for gauge-invariant $O$; the effective interaction $I_{\mathfrak{psl}(4|4)}[T]$ keeps the same term. In radial quantisation $K_b$ is the conjugate of $P_b$ (Corollary \ref{cor:c8-gram}).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: Euclidean conjugation of spinors]
In Euclidean signature complex conjugation keeps the four-dimensional chirality and exchanges $\mathbf4$ and $\bar{\mathbf4}$: $\overline{\xi_\alpha{}^i}$, placed as $\eta^\alpha{}_i$, lies in $(\mathbf2,\mathbf1,\bar{\mathbf4})$, and $-\overline{\bar\xi_{\dot\alpha i}}$, placed as $\bar\eta^{\dot\alpha i}$, in $(\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\mathbf4)$; upper and lower indices are exchanged as for the unitary groups $SU(2)_L$, $SU(2)_R$, $SU(4)_R$ of the compact real form. In Lorentzian signature, by contrast, $(\psi_\alpha)^\dagger$ is a dotted spinor. In ten-dimensional notation the conjugation maps $\zeta_A\in\mathbf{16}$ to an element of $\mathbf{16}'$, with upper index, compatibly with the gamma matrices: $\overline{\Gamma_M\zeta}=\Gamma_M\overline\zeta$ for the real basis vectors $e_M$.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: radial quantisation]
This is the conjugation of radial quantisation, $P^\dagger=K$, $Q^\dagger=S$, $\bar Q^\dagger=\bar S$, $D^\dagger=D$ in physics notation, here with the signs $P_b^\dagger=-K_{\bar b}$, $(Q^i_\alpha)^\dagger=-S^\alpha{}_i$, $(\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i})^\dagger=\bar S^{\dot\alpha i}$, $\Delta^\dagger=\Delta$, and with the matrix adjoint on $\mathfrak{su}(4)_R$. By the dilatation part $\tfrac12(\xi,\eta)\Delta$ of $[\xi,\rho(x)\eta]$, both $[Q^i_\alpha,(Q^i_\alpha)^\dagger]$ and $[\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i},(\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i})^\dagger]$ (no sum) contain $-\tfrac12\Delta$, the physics dilatation $D=-E$; (iii) states that on a superconformal primary (annihilated by $\Sigma^*$, i.e. by $S$ and $\bar S$) of scaling dimension $d>0$ the norms of its $Q$-descendants are positive for the sign $+$. The real form $\mathfrak{psu}(2,2|4)$ has even part $\mathfrak{su}(2,2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(4)_R$, with $\mathfrak{su}(2,2)\cong\mathfrak{so}(4,2)$ the Lorentzian conformal algebra, and maximal compact even subalgebra $\mathfrak{su}(2)_L\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_R\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(4)_R$, the $\mathfrak u(1)$ generated by $i\Delta$.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: Osterwalder--Schrader reflection]
$\Theta_n$ is the Osterwalder--Schrader reflection in the hyperplane orthogonal to $n$; for $n=e_4$ it is Euclidean time reflection $x^4\mapsto-x^4$, and $\dagger_n$ corresponds to the Hermitian conjugation of the Wick-rotated Lorentzian algebra. On the scalars it uses the reality of the $\mathbf6$, $\overline{A_{ij}}=A^{ij}$, and on the gauginos it exchanges $\lambda$ and $\bar\lambda$. For $n=e_4$, where $\sigma^4=i$,
\[
(Q^i_\alpha)^{\dagger_{e_4}}=-\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i}\big|_{\dot\alpha=\alpha},\qquad (\bar Q_{\dot\alpha i})^{\dagger_{e_4}}=-Q^i_\alpha\big|_{\alpha=\dot\alpha},
\]
the Euclidean counterpart, with the sign $-1$, of the Lorentzian relation $(Q_\alpha)^\dagger=\bar Q_{\dot\alpha}$ between undotted and dotted supercharges. The reflection inner product $\langle a,b\rangle_n$ of Definition \ref{def:c8-inner} is the Osterwalder--Schrader inner product.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: BPZ conjugation]
$C$ is the conformal map from the half space to the unit ball that carries planar (Osterwalder--Schrader) quantisation to radial quantisation, and $\Theta_I$, the inversion $x\mapsto x/x^2$ with the conformal factors, is the BPZ conjugation of radial quantisation on the classical fields. $\Theta_I$ is no symmetry of the regularised theory: the heat kernel, the propagator and the mass counterterm $\tfrac94\varepsilon^{-1}$ units times $\int(A,A)$ change under the inversion. At one loop the radial conjugation is therefore the reflection $\Theta_n$ transported by the one-loop generators $P_n-K_n$ (Definition \ref{def:c8-radial}), and it depends on $P_1$ and $K_1$; $P_1=0$ is a renormalisation condition, set by the finite part $\tfrac{a_P}2\{S^{(1)}(\Delta),-\}$ of the operators when $P_1=a_P\{S^{(1)}(P_b),-\}$ (block at the end of Section 9.5). the anomalous part $K_1(O)=-2(b\cdot x)\kappa O$ of the special conformal transformation being that of $b^m\big(x^2\partial_m-2x_m\,x\cdot\partial-2(w_O+\kappa)x_m\big)$.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: two-point functions]
The two-point function of Definition \ref{def:c8-2pt} is the coefficient of $t_2t_1$ at the zero field, at tree level the free expectation $\langle O_1O_2\rangle$ with $O_1$ on the left, the ordering in which the Ward identity of Theorem \ref{thm:c8-adjoint} holds. (i) is the decoupling of BRST-exact operators, (ii) the existence of the limit at separated points and the convergence at large distance, so that the endpoint is taken at $T=\infty$, and (iii) the Callan--Symanzik equation of the two-point function, with $\ell$ the renormalisation length and the dilatation coefficients of Theorem \ref{thm:c6-dil}. For a single scalar operator with the coefficient $D_1(O)=2\gamma_OO$ ($\gamma_O=\frac{3g^2N}{4\pi^2}$ for the Konishi operator, $0$ for the $\mathbf{20'}$) it reads
\[
\langle O,O\rangle(x,0)=|x|^{-2w}\Big[C+(\text{one-loop finite part})-\gamma_O\,C\log\frac{|x|^2}{\ell^2}\Big],
\]
the one-loop expansion of $C\,(|x|^2/\ell^2)^{-\gamma_O}|x|^{-2w}$, the power law of an operator of dimension $w+\gamma_O$.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Physics notation: BPZ inner product and $Q^\dagger=S$]
The radial inner product is the BPZ inner product of radial quantisation, the two-point function of $b$ at the origin with the inversion conjugate of $a$, i.e. the overlap of the in-state of $b$ with the out-state of $a$. On operators at the origin of one classical Weyl weight $w$ it exists exactly when the one-loop dilatation matrix is self-adjoint for the tree part; its Gram matrix $\mathrm{Gram}_{\rm tree}+\hbar\,\mathrm{Gram}_1$ is the matrix of two-point coefficients of the operator basis, the first operator conjugated, at the separation $\ell$, in a scheme with $P_1=0$, and $1-2a_Pw$ times it in a scheme with $P_1=a_P\{S^{(1)}(P_b),-\}$, $a_P$ real. $Y^{\rm adj}$ is the Hermitian adjoint for the tree metric. Corollary \ref{cor:c8-gram}, on a family of operators of one length (number of letters), is the one-loop form of $S=Q^\dagger$, $\bar S=\bar Q^\dagger$ and $K=P^\dagger$, with the signs of Theorem \ref{thm:c8-dag}: the one-loop special supercharges are the adjoints of the one-loop supercharges for the one-loop-corrected metric, the commutator with $\mathrm{Gram}_{\rm tree}^{-1}\mathrm{Gram}_1$ being the change of the metric. $K=P^\dagger$ holds in the form $K_1=\hbar[\{S^{(1)}(K_{\bar b}),-\},\mathrm{Gram}_{\rm tree}^{-1}\mathrm{Gram}_1]$ in a scheme with $P_1=0$, and with the added term $a_P\{S^{(1)}(K_{\bar b}),-\}$ when $P_1=a_P\{S^{(1)}(P_b),-\}$. The relation for $D_1$ is the self-adjointness of the one-loop dilatation operator, hence the reality of the one-loop anomalous dimensions.
\end{remark}
