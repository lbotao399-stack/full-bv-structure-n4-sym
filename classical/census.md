Conventions:
- $|\operatorname{Aut}|$ counts automorphisms fixing the external legs. Vertex functions are the full multilinear forms (spec §9), summed over all component labels inside each line type.

Vertex labels (spec §8):

- $G^V$, $G^W$: $A\vert_V\,c\,A^*\vert_V$ and $A\vert_{W^\perp}\,c\,A^*\vert_{W^\perp}$ in $([A,c],A^*)$;
- $([c,\lambda],\lambda^*)$ and $\frac12([c,c],c^*)$ never enter a loop, because no component of $P$ ends on $\lambda^*$ or $c^*$.
