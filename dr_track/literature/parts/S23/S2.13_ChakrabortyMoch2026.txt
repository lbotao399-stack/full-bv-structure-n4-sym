[S2.13_ChakrabortyMoch2026] M. Chakraborty, S.-O. Moch -- Dimensional reduction is supersymmetric at three loops (2026)
  arXiv: 2603.02892   Journal: none listed (preprint, PUBDB-2026-00811)
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: The three-loop mismatch between the Yukawa (ffs) and ghost-gluon (ccg) vertex beta functions in N=2 SYM in DR (Avdeev 1982; Velizhanin 2008, Table 1) is not intrinsic to DR: it arises from Levi-Civita terms in flavour traces (eqs. 8-9) and Pauli traces (eq. 11) that survive only when both the spinor and the flavour trace have chiral parts (eq. 13); keeping them (eq. 15) cancels the O(a^4) mismatch. N=1 has no chiral flavour part and N=4 acquires one only with six Z matrices in one trace, i.e. first at five loops (Fig. 3, topological argument). Four-loop N=1 gauge and Yukawa beta functions coincide and four-loop N=4 beta functions vanish (eq. 16). N=2 at four loops deferred (needs a non-trivial gamma5 treatment).
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops (N=1, 2, 4); 4 loops (N=1, N=4); 5-loop statement for N=4 is an argument only / generalised adjoint gauge theory with n_f Weyl fermions, n_s real scalars, flavour matrices Z^I (eq. 3-4): N=1 (0,1), N=2 (2,2), N=4 (6,4)
  Scheme: DR implemented by n_s -> n_s + 2eps (epsilon-scalars as extra adjoint scalars, no separate evanescent couplings), MS-bar, infrared rearrangement, general covariant gauge, FORM/Qgraf/FORCER; Levi-Civita indices kept in integer dimension with an O(eps) remainder ('simplified regularisation', eq. 10), justified because at three loops Levi-Civita terms give only simple poles.
  Symmetry argument: Explicit check of the SUSY relation beta_ffs = beta_ccg (eq. 2).
  Proved: (none)
  Assumed: 
      - The simplified Levi-Civita prescription (eq. 10) is adequate at three loops (argued from simple-pole structure).
      - Five-loop onset for N=4 from diagram topology, not computed.
  Checked partially: 
      - Yukawa vs ghost-gluon beta equality at three loops for N=1, 2, 4 and at four loops for N=1, N=4; gauge independence checked in general covariant gauge.
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. I: history, Table 1 (old results).
      - Sec. II: generalised Lagrangian eq. (3), Clifford-like flavour relation eq. (4), regularisation eq. (10).
      - Sec. III: Pauli traces eq. (11), cancellation of gluon-attached loops eq. (12), chirality condition eq. (13), integrals eq. (14), corrected Levi-Civita contribution eq. (15) (2/3) i [C_A^3 (4-3 zeta3) + d44 (12+72 zeta3)]/eps x 12.
      - eq. (16): four-loop N=1 and N=4 results; Fig. 3: five-loop N=4 topology.
  Red flags: 
      - The title is stronger than the content: only one SUSY relation (gauge vs Yukawa) is checked, not the quartic coupling or the full ST identity.
      - The gamma5/Levi-Civita treatment is ad hoc and explicitly justified only at three loops; the authors state the general gamma5 problem is open and blocks N=2 at four loops.
      - The authors cannot say with certainty what the original calculations did (steps not detailed).
