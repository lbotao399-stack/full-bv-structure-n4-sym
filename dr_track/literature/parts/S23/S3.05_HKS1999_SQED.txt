[S3.05_HKS1999_SQED] W. Hollik, E. Kraus, D. Stoeckinger -- Renormalization and symmetry conditions in supersymmetric QED (1999)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9907393   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C 11 (1999) 365
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: SQED in Wess-Zumino gauge with constant SUSY and translation ghosts (ST identity eq. 42); explicit one-loop renormalization in DREG with a non-anticommuting gamma5 of 't Hooft-Veltman/BMHV type ({gamma^mu, gamma5} = 2 ghat^{mu nu} gamma_nu gamma5, ghat^mu_mu = D-4, ghat set to 0 only in final results; Sec. 4). DREG breaks SUSY at one loop; the required non-invariant (SUSY-restoring) counterterms are: electron-selectron mass counterterm difference delta m_Psi - delta m_phi = (5/3)(alpha/4 pi) m (eqs. 158-159); photino Yukawa vertex delta Z_{phi Psi photino} = -(1/2) delta Z_gamma - (1/6)(alpha/4 pi) (eq. 175), i.e. a -(1/6)(alpha/4 pi) deviation from the symmetric value; non-invariant counterterms in the SUSY-transformation vertices (eqs. 182-185); alternative bookkeeping eq. (186). Photon couplings satisfy their identities at the regularized level (eqs. 168-171). For DRED invariant counterterms suffice at one loop (said to be well known).
  Loop order / theory: 1 loop / N=1 SQED (Wess-Zumino gauge)
  Scheme: DREG with BMHV-type gamma5 (Sec. 4); comparison remark on DRED.
  Symmetry argument: ST identity with constant ghosts gives exact symmetry conditions that fix counterterms even when the regularization breaks SUSY; no anomaly in SQED other than the SUSY extension of the Adler-Bardeen anomaly, so all breakings are scheme breakings.
  Proved: 
      - One-loop: SUSY-restoring counterterms exist and are fixed by the ST identity; explicit values eqs. (158)-(159), (175), (182)-(186).
  Assumed: 
      - Absence of genuine anomalies beyond the Adler-Bardeen one (standard algebraic result, cited).
  Checked partially: 
      - DRED: invariant counterterms suffice at one loop (statement with reference).
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 4 intro: gamma5 prescription.
      - eqs. (140)-(144): photon/photino self energies equal at the regularized level.
      - eqs. (147)-(159): matter self energies, (D-4) pieces, mass difference.
      - eqs. (168)-(175): vertex conditions; photino vertex counterterm.
      - eqs. (182)-(186): SUSY-transformation vertices; bookkeeping.
  Red flags: 
      - The paper attributes the breaking to DREG in general; it does not separate the gamma5 (BMHV) contribution from the D- vs 4-component vector counting.
      - DRED behaviour beyond one loop described as not under control (at that time).
