[S3.08_BIMS2020] H. Belusca-Maito, A. Ilakovac, M. Mador-Bozinovic, D. Stoeckinger -- Dimensional regularization and Breitenlohner-Maison/'t Hooft-Veltman scheme for gamma5 applied to chiral YM theories: full one-loop counterterm and RGE structure (2020)
  arXiv: 2004.14398   Journal: JHEP 08 (2020) 024
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Full one-loop renormalization in BMHV of a general massless chiral YM theory (simple group, right-handed fermions in rep R, real scalars in rep S, Yukawa and quartic couplings): singular counterterms split into invariant (eqs. 5.34-5.41, standard Z factors) and purely evanescent non-invariant parts (eq. 5.42); one-loop ST breaking computed via Deltahat insertions (eq. 6.5): divergent part (eqs. 6.10-6.11) cancelled exactly by b_d of the singular counterterms (eq. 6.8); finite part via Bonneau identities (eqs. 6.14-6.25), including epsilon-tensor anomalous terms (eqs. 6.18b, 6.21); finite symmetry-restoring counterterms determined in Sec. 6 (not unique); the BMHV-specific counterterms do not change the one-loop RGE.
  Loop order / theory: 1 loop / general chiral Yang-Mills with right-handed fermions and scalars
  Scheme: BMHV DREG; fermion kinetic term fully d-dimensional with sterile left-handed partner (eq. 3.26); gauge vertex psibar_R gammabar^mu psi_R (eq. 3.25, most symmetric of three options eq. 3.24); evanescent kinetic piece i psibar dslashhat psi (eq. 3.29); MS.
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP: S_d(S_0) = Deltahat = int g T^a c^a psibar (dslashhat-left P_R + dslashhat-right P_L) psi (eqs. 3.39-3.40), linear in the ghost and evanescent; b_d not nilpotent while the 4-dim b is; restoration order by order (ST identity in the 4-dim limit).
  Proved: 
      - Complete one-loop counterterm structure and restorability (anomaly-free case) for the general model.
  Assumed: 
      - All-order consistency of BMHV from S3.02; existence of restoring counterterms beyond one loop from algebraic renormalization.
  Checked partially: 
      - Comparison with S3.03: scalar-free counterterm agrees with its eq. (37); discrepancy in two ghost-type group-factor terms vs its eq. (54) (footnote 17); footnote 11 contradicts a b_d-invariance claim of S3.03.
  Evidence:

      - Sec. 6: eqs. (6.5)-(6.25).
