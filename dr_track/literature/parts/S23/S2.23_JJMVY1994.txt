[S2.23_JJMVY1994] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, S.P. Martin, M.T. Vaughn, Y. Yamada -- Decoupling of the epsilon-scalar mass in softly broken supersymmetry (1994)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9407291   Journal: Phys. Rev. D 50 (1994) R5481-R5483
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: In DRED for softly broken SUSY an unphysical epsilon-scalar mass is required for renormalization and enters the two-loop scalar-mass beta function; a redefinition of scalar masses (DRED' scheme) removes this dependence; earlier DR-bar one-loop calculations that ignored m~^2 effectively used DRED'.
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops (soft-mass beta functions) / softly broken N=1 SUSY (MSSM-type)
  Scheme: DRED -> DRED' (mass redefinition).
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY-preservation argument; scheme redefinition to decouple an evanescent mass.
  Proved: 
      - Decoupling of m~^2 from beta functions by redefinition (at the order treated).
