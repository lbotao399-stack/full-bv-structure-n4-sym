[S3.07_FHRS2003] I. Fischer, W. Hollik, M. Roth, D. Stoeckinger -- Restoration of supersymmetric Slavnov-Taylor and Ward identities in presence of soft and spontaneous symmetry breaking (2003)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0310191   Journal: Phys. Rev. D 69 (2004) 015004
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Abelian MSSM-like model (SQED plus a neutral chiral multiplet with a VEV, R-symmetry, soft breaking via spurion) in Wess-Zumino gauge: one-loop explicit results in DRED and DREG with anticommuting gamma5. Electron mass Ward identity (eq. 4.2) holds in both; the electron-selectron ST identity (eq. 4.4) holds in DRED and is broken in DREG (eq. 4.12), with extra soft-breaking-induced terms; restoring counterterms eqs. (4.27)-(4.44). States that in the HVBM (BMHV) scheme R-invariance is broken at the regularized level (Sec. 3.1, item 4), while naive DREG keeps it.
  Loop order / theory: 1 loop / abelian MSSM-like model with soft and spontaneous breaking
  Scheme: DRED and DREG, both with anticommuting gamma5.
  Symmetry argument: ST identity with constant ghosts; soft breaking through spurion external field.
  Proved: 
      - One-loop restoring counterterms in DREG; DRED needs none for the identities considered.
  Assumed: 
      - DRED and anticommuting-gamma5 DREG treated as usable because their differences from a consistent scheme are local terms.
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 3.1 item 4 (HVBM breaks R-invariance).
      - Sec. 4: eqs. (4.2), (4.4), (4.12)-(4.14), (4.27)-(4.44).
  Red flags: 
      - No explicit BMHV computation; the R-symmetry remark shows a structural obstacle to BMHV in SUSY theories: the chiral R-symmetry (and the SUSY transformations, which mix chiralities) become spuriously broken.
