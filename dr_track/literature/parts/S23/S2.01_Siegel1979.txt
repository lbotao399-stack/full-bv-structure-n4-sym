[S2.01_Siegel1979] W. Siegel -- Supersymmetric dimensional regularization via dimensional reduction (1979)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Phys. Lett. B 84 (1979) 193-196
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Proposes regularization by dimensional reduction (DRED): loop momenta and integrals are continued to d<4, while the action is the one obtained by dimensionally reducing the 4-dimensional theory, so all field multiplets keep their 4-dimensional index structure and boson/fermion counting. Claims that gauge invariance, unitarity and global supersymmetry are preserved; sketches application to supergravity.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (claimed, no proof) / globally supersymmetric gauge theories; supergravity (sketched)
  Scheme: DRED: d-dimensional momenta, 4-dimensional field components and Dirac/sigma algebra; in superspace the D-algebra is done in 4 dimensions and the remaining scalar momentum integrals in d dimensions.
  Symmetry argument: Heuristic: dimensional reduction of a supersymmetric theory keeps the supermultiplet structure (equal numbers of bosonic and fermionic components), hence SUSY is expected to survive regularization.
  Proved: (none)
  Assumed: 
      - that 4-dimensional field/spinor algebra can be combined consistently with d-dimensional momenta (shown false in general by the same author, S2.02)
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence: 
      - INSPIRE abstract (record of PLB 84 (1979) 193): method preserves gauge invariance, unitarity and global SUSY; action obtained by dimensional reduction; supergravity application explored.
  Red flags: 
      - No proof of SUSY preservation; the author himself showed the scheme inconsistent one year later (S2.02).
      - Implicit use of 4-dimensional identities (Fierz, epsilon tensors) together with d<4 momenta is the origin of the later inconsistency.
