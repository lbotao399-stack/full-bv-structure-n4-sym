[S2.14_BDDW2002] Z. Bern, A. De Freitas, L.J. Dixon, H.L. Wong -- Supersymmetric regularization, two-loop QCD amplitudes and coupling shifts (2002)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0202271   Journal: Phys. Rev. D 66 (2002) 085002
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: In the four-dimensional helicity (FDH) scheme the SUSY Ward identities for two-loop gg -> gg helicity amplitudes (built from N=1 and N=2 supersymmetric decompositions) are satisfied, the N=1 ones as eps -> 0 and the N=2 one to all orders in eps; in HV the N=1 identities with gluon loops fail even at O(1/eps). The two-loop coupling shift of FDH relative to MS-bar equals that of DR-bar (eq. 8.12); from it a three-loop DR-bar/FDH beta coefficient is derived (eqs. 8.20-8.21).
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops (amplitudes); 3 loops (beta function by conversion) / QCD and SUSY decompositions (N=1 SYM-like, N=2) of gg -> gg
  Scheme: FDH: observed states 4-dimensional, internal states D_s-dimensional with D_s -> 4 (family D_s = 4 - 2 eps delta_R, delta_R=0 FDH, 1 HV); unrenormalized amplitudes; epsilon-scalar coupling identified with the gauge coupling (only a gauge coupling present).
  Symmetry argument: Explicit verification of SUSY Ward identities for helicity amplitudes (eqs. 2.3-2.4, 5.27, 7.1-7.4).
  Proved: (none)
  Assumed: 
      - Identification of the epsilon-scalar (evanescent) coupling with g when computing the three-loop beta (Sec. 8.2).
  Checked partially: 
      - Two-loop N=1 SUSY Ward identities for gg->gg in FDH as eps->0 (eqs. 7.7-7.17); N=2 identity to all orders in eps (eqs. 7.18-7.23).
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 3.1-3.2: scheme definitions.
      - Sec. 7.1: N=1 identities; combinations R^P, R^NP non-zero at O(eps); HV fails.
      - Sec. 7.2: N=2 identity.
      - Sec. 8.1, eqs. (8.11)-(8.12): coupling shift; Sec. 8.2, eqs. (8.18)-(8.21): three-loop beta.
      - Sec. 9: open whether FDH preserves SUSY beyond two loops.
  Red flags: 
      - O(eps) violations of the N=1 identities may feed into three-loop unitarity constructions (Sec. 7.1, flagged by the authors).
      - The three-loop DR-bar beta of eqs. (8.20)-(8.21) was later found incorrect in its fermionic colour structures because alpha_e was identified with alpha_s (S2.11).
      - FDH as defined here (evanescent couplings identified, no separate renormalization) was later shown non-unitary beyond one loop (S2.15, S2.16).
      - No all-order statement; only gg->gg checked; no external fermions.
