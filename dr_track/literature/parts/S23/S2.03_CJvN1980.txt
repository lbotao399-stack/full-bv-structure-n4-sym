[S2.03_CJvN1980] D.M. Capper, D.R.T. Jones, P. van Nieuwenhuizen -- Regularization by dimensional reduction of supersymmetric and nonsupersymmetric gauge theories (1980)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B 167 (1980) 479-499
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Component-field formulation of DRED for SUSY and non-SUSY gauge theories (4-dimensional vector field split into a d-dimensional gauge field plus 4-d epsilon-scalars). Explicit one- and two-loop calculations: for non-SUSY theories DRED is a workable alternative to DREG; for SUSY theories the Slavnov-Taylor identities of SUSY and of gauge invariance are preserved.
  Loop order / theory: 1 and 2 loops (explicit) / SUSY and non-SUSY gauge theories
  Scheme: Component DRED, MS-type subtraction; epsilon-scalars as the 4-d components of the gauge field.
  Symmetry argument: Explicit diagrammatic verification of selected Slavnov-Taylor / Ward identities.
