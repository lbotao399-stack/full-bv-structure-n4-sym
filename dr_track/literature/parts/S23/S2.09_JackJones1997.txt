[S2.09_JackJones1997] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones -- Regularisation of supersymmetric theories (1997)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9707278   Journal: contribution to 'Perspectives on Supersymmetry' (ed. G.L. Kane, World Scientific); no journal ref on arXiv
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Review. DRED is the regulator of choice for SUSY theories; the Siegel-type ambiguities (epsilon tensors, gamma5) are regarded as technical and avoidable for physical results; the one-loop SUSY Ward identity holds in DRED; the Avdeev-Chochia-Vladimirov ambiguity in the propagator Ward identity of pure SYM needs at least four loops; superfield methods do not escape it; DRED beta functions deviate from the NSVZ form from three loops but are related to it by a coupling redefinition; in softly broken SUSY the epsilon-scalar mass enters two-loop scalar-mass beta functions and is removed by the DRED' redefinition; the van Damme-'t Hooft unitarity problem is resolved by separate treatment of epsilon-scalar couplings.
  Loop order / theory: review: 1-loop Ward identity; >= 4-loop onset of the ACV ambiguity; 3-loop DRED/NSVZ deviation; 4-loop DRED beta up to one unknown parameter (status 1997) / N=1 SYM and general N=1 gauge theories; N=2, N=4; softly broken SUSY
  Scheme: DRED (component and superfield), DRED' for soft breaking, comparison with DREG.
  Symmetry argument: (i) Insertion of the Fierz-vanishing variation delta L_S and gamma-trace counting (ACV); (ii) NSVZ vs DRED related by coupling redefinitions; (iii) N=2 one-loop finiteness used to fix the three-loop DRED gauge beta function.
