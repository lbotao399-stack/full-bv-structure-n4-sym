[S2.11_HKMS2006] R. Harlander, P. Kant, L. Mihaila, M. Steinhauser -- Dimensional reduction applied to QCD at three loops (2006)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0607240   Journal: JHEP 0609 (2006) 053
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Three-loop DR-bar beta function and three-loop quark-mass anomalous dimension of QCD in DRED with epsilon-scalars, keeping the evanescent Yukawa coupling alpha_e (q qbar eps vertex) and quartic couplings eta_r separate. In the SUSY limit the three-loop gauge beta function of N=1 SYM is reproduced (eq. 15) in agreement with Avdeev-Tarasov (PLB 112 (1982) 356). Identifying alpha_e with alpha_s in intermediate steps gives a finite, gauge-independent error in beta and non-local terms in Z_m with non-finite gamma_m at three loops; the three-loop DR-bar beta of BDDW (S2.14) is stated to be incorrect for the C_F^2 T n_f, C_A C_F T n_f and C_F T^2 n_f^2 terms for this reason.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops / QCD (SU(N_c)); eta_r terms only for N_c=3; SUSY Yang-Mills limit
  Scheme: DRED: D=4-2eps momentum integrals, 4-component gauge field split into D-dim gluon + epsilon-scalars with own couplings g_e, lambda_r; Z_s from ghost-gluon or quark-gluon vertex (eq. 6), Z_e from q qbar eps vertex (eq. 7); DR-bar; gamma algebra done in 4-2eps, no detailed gamma5 prescription (none needed).
  Symmetry argument: SUSY-limit check: with C_A=C_F=2T, n_f=1 (adjoint Majorana), alpha_s=alpha_e=eta_1, eta_2=eta_3=0 the gauge beta must be the N=1 SYM one; Sec. 2 states beta_s = beta_e in a SUSY theory.
  Proved: (none)
  Assumed: 
      - beta_s = beta_e in the SUSY limit is used as SUSY input (Sec. 2); its separate three-loop verification not seen in the text.
  Checked partially: 
      - Three-loop N=1 SYM gauge beta in SUSY limit: beta_20=(3/4)C_A, beta_30=(3/8)C_A^2, beta_40=(21/64)C_A^3 (eq. 15).
      - Z_s from two vertices agree; indirect derivation from MS-bar + scheme relation agrees (eqs. 16-18); gamma_m gauge independent (eqs. 21-22).
  Evidence:
