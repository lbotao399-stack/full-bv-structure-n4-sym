Stoeckinger -- Two-loop application of the Breitenlohner-Maison/'t Hooft-Veltman scheme with non-anticommuting gamma5: full renormalization and symmetry-restoring counterterms in an abelian chiral gauge theory (2021)
  arXiv: 2109.11042   Journal: JHEP 11 (2021) 159
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Complete two-loop renormalization of right-handed chiral QED (N_f flavours, hypercharges Y_R, Tr(Y_R^3) = 0, no scalars) in BMHV. One-loop finite restoring counterterm (eq. 6.5): (hbar/16 pi^2) int [ -(e^2 Tr(Y_R^2)/6) Abar.dbar^2 Abar + (e^4 Tr(Y_R^4)/12)(Abar^2)^2 + ((5+xi)/6) e^2 sum_j (Y_R^j)^2 i psibar_j gammabar^mu dbar_mu P_R psi_j ]. Two-loop singular counterterms contain a non-invariant non-evanescent fermion term (eq. 7.4 last line). Two-loop finite restoring counterterm (eq. 8.11): (hbar/16 pi^2)^2 int e^4 [ Tr(Y_R^4)(11/48) Abar dbar^2 Abar + (3 e^2 Tr(Y_R^6)/8)(Abar^2)^2 - sum_j (Y_R^j)^2 ((127/36)(Y_R^j)^2 - (1/27) Tr(Y_R^2)) psibar_j i dslashbar P_R psi_j ]; same three structures as at one loop. UV divergences cannot all be removed by field/parameter renormalization (divergent evanescent counterterms needed).
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops / right-handed chiral QED
  Scheme: BMHV DREG, sterile left-handed partner, purely right-handed 4-dim gauge coupling, evanescent kinetic term S_0,evan = int i psibar dslashhat psi (eq. 3.12b); MS; Feynman gauge in parts (Sec. 7).
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP (eq. 4.7): breakings = insertions of Deltahat and Delta_ct; finite restoring counterterms fixed by cancelling the finite 4-dim part of the breaking (eqs. 4.9, 6.3, 8.2-8.3).
  Proved:
