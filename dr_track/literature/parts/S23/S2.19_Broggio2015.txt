[S2.19_Broggio2015] A. Broggio, Ch. Gnendiger, A. Signer, D. Stoeckinger, A. Visconti -- SCET approach to regularization-scheme dependence of QCD amplitudes (2015)
  arXiv: 1506.05301   Journal: JHEP 1601 (2016) 078
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: FDH and DRED give consistent massless-QCD amplitudes through NNLO provided renormalization (evanescent couplings) is done correctly; scheme dependence is tied to UV and IR singularities; scheme-dependent anomalous dimensions extracted via SCET jet/soft functions give transition rules CDR/HV/FDH/DRED to NNLO; hard, soft and jet functions shown scheme independent.

  Scheme: CDR, HV, FDH, DRED with evanescent couplings kept separate.
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY argument.
  Proved:
