[S3.13_KuehlerStoeckinger2025] P. Kuehler, D. Stoeckinger -- Two-loop renormalization of a chiral SU(2) gauge theory in dimensional regularization with non-anticommuting gamma5 (2025)
  arXiv: 2504.06080   Journal: none listed (preprint v1, April 2025)
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: First complete two-loop renormalization of a non-abelian gauge theory in BMHV: SU(2) with N_f right-handed fermions in a reducible representation (anomaly-free since d^{abc} = 0), no scalars. Two-loop divergent counterterms by sector (eqs. 4.1-4.14); finite two-loop restoring counterterm action eq. (4.22) = sum of eqs. (4.15)-(4.21): gauge bilinear Gbar box Gbar, fermion bilinear psibar dslashbar P_R psi, source terms Rbar c T P_R psi and psibar c T P_L c R, cubic and quartic gauge terms, and one novel term (4.20) involving the external source zeta (zeta c c) not present at one loop; finite counterterms purely non-evanescent.
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops / chiral SU(2) gauge theory, right-handed fermions
  Scheme: BMHV DREG, option 2b of S3.12 (sterile partners, no evanescent gauge couplings); general xi gauge fixing; tadpole expansion with auxiliary mass.
  Symmetry argument: QAP (eqs. 3.4-3.8); ST identity in the 4-dim limit (eqs. 3.1-3.3); b4-exact breakings removable; non-exact cohomology classes (anomalies) assumed absent (Sec. 3.1).
  Proved: 
      - Two-loop finiteness and ST restoration for the model.
  Assumed: 
      - Absence of non-trivial cohomology classes (standard gauge-anomaly assumption).
  Checked partially: 
      - UV poles checked in all sectors; finite parts checked only for selected Green functions; two-loop gauge-parameter independence not established.
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 2: eqs. (2.2)-(2.16).
      - Sec. 3: eqs. (3.1)-(3.22).
      - Sec. 4.1: eqs. (4.1)-(4.14); Sec. 4.2: eqs. (4.15)-(4.22) with coefficients, e.g. quartic g^6 terms with sum C2^2 S2 etc.
  Red flags: 
      - Simpler FeynArts implementations of the breaking vertex gave wrong relative signs (Sec. 3.3).
