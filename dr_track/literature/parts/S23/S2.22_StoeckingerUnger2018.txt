[S2.22_StoeckingerUnger2018] D. Stoeckinger, J. Unger -- Three-loop MSSM Higgs-boson mass predictions and regularization by dimensional reduction (2018)
  arXiv: 1804.05619   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B 935 (2018) 1 (volume/page not verified)
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: DRED preserves the SUSY needed for the three-loop MSSM Higgs-mass prediction at O(alpha_t,b alpha_s^2, alpha_t,b^2 alpha_s, alpha_t,b^3) in the gaugeless limit: the ST identity linking the quartic Higgs coupling to the electroweak gauge couplings (eqs. 37-38) holds, so the non-SUSY three-loop quartic counterterm vanishes (eq. 41). Two-loop STIs (eqs. 14-36, Table 2) fix the needed two-loop subrenormalization (Higgs/Higgsino-quark/squark Yukawa, squark-Higgs quartics, gluon/epsilon-scalar-Higgs quartics); eq. (35)-(36) give Z_{AhatAhat H2H2} = 0 and Z_{AtildeAtilde H2H2} = 0, the latter (epsilon-scalar) not implied by gauge invariance.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops (restricted orders) / MSSM, gaugeless limit
  Scheme: DRED (Q4S, anticommuting gamma5), epsilon-scalars renormalized independently; three-loop STI at zero external momenta.
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP in DRED: breaking = insertion Delta = S(Gamma_cl+ct), non-zero only through Fierz violation; at three loops Delta^{<=2L} (eq. 40) split into a symmetric-renormalization part and a possible non-SUSY two-loop piece (dropped: needs >= 3 coloured fields); breaking diagrams (Figs. 3-5, classes A-C, Table 3) all satisfy the gamma-count bound of S2.10, so they vanish.
  Proved: 
      - The specific three-loop STI (eqs. 37-38) holds in DRED at the stated orders, gaugeless limit, zero external momenta.
  Assumed: 
      - QAP in DRED.
      - Non-SUSY two-loop counterterms to y-gluino-squark-quark and four-squark vertices (Fig. 2) argued irrelevant at this order, not shown to vanish.
  Checked partially: 
      - Down-type (H1, d_R) cases said to follow in the same way, details not given.
  Evidence: 
      - eqs. (14)-(36), Table 2: two-loop STIs.
      - eqs. (37)-(41), Figs. 3-5, Table 3: three-loop analysis.
  Red flags: 
      - Restricted: gaugeless limit, specific orders, zero momenta; soft-breaking and EWSB contributions neglected in the two-loop part.
      - Quartic interactions with four coloured fields covered by no analysis cited (open).
      - Epsilon-scalar-Higgs quartic Z_{AtildeAtilde H2H2} = 0 is a SUSY requirement that DREG would violate.
