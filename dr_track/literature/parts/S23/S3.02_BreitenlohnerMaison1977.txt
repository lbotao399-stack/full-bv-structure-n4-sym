[S3.02_BreitenlohnerMaison1977] P. Breitenlohner, D. Maison -- Dimensional renormalization and the action principle; Dimensionally renormalized Green's functions for theories with massless particles I, II (1977)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Commun. Math. Phys. 52 (1977) 11-38; 39; 55
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Define dimensional renormalization such that the renormalized (quantum) action principle holds, giving a minimal additive scheme; derive Ward-Takahashi identities and Callan-Symanzik equations from it; prove existence of dimensionally renormalized Green functions to all orders for massless theories without super-renormalizable couplings (and, part II, with interactions of infrared degree >= 4), with the action principle holding as in the massive case if dimensional regularization respects the symmetry that protects masslessness. Together with S3.01 this fixes the consistent (BMHV) treatment of gamma5 in d dimensions.
  Loop order / theory: all orders / general renormalizable QFT (massive and massless)
  Scheme: DREG with minimal subtraction; BMHV gamma5 (gamma5 = 4-dimensional object; {gamma^mu, gamma5} = 2 gammahat^mu gamma5).
  Symmetry argument: Regularized quantum action principle: the variation of the regularized generating functional under a field transformation equals the insertion of the variation of the regularized action, a local (possibly evanescent) operator; Ward identities follow when the variation vanishes.
  Proved: 
      - All-order existence and the action principle in dimensional renormalization (abstract-level statements).
