[S2.07_vanDammeHooft1985] R. van Damme, G. 't Hooft -- Breakdown of unitarity in the dimensional reduction scheme (1985)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Phys. Lett. B 150 (1985) 133-138
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Beta functions in different schemes should be related by finite coupling redefinitions; in non-SUSY gauge theories DRED fails this test; the culprit is the counterterm structure needed for overlapping divergences, which violates unitarity. SUSY theories (N=2, N=4 gauge theories, any SUSY theory without vector field) are not affected because the epsilon-scalar renormalizes like the scalar, fermion and vector fields.
  Loop order / theory: multi-loop (overlapping divergences, i.e. from two loops) / non-SUSY gauge theories; SUSY theories as stated exceptions
  Scheme: DRED in which the counterterms of the 4-dimensional couplings are reused for the epsilon-scalar couplings (called 'System 4' in S2.08).
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY argument; consistency of scheme change by coupling redefinition (unitarity).
  Proved: 
      - With epsilon-scalar couplings identified with gauge couplings in non-SUSY theories, DRED is not related to DREG by a coupling redefinition.
