[S2.16_Kilgore2012] W.B. Kilgore -- The four dimensional helicity scheme beyond one loop (2012)
  arXiv: 1205.4015   Journal: Phys. Rev. D 86 (2012) 014019
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Explains the failure of FDH found in S2.15: FDH sets evanescent couplings equal to the gauge coupling without a renormalization mechanism for them, so the error is O(eps) at NLO, O(eps^0) at NNLO and singular from N3LO. Defines FDH as DR with two extra conditions (4-dimensional external states, alpha_e and eta_1 identified with alpha_s at the end) and gives a procedure (eqs. 10-18) to convert bare FDH amplitudes into properly renormalized HV amplitudes using DR-hat counterterms and SCET-like jet/soft functions.
  Loop order / theory: general n-loop conversion; explicit at 1 loop (eq. 20) / QCD (non-supersymmetric)
  Scheme: DR with D-dimensional gauge field plus evanescent field (couplings g_e, eta_1..3) renormalized separately; FDH obtained by identification at the end.
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY argument; notes that in SUSY theories SUSY supplies the missing Ward identity so evanescent components may be treated as gauge bosons (Sec. II.2, IV.5).
  Proved: 
      - Renormalizable definition of FDH as a variant of DR; conversion formula eq. (18).
