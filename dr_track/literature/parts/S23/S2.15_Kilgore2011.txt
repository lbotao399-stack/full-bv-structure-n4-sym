[S2.15_Kilgore2011] W.B. Kilgore -- Regularization schemes and higher order corrections (2011)
  arXiv: 1102.5353   Journal: Phys. Rev. D 83 (2011) 114005
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Tests CDR, DRED and FDH on the massless non-singlet QCD corrections to the hadronic width of a vector boson and to R(e+e- -> hadrons) via the vacuum polarization: CDR and DRED (with independent, minimally subtracted evanescent couplings) agree through NNLO and in the N_f^2 N3LO terms; FDH (evanescent gluons identified with gluons, alpha_e = alpha_s) gives finite but wrong NNLO coefficients and singular N3LO results, hence is not a consistent (unitary) scheme in that form.
  Loop order / theory: 3-loop vacuum polarization (NNLO); 4-loop N_f^2 terms (N3LO) / QCD (non-supersymmetric)
  Scheme: CDR; DRED with separate evanescent couplings; FDH with alpha_e = alpha_s.
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY test; consistency of renormalization (finiteness/unitarity).
  Proved: 
      - FDH with identified evanescent couplings fails at NNLO for this observable (explicit).
  Assumed: (none)
  Checked partially: 
      - DRED = CDR through NNLO and N_f^2 at N3LO.
  Evidence: 
      - eqs. (47)-(48) vs (16), (19), (33), (36): FDH coefficients C_F^2: -15/32 vs -3/32; C_F C_A: 133/32 - (11/4) zeta3 vs 123/32 - (11/4) zeta3; C_F N_f: -5/8 + zeta3/2 vs -11/16 + zeta3/2.
  Red flags: 
      - Directly contradicts the use of FDH (as then defined) beyond one loop; does not test SUSY theories, where the evanescent couplings are protected.
