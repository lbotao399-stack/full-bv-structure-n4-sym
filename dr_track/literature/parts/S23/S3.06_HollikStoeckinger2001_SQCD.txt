[S3.06_HollikStoeckinger2001_SQCD] W. Hollik, D. Stoeckinger -- Regularization and supersymmetry-restoring counterterms in supersymmetric QCD (2001)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0103009   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C 20 (2001) 105
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: One-loop SUSY-restoring counterterms in SQCD: compares DREG with anticommuting gamma5 (theta_DReg = 1) and DRED (theta_DReg = 0). DREG needs finite non-symmetric counterterms: in the gluon/gluino SUSY transformations -(alpha_s C(A)/4 pi)(1/3) theta_DReg (eq. 28), quark/squark SUSY transformations -(alpha_s C(F)/4 pi)(1/2) theta_DReg (eq. 35), gluino-quark-squark vertex (alpha_s/4 pi)(2/3 C(A) - 1/2 C(F)) theta_DReg (eqs. 42-43); gauge interactions need none (eq. 46); gluon-gluino-gluino none (eq. 52). DRED needs no non-symmetric counterterms at one loop.
  Loop order / theory: 1 loop / SQCD (Wess-Zumino gauge, soft breaking via auxiliary multiplet)
  Scheme: DREG with anticommuting gamma5 (not BMHV); DRED.
  Symmetry argument: ST identity with constant SUSY/translation ghosts; uniqueness of counterterms requires the SUSY-algebra identities, not only Ward identities (Sec. 3.2).
  Proved: 
      - One-loop SUSY-restoring counterterms in DREG (values above); none needed in DRED for the considered trilinear interactions and SUSY-transformation operators.
  Assumed: 
      - Both DREG with anticommuting gamma5 and DRED are inconsistent beyond one loop but differ from a consistent scheme by local counterterms (App. C, footnote 9).
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 3.2-3.6, eqs. (25), (28), (35), (41)-(43), (46), (49), (52); App. C.
  Red flags: 
      - Uses anticommuting gamma5 in DREG, so it is not a BMHV analysis; R-invariance is preserved by the regularization (Sec. 3.4), which BMHV would break (see S3.07).
      - Squark quartic couplings not discussed.
