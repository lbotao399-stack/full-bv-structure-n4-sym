[S3.14_vMSW2025] A. von Manteuffel, D. Stoeckinger, M. Weisswange -- Four-loop renormalisation of chiral gauge theories with non-anticommuting gamma5 in the BMHV scheme (2025)
  arXiv: 2506.12253   Journal: JHEP 08 (2025) 088 (from search result; not verified on the arXiv page)
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Complete four-loop renormalization of the abelian right-handed chiral gauge theory (Tr(Y_R^3) = 0) in BMHV, the highest-order BMHV application to date. Singular counterterms: BRST-invariant part (eq. 4.14) and BRST-breaking part (eq. 4.16) with no new field monomials beyond three loops, only higher poles up to 1/eps^4. Finite restoring counterterms (eq. 4.31): only the three structures gauge bilinear, quartic gauge, fermion bilinear (choice not unique, eq. 4.32). SM fermion content mapped with numerical coefficients.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops / abelian chiral gauge theory (SM hypercharge sector as special case)
  Scheme: BMHV DREG; traces factorized into D-dim and 4-dim gamma5 parts (eqs. 3.8-3.12, App. A), cyclicity kept; tadpole decomposition with auxiliary mass; no mu-terms; Feynman gauge; QGRAF/FORM, IBP (FIRE, Reduze2, Kira, Finred).
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP with insertions; validated by four-loop vector-like QED and reproduction of three-loop chiral results.
  Proved: 
      - Complete four-loop counterterm action of the model (locality and Delta-insertion vs 1PI agreement checks).
