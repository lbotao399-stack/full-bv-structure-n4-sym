[S3.12_EKSW2024] P.L. Ebert, P. Kuehler, D. Stoeckinger, M. Weisswange -- Shedding light on evanescent shadows -- exploration of non-anticommuting gamma5 in dimensional regularisation (2024)

  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Systematic study of the D-dimensional continuations in BMHV for a general abelian chiral gauge theory with scalars (specializable to the abelian sector of the SM): fermion options 1, 2a, 2b (single Dirac spinor vs sterile partners; Sec. 2.1, eqs. 2.3-2.18), evanescent gauge interactions (eqs. 2.19-2.21) and new evanescent hypercharges Y_LR = Y_RL^dagger; the tree-level breaking splits into Deltahat_1 (evanescent kinetic terms) and a new Deltahat_2 (evanescent gauge couplings) (eq. 2.39). There is no unique way to implement BMHV; each consistent option gives a correct renormalized theory with different intermediate results and different restoring counterterms; full one-loop UV and finite restoring counterterms are given and a convenient formulation is identified.
