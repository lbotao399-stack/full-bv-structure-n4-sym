[S2.10_Stoeckinger2005] D. Stoeckinger -- Regularization by dimensional reduction: consistency, quantum action principle, and supersymmetry (2005)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0503129   Journal: JHEP 0503 (2005) 076
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Gives a mathematically consistent formulation of DRED: a quasi-4-dimensional space Q4S = QdS + QepsS (formal dimensions 4, d, eps=4-d; all infinite-dimensional), metric g = ghat + gtilde with only the contraction rules (5a)-(5d); no index counting, no Fierz identities; this avoids Siegel's contradiction. Argues that the quantum action principle (QAP) holds in this DRED as in DREG. Derives the general breaking of the Slavnov-Taylor identity (with constant SUSY and translation ghosts) as an insertion of S(Gamma_cl), eq. (34), whose non-zero pieces (35a)-(35h) are Fierz-vanishing terms (gauge, matter, potential and Yukawa sectors; gauge-fixing part zero). Using gamma-counting rules it shows: the phi-psi propagator identity (37) and the SUSY-transformation identity (38) hold at one and two loops; a new phi^4 identity (39) linking the quartic scalar vertex to gauge and superpotential couplings holds at one loop, its two-loop status is open. Conclusion: no general proof; checked identities hold up to two loops; at higher orders SUSY relations could fail, then requiring counterterms, but much fewer than in DREG.
  Loop order / theory: general structure all orders; identities (37),(38) at 1 and 2 loops; (39) at 1 loop / general N=1 SUSY gauge theory, simple gauge group, chiral matter (ST identity with constant ghosts, conventions of ref. [7])
  Scheme: DRED in Q4S, anticommuting gamma5 (eq. 8), Tr 1 = 4 (eq. 57), traces with gamma5 and >= 4 gamma matrices vanish for non-integer d (footnote 1); alternative 4-dimensional gamma5bar of HVBM type (eqs. 9-11) rejected because it breaks SUSY and gauge invariance at one loop (eq. 36).
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP: S(Gamma_DRED) = i [S(Gamma_cl)].Gamma_DRED (eq. 34). S(Gamma_cl) is non-zero only through terms that vanish by 4-dimensional Fierz rearrangement; diagrams with this insertion vanish if the gamma string B in the attached fermion loop/line contains at most 4, 2 or 3 gamma matrices for topologies (a), (b), (c) (eqs. 41a-41d); eq. (42) exhibits a non-vanishing case (topology b with three gamma matrices).
  Proved: 
      - Consistency of the Q4S construction (Sec. 2, App. A).
      - General formula (34)-(35) for the breaking of the ST identity in DRED (given the QAP).
      - Identities (37) and (38) at one and two loops; identity (39) at one loop (Sec. 4.3).
  Assumed: 
      - QAP in DRED: the DREG proof of Breitenlohner-Maison is said to carry over; combinatorial part not discussed; needs D_ij P_jk = i delta_ik on the regularized level (eq. 30) and vanishing scaleless integrals (Sec. 3).
  Checked partially: 
      - Only the three identities (37)-(39); nothing at three loops.
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 1: Siegel identity 0 = D(D-1)(D-2)(D-3)(D-4) (unnumbered).
      - Sec. 2: eqs. (3)-(5d) Q4S rules; App. A explicit construction, eqs. (55)-(57).
      - Sec. 3: QAP eq. (1), (17)-(19), (30), (32)-(34).
      - Sec. 4.1: previous checks (CJvN NPB 167 for gauge WIs to two loops; ACV assumed QAP, propagator identity to three loops).
      - Sec. 4.2: eqs. (35a)-(35h) breaking terms; (36) extra terms with gamma5bar.
      - Sec. 4.3: eqs. (37)-(42); Figs. 2-4.
      - Sec. 5: conclusions (two-loop usability, no general proof).
  Red flags: 
      - Anticommuting gamma5 in DRED cannot compute gamma5-odd traces (axial anomaly) correctly; the paper suggests Larin/Trueman-type prescriptions as models (refs [25]).
      - The 4-dimensional gamma5bar (BMHV-like) breaks SUSY already at one loop in DRED.
      - The phi^4 identity (39), the closest analogue of 'quartic coupling = gauge coupling squared', is not established at two loops here.
      - The QAP for DRED is argued by analogy, not proved in full (combinatorics not discussed).
