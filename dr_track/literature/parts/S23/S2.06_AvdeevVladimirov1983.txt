[S2.06_AvdeevVladimirov1983] L.V. Avdeev, A.A. Vladimirov -- Dimensional regularization and supersymmetry (1983)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B 219 (1983) 262-276
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: Detailed analysis of supersymmetric dimensional regularization: a manifestly supersymmetric (superfield) version leads to contradictions; removing the contradictions breaks SUSY at higher orders; an explicit three-loop example of SUSY breakdown is analysed at the diagram level; the validity of DR for global SUSY gauge theories is reconsidered.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops (explicit example); general higher-order statement / globally supersymmetric gauge theories
  Scheme: Superfield DRED versus its consistent component reformulation.
  Symmetry argument: Dichotomy: manifest SUSY (superfield) + DR is inconsistent; consistent component DR is not manifestly SUSY and fails Ward identities at higher orders.
  Proved: 
      - The dichotomy at the level of the regularization algebra (consistent with S2.02, S2.10).
