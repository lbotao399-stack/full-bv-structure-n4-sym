[S3.03_MartinSanchezRuiz1999] C.P. Martin, D. Sanchez-Ruiz -- Action principles, restoration of BRS symmetry and the renormalization group equation for chiral non-Abelian gauge theories in dimensional renormalization with a non-anticommuting gamma5 (1999)
  arXiv: hep-th/9905076   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B 572 (2000) 387 (journal ref not shown in the text accessed)
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: One-loop renormalization of a general chiral non-Abelian gauge theory (left- and right-handed fermion multiplets, no scalars) in BMHV: the evanescent part of the fermion kinetic term breaks BRS invariance (s_d S_0 = Deltahat, eq. 34); the breaking is computed via the regularized action principles QAP1-QAP4 (eqs. 14-17) and Bonneau identities (eq. 25); the BRS anomaly is obtained for the first time in this framework (essential chiral anomaly, Sec. 3.6) and finite counterterms S_fct^(1) (ansatz eq. 66: (dA)^2, A box A, cubic and quartic gauge terms with d^{abc} and T^{abcd}, fermion kinetic and source terms) restore the ST identity iff d_{L-R}^{abc} = 0; the RGE is worked out in the anomaly-free case.
  Loop order / theory: 1 loop (explicit); all-order consistency of BMHV quoted from S3.02 / chiral non-Abelian gauge theory, simple and then general compact group, no scalars
  Scheme: BMHV DREG, MS for singular parts, finite counterterms fixed algebraically.
  Symmetry argument: Regularized QAP: breaking = N[O].Gamma_ren (eq. 18); its finite four-dimensional part from Bonneau identities (residues of simple poles); cohomological split into removable (b-exact) part and anomaly.
  Proved: 
      - At one loop: BRS restorable by finite counterterms iff the gauge anomaly coefficient vanishes; explicit anomaly expression S(Gamma_ren) = (hbar/(4 pi)^2)(2/3) int eps Tr_{L-R}[omega d(dA A - (i/2) A A A)] + O(hbar^2).
  Assumed: 
      - Absence of anomaly at higher orders argued from the Adler-Bardeen theorem (expectation, not computed).
