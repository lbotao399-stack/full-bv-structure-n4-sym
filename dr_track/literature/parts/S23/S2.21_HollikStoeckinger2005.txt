[S2.21_HollikStoeckinger2005] W. Hollik, D. Stoeckinger -- MSSM Higgs-boson mass predictions and two-loop non-supersymmetric counterterms (2005)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0509298   Journal: Phys. Lett. B 634 (2006) 63-68
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: For the Yukawa-enhanced two-loop MSSM Higgs-mass corrections (O(alpha_t,b alpha_s), O(alpha_t,b^2), O(alpha_t alpha_b), gaugeless limit) DRED preserves the needed SUSY: the relevant ST identity (fifth derivative of S(Gamma) w.r.t. three Higgs components, a Higgsino and the SUSY ghost) holds, so the non-supersymmetric two-loop quartic Higgs counterterm vanishes and multiplicative (symmetric) renormalization is justified.
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops (Yukawa-enhanced, gaugeless) / MSSM (Higgs sector)
  Scheme: DRED of S2.10 with anticommuting gamma5.
  Symmetry argument: ST identity S(Gamma)=0 with constant SUSY ghost (eq. 7); its two-loop breaking rewritten as a single insertion [S(Gamma^(0))] (eq. 18, method of S2.10); gamma-matrix counting (properties after eq. 19) and momentum arguments (eq. 20) make all breaking diagrams vanish.
  Proved: 
      - Delta^(2),DRED = 0 for the identity of eqs. (8)-(10) at the stated orders (eq. 21), hence delta^(2) V^quartic_non-susy = 0 (eq. 22).
  Assumed: 
      - QAP in DRED (as in S2.10).

  Red flags:
