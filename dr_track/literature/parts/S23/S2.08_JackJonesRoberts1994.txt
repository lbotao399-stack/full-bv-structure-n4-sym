[S2.08_JackJonesRoberts1994] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, K.L. Roberts -- Equivalence of dimensional reduction and dimensional regularisation (1994)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9401349   Journal: Z. Phys. C 63 (1994) 151-160
  Section: 2 DRED consistency / FDH / HV / epsilon-scalars
  Claim: For a general renormalizable theory, DRED (with epsilon-scalars carrying their own evanescent couplings, renormalized so that Green functions with external epsilon-scalars are finite) is equivalent to DREG to all orders: the two are related by an analytic redefinition of all couplings, so S-matrix elements agree. The van Damme-'t Hooft unitarity problem is attributed to their counterterm prescription.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (formal argument); explicit one- and two-loop formulae, three-loop structure only / general renormalizable theory (gauge fields + scalars; fermions said to follow straightforwardly)
  Scheme: DRED realized as DREG plus a multiplet of epsilon-scalars of multiplicity E, with E = epsilon set after expansion ('System 3'); MS.
  Symmetry argument: Not a SUSY argument: renormalization-theory argument (scheme change = coupling redefinition). For SUSY theories, identifying evanescent with real couplings is stated to be consistent because their beta functions coincide (App. A).
  Proved: 
      - DRED-DREG equivalence via coupling redefinitions at the level of the formal all-order argument (Sec. 3), with explicit one- and two-loop beta-function differences (App. A).
  Assumed: 
      - gamma5 and epsilon^{mu nu rho sigma} issues explicitly excluded (Sec. 1)
      - SUSY of DRED itself is not addressed; equality of evanescent and real beta functions in SUSY theories is taken as input
  Checked partially: 
      - Two-loop delta Z^{WW} and delta Z^{psibar psi} compared with earlier calculations (App. A).
  Evidence: 
      - Sec. 1: van Damme-'t Hooft problem traced to counterterm prescription; gamma5 not addressed.
      - Sec. 3: evanescent couplings introduced; equivalence by analytic redefinition lambda -> lambda', lambda_E -> lambda'_E.
      - App. A: one- and two-loop formulae; two-loop delta Z^{WW} = g^4 C2(G)^2/[2(16 pi^2)^2 eps].
      - eq. nos. lost in ar5iv conversion (not verified).
  Red flags: 
      - The all-order equivalence is a statement about the full theory including evanescent couplings; it does not imply that DRED preserves SUSY.
      - Excludes gamma5/epsilon-tensor traces, which are the source of the higher-loop subtleties (S2.13).
      - Sec. 4 suggests natural values (h = g) are acceptable for S-matrix predictions but not for running only the real-coupling beta functions.
