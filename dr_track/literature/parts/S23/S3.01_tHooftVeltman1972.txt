[S3.01_tHooftVeltman1972] G. 't Hooft, M.J.G. Veltman -- Regularization and renormalization of gauge fields (1972)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B 44 (1972) 189-213
  Section: 3 BMHV (incl. SUSY applications)
  Claim: Introduces dimensional regularization for gauge theories: separates overlapping divergences, preserves unitarity and causality, allows shifts of integration variables; Ward identities hold throughout when there are no anomalies; makes manifest where anomalies (Bell-Jackiw-Adler) can arise. Contains the non-anticommuting gamma5 (gamma5 anticommutes with the first four gamma matrices and commutes with the others) that is the 'HV' part of BMHV.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (method) / gauge theories (Yang-Mills type, renormalizable theories)
  Scheme: DREG with 't Hooft-Veltman gamma5.
  Symmetry argument: Gauge invariance of the d-dimensional regularized action (vector-like theories); anomalies localized in gamma5-dependent pieces.
  Proved: 
      - Unitarity/causality and Ward identities for anomaly-free cases (as stated in the abstract).

  Red flags: 
      - For chiral couplings the HV gamma5 breaks gauge invariance of the regularized action by evanescent terms; restoration requires finite counterterms (worked out much later, S3.03 onward).
