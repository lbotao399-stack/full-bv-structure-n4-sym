[S5.01_KoernerKreimerSchilcher1992] J.G. Koerner, D. Kreimer, K. Schilcher (and D. Kreimer 1990) -- A practicable gamma5-scheme in dimensional regularization (KKS); The gamma5-problem and anomalies - a Clifford algebra approach (Kreimer 1990) (1992)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Z. Phys. C54 (1992) 503-512 (KKS); Phys. Lett. B237 (1990) 59 (Kreimer)
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Keep an anticommuting gamma5 in D dimensions and give up cyclicity of the trace: gamma5-odd traces are evaluated with a non-cyclic linear functional ('Trace') that agrees with the 4d trace at D=4; the result then depends on where the trace is started (the 'reading point'), and the ABJ anomaly appears as the non-cyclic output of the scheme. KKS turn this into a practical prescription and show (per Kreimer 1994) that starting the reading at different points in graphs contributing to the same process can violate gauge invariance in the SM.
  Loop order / theory: one-loop anomaly and low-order applications (details not seen) / QED/QCD/electroweak SM fermion loops
  Scheme: DREG with anticommuting gamma5, non-cyclic trace functional, reading-point prescription; epsilon tensor projected on the 4d index set
  Symmetry argument: Ward identities of vector vertices kept by anticommutation; the anomaly localized at the reading point (axial vertex)
  Proved:
      (none)
  Assumed:
      - a consistent rule for choosing reading points in every graph and its counterterm graphs (formalized only in Kreimer 1994, S5.02)
  Checked partially:
      - one-loop anomaly reproduced; equivalence of reading points of types i_+ and i_I found in the 1-loop anomaly analysis (as reported in S5.02)
  Evidence:
      - Kreimer hep-ph/9401354 refs. [4], [6] and Sec. 2: AAA = (1/3) AVV obtained by Bose-symmetrizing over reading points; gauge-invariance violation in the SM if reading starts at different points in graphs of the same process (citing [6] = KKS)
  Red flags:
      - not on arXiv; content known only through Kreimer hep-ph/9401354 and later citations
      - the prescription is incomplete without a rule for multi-loop graphs with subdivergences, several gamma5-odd loops, or anomalous subgraphs; later works (S5.07_Chen2023, S5.11, S5.12) show residual ambiguities at high loop order
  Cross-checks:
      - formalized in S5.02_Kreimer1994; critique/alternatives in S5.03_Jegerlehner2001; modern variant in S5.07_Chen2023
  Access / confidence: secondary: cited in hep-ph/9401354 (full text read) / medium
