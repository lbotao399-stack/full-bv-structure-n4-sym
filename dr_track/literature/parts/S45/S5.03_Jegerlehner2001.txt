[S5.03_Jegerlehner2001] F. Jegerlehner -- Facts of life with gamma5 (2001)
  arXiv: hep-th/0005255   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C18 (2001) 673-679
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: In D = 4 - 2 epsilon an anticommuting gamma5 with a cyclic trace is incompatible with the nonzero 4d trace Tr(gamma^mu gamma^nu gamma^rho gamma^sigma gamma5); one must choose between the trace condition and the Ward-Takahashi identities. Keeping a non-vanishing anticommutator makes the fermion propagator effectively 4-dimensional and fermion loops are then not regularized. Recommends for practice: strictly anticommuting gamma5 (NDR) for everything except hard anomalies, and treat gamma5-odd closed loops (VVA-type) as intrinsically 4-dimensional, fixed by Bose symmetry and vector-current conservation (Adler's approach), relying on Adler-Bardeen for higher orders.
  Loop order / theory: general; examples at 1 loop; NDR use claimed at 1-2 loops and beyond / electroweak SM
  Scheme: discussion of HV/BM, NDR, 'chirally improved HV' (AC(mu) != 0) and 'quasi self-chiral' (AC(mu) = 0) schemes
  Symmetry argument: gauge invariance (WT identities) prioritized over the trace condition; anomaly cancellation in the SM by quark-lepton duality
  Proved:
      - inconsistency argument for cyclic trace + anticommuting gamma5 + nonzero epsilon trace in D != 4 (eqs. (1)-(5))
      - non-regularization argument when AC(mu) != 0 (Sec. 3, eq. (31))
  Assumed:
      - that treating gamma5-odd loops as 4-dimensional with Bose symmetry and vector current conservation (eq. (45)) is sufficient at higher orders, via the Adler-Bardeen theorem
  Checked partially:
      - App.: one-loop SM self energies and vertices independent of AC terms
  Evidence:
      - Secs. 1-4 and App. (as listed); eqs. (1)-(5), (31), (45)
  Red flags:
      - does not analyse the non-cyclic (reading-point) trace formally; KKS/Kreimer cited only in reference lists
      - no explicit statement about 3-loop and higher SM diagrams with gamma5-odd traces (where the 4d treatment of subloops embedded in divergent graphs is precisely the issue)
