[S5.04_Larin1993] S.A. Larin -- The renormalization of the axial anomaly in dimensional regularization (1993)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9302240   Journal: Phys. Lett. B303 (1993) 113
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Practical multiloop gamma5 prescription: 't Hooft-Veltman gamma5 = i(1/4!) epsilon gamma gamma gamma gamma with the epsilon tensor kept outside the R-operation, symmetrized axial current J5 = i(1/3!) epsilon_{mu nu1 nu2 nu3} psi-bar gamma^nu1 gamma^nu2 gamma^nu3 psi; products of two epsilon tensors rewritten as D-dimensional metric determinants. Finite renormalization Z5 restores the anticommuting-gamma5 Ward identities: Z5^ns fixed by equality of renormalized axial and vector vertices; Z5^s fixed by demanding the one-loop character of the anomaly equation. Gives the three-loop singlet axial anomalous dimension.
  Loop order / theory: 3 (Z_MS and anomalous dimensions); Z5 displayed to 2 loops (3-loop in ref. [10] there) / massless QCD (non-singlet and singlet axial, pseudoscalar currents)
  Scheme: DREG + MS-bar, HV/BM-type gamma5 (Larin scheme), epsilon tensors outside DR then contracted as D-dim determinants (eq. (9)); finite Z5 factors
  Symmetry argument: finite renormalization restoring anticommuting Ward identity (non-singlet) and one-loop exactness of the ABJ equation in renormalized form (singlet), via anomalous-dimension relations gamma_GG~ = -beta/a and gamma_GJ relation (eq. (28))
  Proved:
      - equivalence of the symmetrized definitions (eqs. (3), (4)) argued generally and checked at 3 loops on the axial vertex
      - explicit 3-loop Z_MS for P, S, singlet currents (eqs. (13), (19)), Z5^p (eq. (15)), singlet anomalous dimension (eq. (34))
  Assumed:
      - Z5^s is fixed by imposing the Adler-Bardeen form of the renormalized anomaly equation (a physical requirement, not derived)
      - eq. (27) admits more general solutions than the adopted eq. (28); choice supported by a two-loop check
  Checked partially:
      - anomaly matching uses three-loop left side vs two-loop right side (eqs. (30), (32)); massive case asserted only
  Evidence:
      - eqs. (2)-(4) prescription; (5)-(10) non-singlet Z5 (2-loop: 1 - 4 C_F a + a^2 (22 C_F^2 - 107/9 C_F C_A + 2/9 C_F n_f)); (20)-(34) singlet; Z5^s eq. (33) differs from Z5^ns only in the C_F n_f term
  Red flags:
      - BRST/chiral Ward identities are restored only for the currents considered (QCD with external axial currents); a full chiral gauge theory needs the general BMHV symmetry-restoring counterterm program (section 3 records)
      - the singlet finite renormalization is defined by requiring the anomaly equation, so the Adler-Bardeen property is input, not output
  Cross-checks:
      - extended to 4 loops in S5.05_AhmedChenCzakon2021 and S5.06_ChenCzakon2022
      - ABJ operator relation in anticommuting schemes: S5.07_Chen2023
