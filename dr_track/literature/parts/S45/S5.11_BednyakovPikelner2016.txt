[S5.11_BednyakovPikelner2016] A.V. Bednyakov, A.F. Pikelner -- Four-loop strong coupling beta-function in the Standard Model (2016)
  arXiv: 1508.02680   Journal: Phys. Lett. B762 (2016) 151-156
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Four-loop beta function of g_s in the SM with g_s, y_t, lambda (electroweak gauge couplings neglected). The gamma5 ambiguity arises from 72 diagrams with two fermion traces containing gamma5 from Goldstone (chi, phi+-) Yukawa vertices, at order a_s^2 a_t^2 T_F^2. With anticommuting gamma5 and the trace cut at a 'reading point', three classes give different divergent results: A (both traces start/end at external vertices), B (only one), C (both at internal vertices); the divergent ambiguity is a_s^2 a_t^2 T_F^2/epsilon (X1 + X2 zeta3) (eq. (8)) with A giving (4/3 + 8 zeta3) (eq. (9)), B twice, C three times; in beta_3 it appears as R [16/3 + 32 zeta3] with 1 <= R <= 3 (eqs. (14), (18)). The authors adopt R = 3 (prescription C).
  Loop order / theory: 4 / SM, gaugeless limit (g1 = g2 = 0)
  Scheme: DREG, MS-bar, anticommuting gamma5 with non-cyclic traces cut at a reading point; cross-check with infrared rearrangement and BMHV algebra applied after tensor integration
  Symmetry argument: transversality of the finite gluon self-energy (cutting at external vertices breaks it), charge-conjugation consistency (eqs. (6)-(7), (12)-(13)), a Fierz-identity check (eq. (15)) and the IRR/BMHV recalculation independent of the cut point - all supporting R = 3
  Proved:
      - explicit 4-loop result up to the R-dependent term; xi independence; reproduction of pure QCD and gluino-model results
  Assumed:
      - R = 3 adopted on the basis of transversality/C-symmetry/Fierz arguments, not from a regularization proven consistent for chiral theories
  Checked partially:
      - the IRR + BMHV-after-integration recalculation (cut-point independent) supports R = 3
  Evidence:
      - eqs. (3), (6)-(9), (12)-(15), (17)-(18); Fig. 1; numerical statement: non-trivial gamma5 terms reduce beta_3 by about 0.5 percent
  Red flags:
      - the first version missed the remaining ambiguity, pointed out by Zoller (acknowledgments) - shows how subtle the reading point is at four loops
      - the size of the ambiguous term exceeds the pure-QCD five-loop correction numerically (authors' remark)
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.12_Zoller2016 (R = 1 originally; added note says R = 3 is suggested); confirmed by WCC in S5.17_PooleThomsen2019a (R = 3) and used in S5.19
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv / high
  Notes: This is the concrete 'reading point' ambiguity in the SM at four loops: R in {1,2,3}, coefficient R(16/3 + 32 zeta3) T_F^2 a_s^2 a_t^2.
