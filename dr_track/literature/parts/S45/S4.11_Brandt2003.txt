[S4.11_Brandt2003] F. Brandt -- Extended BRST cohomology, consistent deformations and anomalies of four-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theories (2003)
  arXiv: hep-th/0212070   Journal: JHEP 0304 (2003) 035
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: Complete computation, without restrictions on mass dimension or ghost number, of the local cohomology of the extended BRST differential that includes rigid N=1 SUSY and Poincare transformations through constant ghosts, for SYM + chiral matter (+ linear multiplets). For semisimple gauge groups without linear multiplets the candidate anomalies are exhausted by supersymmetric versions of the non-abelian chiral anomalies and the first-order deformations are standard superspace integrals; abelian factors and linear multiplets give additional deformations and candidate anomalies.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (cohomological; first-order deformations / lowest-order anomalies) / N=1 SYM + chiral multiplets (+ linear multiplets with 2-form potentials), components, on-shell (WZ-gauge-type) formulation; Sec. 9 also with auxiliary fields and with linearly realized SUSY
  Scheme: no regulator; classical local BRST cohomology
  Symmetry argument: extended BRST differential s_ext with constant ghosts c^mu (translations), c^{mu nu} (Lorentz), commuting spinor ghosts xi^alpha, xi-bar (SUSY, ghost number 1); antifield-dependent terms quadratic in antifields in L_ext (eq. (2.24)) encode on-shell closure; s_ext^2 = 0 on fields, antifields and constant ghosts
  Proved:
      - H^{1,4} (candidate anomalies): for semisimple gauge group without linear multiplets only representative (7.8), a supersymmetric generalization of the non-abelian chiral anomaly (Sec. 7, comments (g), (m)); no further SUSY anomaly candidate
      - additional candidates only with abelian factors / linear multiplets: (7.9)-(7.11) (abelian factors under which matter is uncharged), (7.12)-(7.13) (at least two abelian factors), (7.14), (7.15) (supersymmetrized abelian chiral anomalies)
      - H^{0,4} (counterterms / deformations): representatives (7.1)-(7.6), generic invariants (7.6) infinitely many of arbitrary dimension (comment (f))
      - with auxiliary fields every representative of H^4 can be made antifield independent (comment (c)); antifield dependence otherwise reflects characteristic cohomology
  Assumed:
      - the step from cohomology to renormalized perturbation theory (quantum action principle, a regularization) is outside the paper; anomalies are classified only to lowest nontrivial order
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2, eqs. (2.8)-(2.21): s_ext on fields and constant ghosts; (2.22) c-hat^mu = c^mu - x^nu c_nu^mu; (2.23)-(2.24) L_ext with quadratic antifield terms
      - Secs. 4-5: change of variables and gauge covariant algebra, eqs. (5.1)-(5.2); Sec. 6(e) supersymmetry algebra cohomology; Lemmas 6.1-6.3 extra invariants for abelian factors (6.22)-(6.25)
      - Sec. 7: counterterms (7.1)-(7.6), anomalies (7.8)-(7.15)
  Red flags:
      - classical cohomology only; which finite counterterms a concrete scheme (DREG/DRED/BMHV) needs is not addressed
      - abelian factors (e.g. U(1) factors with neutral matter, as in U(N) theories) enlarge both the deformation and the anomaly cohomology
  Cross-checks:
