[S5.15_AntipinEtAl2013] O. Antipin, M. Gillioz, J. Krog, E. Molgaard, F. Sannino -- Standard Model vacuum stability and Weyl consistency conditions (2013)
  arXiv: 1306.3234   Journal: JHEP 08 (2013) 034
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: The WCC (gradient-flow relation from Osborn's equation, eqs. (4)-(6), leading metric chi = diag(1/alpha1^2, 3/alpha2^2, 8/alpha3^2, 2/alpha_t, 4), eq. (8)) can be satisfied order by order only with a loop counting in which gauge, Yukawa and quartic beta functions are taken at 3-2-1 (or 4-3-2, 5-4-3) loops; the then state-of-the-art 3-3-3 truncation violates them. Applied to SM vacuum stability: small change in the instability scale, larger effect on metastability/degenerate-minimum questions.
  Loop order / theory: 3-2-1 counting (and higher patterns) / SM
  Scheme: MS-bar beta functions from the literature; no discussion of gamma5 or of the regularization in which the WCC hold
  Symmetry argument: Weyl consistency (abelian Weyl anomaly)
  Proved:
      - structural statement: 3-3-3 truncation is inconsistent with the WCC (a term like alpha2 alpha_t alpha_lambda^2 in a-tilde needs a 3-loop beta_t and a 4-loop beta_2)
  Assumed:
      - WCC valid for the SM in MS-bar as computed (gamma5 not discussed)
      - beta_i = B_i at the orders considered (dimension-3 current subtlety noted)
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:
      - eqs. (4)-(6), (8); conclusions on lambda zero-crossing near 10^10 GeV (3-2-1)
