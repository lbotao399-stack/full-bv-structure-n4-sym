[S4.08_FischerHollikRothStoeckinger2004] I. Fischer, W. Hollik, M. Roth, D. Stoeckinger -- Restoration of supersymmetric Slavnov-Taylor and Ward identities in presence of soft and spontaneous symmetry breaking (2004)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0310191   Journal: Phys. Rev. D69 (2004) 015004
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts (+ SUSY-restoring counterterms)
  Claim: In an abelian model (SQED plus a neutral Higgs-like chiral multiplet with a VEV, R-symmetry, spurion soft breaking) the ST identities relating electron and selectron masses to the Yukawa coupling are derived and the one-loop symmetry-restoring counterterms computed: the identities hold in DRED and fail in DREG; DREG even violates softly broken SUSY (terms proportional to the photino soft mass).
  Loop order / theory: 1 / SQED + neutral chiral multiplet with VEV, soft breaking via spurion eta -> theta theta f0
  Scheme: DRED vs DREG with anticommuting gamma5, one loop
  Symmetry argument: S = S0 + S_soft (eqs. (2.10)-(2.12)), S(Gamma)=0 and nilpotency condition on A^mu (eqs. (2.17)-(2.18)), constant SUSY ghost; identities from derivatives of S(Gamma): electron-selectron relation (eqs. (4.3)-(4.4)), SUSY algebra (eq. (4.6)), electron mass Ward identity (eq. (4.2))
  Proved:
      - electron mass Ward identity (4.2) holds in DRED and DREG
      - electron-selectron ST identity (4.4): breaking vanishes in DRED, nonzero in DREG (eq. (4.12)), including terms proportional to the soft photino mass
      - DREG restoring counterterms nonzero (eqs. (4.40)-(4.44), incl. a new spurion-related Xi interaction (4.44)); breaking coefficients a1,a2,a3,a5,a6 nonzero in DREG, a3 vanishes in DRED (Sec. 4.4)
      - SUSY algebra identity (4.6) and high-momentum limits respected by both schemes
  Assumed:
      - results of an inconsistent scheme (DRED) usable because they differ from a consistent scheme only by local terms of dimension <= 4 (Sec. 3.1, item 3)
  Checked partially:
      - only two identities for vertex functions treated as examples (Introduction); some counterterms left undetermined or dependent on unphysical parameters u3 (Sec. 4.4); the P_R-projected identity not fully evaluated
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.1 model, eqs. (2.4), (2.6) spurion; Sec. 2.2 identities; Sec. 3 counterterm structure; Sec. 4.1-4.4 results
  Red flags:
      - limited set of identities; DRED statement is one-loop and partial
      - DREG with anticommuting gamma5 (not BMHV) - itself not consistent beyond the traces used
  Cross-checks:
      - same pattern as S4.05_HollikKrausStoeckinger1999 and S4.06_HollikStoeckinger2001
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv (main text); INSPIRE for journal data / high
  Notes: Shows that the DREG breaking is not only of hard SUSY: it also violates soft-breaking relations, which a constant-ghost ST identity with spurion doublets detects.
