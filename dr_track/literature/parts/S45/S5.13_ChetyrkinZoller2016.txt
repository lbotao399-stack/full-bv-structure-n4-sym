[S5.13_ChetyrkinZoller2016] K.G. Chetyrkin, M.F. Zoller -- Leading QCD-induced four-loop contributions to the beta-function of the Higgs self-coupling in the SM (2016)
  arXiv: 1604.00853   Journal: JHEP 06 (2016) 175
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Leading four-loop terms y_t^4 g_s^6 in beta_lambda (eq. (11)), y_t g_s^8 in beta_yt (eq. (12)) and y_t^2 g_s^6 in the Higgs anomalous dimension (eq. (13)), in QCD + top Yukawa. Only diagrams with external H are kept, so gamma5 (which appears only in chi and phi+- Yukawa vertices) is avoided completely.
  Loop order / theory: 4 (leading QCD-induced terms) / QCD + top Yukawa (electroweak gauge couplings, lambda, other Yukawas neglected)
  Scheme: DREG, MS-bar, massive tadpoles; gamma5 never enters
  Symmetry argument: choice of external Higgs legs avoids gamma5; standard DR action principle (modulo axial anomalies)
  Proved:
      - explicit results; agreement with Martin's effective-potential result (eq. (4.32) of Martin 2015) and vacuum-energy relation (eq. (30)); gamma_0 = gamma_m^SS/12 (eq. (36))

  Evidence:

  Red flags:
