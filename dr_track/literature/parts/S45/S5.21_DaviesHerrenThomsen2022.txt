  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: All coefficients of the general 4-loop gauge and 3-loop Yukawa beta functions (510 unknowns) from new diagrammatic computations in SM-adjacent models plus 266 WCC, without using the T_IJ symmetry conjecture (thereby verifying it at 4-3-2). gamma5-odd contributions are ignored in the new computations; the 5 gamma5-odd Yukawa structures are fixed by matching to SM/2HDM results where odd traces were evaluated in 4 dimensions; the 4 gamma5-odd gauge structures (g199-g202) are completely predicted by the WCC from the 3-loop Yukawa ones, confirming R = 3.
