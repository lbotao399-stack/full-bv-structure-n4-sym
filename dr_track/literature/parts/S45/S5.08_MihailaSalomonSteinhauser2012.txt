[S5.08_MihailaSalomonSteinhauser2012] L.N. Mihaila, J. Salomon, M. Steinhauser -- Gauge coupling beta functions in the Standard Model to three loops; Renormalization constants and beta functions for the gauge couplings of the Standard Model to three-loop order (2012)
  arXiv: 1201.5868; 1208.3357   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 108 (2012) 151602; Phys. Rev. D86 (2012) 096008
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Three-loop MS-bar beta functions of the three SM gauge couplings (with top, bottom, tau Yukawas and lambda, which first enters at three loops). gamma5-odd traces can contribute only in diagrams with at least two closed fermion loops (two epsilon tensors needed); of the three-loop classes, one cannot give problematic terms, one vanishes (traces with five gamma matrices), and the class of fermion triangles with three external gauge bosons cancels after summing over all fermions and orientations by ABJ anomaly cancellation in the SM.
  Loop order / theory: 3 / SM (unbroken phase with Lorenz gauge and general gauge parameters; broken phase in background field gauge)
  Scheme: DREG d = 4 - 2 epsilon, MS-bar; naive anticommuting gamma5 with triangle-anomaly diagrams set to zero from the start; cross-check with a 'semi-naive' prescription (eq. (14)) using a formal epsilon-tilde tensor (eq. (15)); MINCER/MATAD
  Symmetry argument: gauge-anomaly cancellation in the SM spectrum removes the dangerous gamma5-odd contributions; semi-naive ambiguity is O(epsilon) and affected terms produce at most simple poles, hence unambiguous
  Proved:
      - explicit three-loop results (eqs. (22)-(24)); gauge-parameter independence; agreement Lorenz vs background field gauge; BBB vertex finite
  Assumed:
      - that setting anomaly-type triangles to zero (naive gamma5) is legitimate because of SM anomaly cancellation (Sec. II.4, footnote 4)
  Checked partially:
      - semi-naive cross-check only for the classes where single poles occur
  Evidence:
      - 1208.3357 Sec. II.4, eqs. (12)-(15): gamma5 discussion and the three classes of two-fermion-loop diagrams
      - agreement with the general gauge theory three-loop result of Pickering et al. after two corrections to their eq. (33) (eqs. (28)-(30), App. D)
  Red flags:
      - the argument is specific to anomaly-free fermion content and to three loops; it does not say anything about four loops
      - the naive treatment relies on cancellations between diagrams, not on a consistent regularization of individual graphs
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.10_BednyakovPikelnerVelizhanin2013 (independent 3-loop gauge beta), Pickering-Gracey-Jones general 3-loop gauge beta
