[S4.10_KrausRuppSibold2003] E. Kraus, C. Rupp, K. Sibold -- Supersymmetric Yang-Mills theories with local coupling: the supersymmetric gauge (2003)
  arXiv: hep-th/0212064   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B661 (2003) 83-98
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: With manifest superspace SUSY and a space-time dependent complex coupling, the local-coupling anomaly appears in two identities (non-renormalization of the topological term; relation between gauge-coupling and complex-supercoupling renormalization); only one can be maintained. The two versions give a non-holomorphic, or a holomorphic but anomalously related, beta function; the closed form beta_{g^2} = beta-hat^(1) g^4 (1 + r_eta^(1) g^2) arises in one normalization, higher orders being scheme dependent.
  Loop order / theory: anomaly at 1 loop; all-order statements on the structure of beta / pure N=1 SYM in superspace, local complex coupling
  Scheme: assumes consistent SUSY-invariant schemes (BPHZ/BPHZL or Wilsonian), no specific regulator; soft gauge-breaking masses for off-shell IR (eqs. (19)-(20)), ST identity up to soft terms
  Symmetry argument: algebraic: anomaly classification (eqs. (39)-(40)); shifting the one-loop anomaly (eq. (24)) into the ST identity (eq. (26)) or the shift equation (eqs. (27)-(28))
  Proved:
      - eq. (24) is the only anomaly (classification eqs. (39)-(40), with an exception at one loop noted by the authors)
      - two consistent versions (eqs. (32)-(33), (46), (49) vs (35)-(36), (47)); beta formula (eq. (50)); higher orders change under finite redefinitions (eqs. (37)-(38))
  Assumed:
      - r_eta^(1) not computed here; taken from the WZ-gauge calculation (S4.09_Kraus2002) and the two-loop beta
      - existence of SUSY-invariant schemes
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:

  Red flags:
