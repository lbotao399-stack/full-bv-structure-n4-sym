[S5.14_JackOsborn1990] I. Jack, H. Osborn (1990); H. Osborn (1991) -- Analogs of the c-theorem for four-dimensional renormalisable field theories; Weyl consistency conditions and a local renormalisation group equation for general renormalisable field theories (1990)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B343 (1990) 647-688; Nucl. Phys. B363 (1991) 486-526
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Composite-operator products on curved space with x-dependent couplings lead to a local renormalization group equation realizing Weyl rescalings; its consistency (abelian nature of Weyl transformations) gives relations among trace-anomaly coefficients and beta functions to all orders (Osborn's equation d_I A-tilde = T_IJ beta^J + ...), a 4d analog of the c-theorem; BRS analysis for gauge theories; Yukawa beta constrained at two loops; positivity of the metric only at weak coupling.
  Loop order / theory: all-order structure; explicit low-order checks / general renormalizable 4d theories (gauge, Yukawa, scalar), curved background, local couplings
  Scheme: dimensional regularization in the explicit computations (per later usage); Osborn 1991 states the Curci-Paffuti relation is derived without commitment to a scheme; gamma5/chiral fermions not addressed in the abstracts
  Symmetry argument: Wess-Zumino-type consistency of local Weyl transformations with x-dependent couplings
  Proved:
      - (per abstracts) consistency relations from the local RG; one-loop trace-anomaly results extended to all orders
  Assumed:
      - existence of a regularization and renormalization of composite operators with local couplings compatible with the local RG and with gauge (BRS) invariance; for chiral fermions this is exactly the gamma5 question (not discussed in the abstracts)
      - parity-conserving trace anomaly (made explicit in later works, e.g. S5.18 Sec. 2.2)
