[S5.12_Zoller2016] M.F. Zoller -- Top-Yukawa effects on the beta-function of the strong coupling in the SM at four-loop level (2016)
  arXiv: 1508.03624   Journal: JHEP 02 (2016) 095
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Four-loop top-Yukawa contributions to beta(g_s) in the SM (gaugeless limit, only y_t, lambda kept). Only gluon self-energy diagrams with two fermion loops each carrying an external gluon (72 diagrams) are gamma5-sensitive; two epsilon tensors contract to metrics (eq. (17)). Moving every gamma5 to the external gluon vertex (KKS-type external reading point) is adopted; keeping gamma5 where the Feynman rules put it changes the coefficient by a factor 3; arbitrary anticommutation spoils the result; epsilon-tilde/epsilon^2 terms depend on the reading point. Result: non-naive piece a_t^2 a_s^2 T_F^2 (16/3 + 32 zeta3) in the R = 1 normalization. An added note reports that Bednyakov-Pikelner's internal prescription (R = 3) gives a transverse finite self-energy, suggesting R = 3, while a formal proof is not available.
  Loop order / theory: 4 / SM, gaugeless limit
  Scheme: DREG, MS-bar, naive anticommuting gamma5 except for the sensitive class (external reading point; Larin prescription reported to give the same answer); massive tadpoles with auxiliary mass
  Symmetry argument: reading point at the external gluon vertex (KKS); transversality of the gluon self-energy checked; the author states the issue is not fully understood
  Proved:
      - explicit computation; only 1/epsilon poles in the gamma5-sensitive diagrams; epsilon-tensor uncertainty drops out of the divergent part
  Assumed:
      - the external reading point (R = 1) as 'intuitive'; later note leans to R = 3 without proof
  Checked partially:
      - agreement with Bednyakov-Pikelner when the same prescription is used
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.3, Figs. 2-3, eqs. (16)-(20); eq. (24) (coefficient 80/3 - 32 zeta3 of the g_s^4 y_t^4 T_F^2 term); eqs. (31)-(32), (40); added note eq. (41)
  Red flags:
      - explicit statement that a fully consistent gamma5 treatment is not established at four loops
      - the author notes that in the full SM the scalar sector allows non-naive gauge-sector contributions at higher orders and warns that a complete 4-loop SM calculation must handle them with special care
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.11_BednyakovPikelner2016; S5.17_PooleThomsen2019a (WCC select R = 3)
