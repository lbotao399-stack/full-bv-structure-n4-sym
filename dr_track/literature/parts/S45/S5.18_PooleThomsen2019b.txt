[S5.18_PooleThomsen2019b] C. Poole, A.E. Thomsen -- Constraints on 3- and 4-loop beta-functions in a general four-dimensional Quantum Field Theory (2019)
  arXiv: 1906.04625   Journal: JHEP 09 (2019) 055
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Systematic WCC constraints for general 4d theories: complete 3-loop gauge beta (with kinetic mixing) as a corollary; first predictions of some 4-loop gauge coefficients (g55 = -27/2, g60 = 1/2, g67 = -1/12, g110 = -4, g158 = 0, eq. (5.29)); the gamma5-sensitive g199..202 fixed in terms of 3-loop Yukawa coefficients (eqs. (5.23)-(5.24)); the chiral nature of theories does not affect betas through 3-2-1, first non-trivial gamma5 at 4-3-2.
  Loop order / theory: 3-2-1 complete; 4-3-2 partial / general 4d gauge-Yukawa-scalar theory
  Scheme: MS-bar results (losing manifest scheme independence of the constraints); semi-naive epsilon treatment stated sufficient for the 3-loop Yukawa beta; 3-loop Yukawa values extracted by comparison with SM results (footnote 24)
  Symmetry argument: WCC from Osborn's equation; (3,0) gamma5 lines argued to vanish only in anomaly-free gauge theories
  Proved:
      - set of WCC relations and their solutions at 3-2-1 and partially 4-3-2
  Assumed:
      - parity-conserving trace anomaly (no Pontryagin-type terms) and non-anomalous global flavour symmetries (Sec. 2.2)
      - symmetry of T_IJ via counterterms: conjecture, shown to two loops (Sec. 2.3)
      - the 3-loop Yukawa beta free of gamma5 ambiguity
  Checked partially:
      - corroboration of T_IJ symmetry by ten extra CCs; the purely-gauge 4-loop terms likely under-determined (Sec. 5); one fermionic shift coefficient in S(3) undetermined (Sec. 6)
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 1.1-1.2, 2.2-2.3, 3.1, 4.2 (2890 graphs -> 198 tensor structures), 5.4 (eqs. (5.19)-(5.24), footnote 24), 5.5 (eq. (5.29)), 6 (open problems)
  Red flags:
      - does not discuss whether the local-RG derivation requires a regularization preserving gauge invariance with chiral fermions
      - relies on an external comparison for the 3-loop Yukawa gamma5-odd values
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.17 (letter), S5.19-S5.21 (later completion)
