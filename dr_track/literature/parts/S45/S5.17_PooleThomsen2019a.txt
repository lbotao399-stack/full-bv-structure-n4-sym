[S5.17_PooleThomsen2019a] C. Poole, A.E. Thomsen -- Weyl consistency conditions and gamma5 (2019)
  arXiv: 1901.02749   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 123 (2019) 041602
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Osborn's equation d_I A-tilde = T_IJ beta^J (eq. (1.7)) relates the gamma5-ambiguous coefficients of the general 4-loop gauge beta function (g^(4)_199..202) to 3-loop Yukawa coefficients (y^(3)_304..308) that are taken as unambiguous: g199 = y304/6, g200 = y305/6, g201 = y306/6, g202 = y307/3, y307 = y308 (eq. (3.4)); with y304 = -24, y305 = -12, y306 = y307 = y308 = 8 - 24 zeta3 (eq. (4.1)) this gives g199 = -4, g200 = -2, g201 = 4/3 - 4 zeta3, g202 = 8/3 - 8 zeta3 (eq. (4.2)). For the SM strong coupling this yields (16 + 96 zeta3) T_F^2 a_s^2 a_t^2 (eq. (4.3)), i.e. R = 3 (eq. (4.4)): the trace must be cut at internal vertices. Presented as the first proof that it must be so.
  Loop order / theory: 4-loop gauge / 3-loop Yukawa / general 4d gauge-Yukawa theory; SM
  Scheme: MS-bar; the 3-loop Yukawa inputs from explicit SM computations (semi-naive gamma5); WCC assumed to hold for the 4d beta functions after epsilon -> 0 'for all perturbative renormalization schemes'
  Symmetry argument: Weyl consistency conditions; ABJ anomaly cancellation for other gamma5-odd structures (gauge and scalar betas spared at 3 loops)
  Proved:
      - given the WCC and the 3-loop Yukawa inputs: the 4-loop gauge gamma5-odd coefficients (eq. (4.2)) and R = 3 (eq. (4.4))
  Assumed:
      - validity of the WCC (local RG of Jack-Osborn) in the scheme actually used for the chiral SM, i.e. with an anticommuting/reading-point gamma5 that is not a consistent regularization of chiral gauge theories; not discussed
      - the 3-loop Yukawa gamma5-odd coefficients are unambiguous (single-pole argument of the semi-naive treatment)
      - no lower-order beta insertions contribute to the relevant A-tilde tensors (primitive, topologically symmetric graphs: cubes, Moebius ladders)
      - other diagrams vanish by anomaly cancellation
  Checked partially:
      - agreement with the independent explicit choice R = 3 of S5.11 (transversality arguments)
  Evidence:
      - eqs. (1.1)-(1.7), (3.1)-(3.4), (4.1)-(4.4)
  Red flags:
      - the argument fixes the coefficients by consistency, not by a regularization proven to be consistent; if the WCC derivation itself were scheme-sensitive in the gamma5 sector, the conclusion would be circular
      - the classification of relevant topologies at higher orders is stated to be incomplete; gamma5 may enter more complicated conditions there
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.11_BednyakovPikelner2016 (R = 3); S5.12_Zoller2016 (originally R = 1); used in S5.19 and S5.20, S5.21
