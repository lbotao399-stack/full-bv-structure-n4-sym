[S4.05_HollikKrausStoeckinger1999] W. Hollik, E. Kraus, D. Stoeckinger -- Renormalization and symmetry conditions in supersymmetric QED (1999)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9907393   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C11 (1999) 365-381
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts (+ SUSY-restoring counterterms)
  Claim: SQED in Wess-Zumino gauge is renormalized with an arbitrary (non-invariant) regularization by imposing the Slavnov-Taylor identity with constant SUSY and translation ghosts; the ST identity yields simple all-order symmetry conditions on renormalized vertex functions (electron-selectron mass identity, photon-photino relations, photino coupling = gauge coupling, four-scalar couplings), which fix the non-invariant counterterms. Applied at one loop in DREG, several non-invariant counterterms are needed; the SUSY transformations themselves acquire non-local one-loop corrections.
  Loop order / theory: all-order identities; explicit one-loop application / SQED (massive electron/selectrons), components, WZ gauge, general linear gauge
  Scheme: one-loop: dimensional regularization with BMHV-type gamma5 algebra {gamma^mu, gamma5} = 2 g-hat^{mu nu} gamma_nu gamma5 (g-hat = (D-4)-dimensional metric, set to zero only in final results); DRED is discussed only as invariant-but-inconsistent, with literature statements
  Symmetry argument: ST identity S(Gamma)=0 (eqs. (7), (29), (42)) with constant ghosts: s epsilon = s epsilon-bar = 0, s omega^nu = 2 epsilon sigma^nu epsilon-bar (eqs. (20)-(22)); external sources Y_i for nonlinear transformations (eq. (26)) and a bilinear term Gamma_bil absorbing equation-of-motion terms of the open algebra (eqs. (27)-(28)); gauge fixing with epsilon-dependent compensating terms (eqs. (40)-(41)); only possible anomaly = SUSY extension of Adler-Bardeen, absent in SQED, so all breakings are removable
  Proved:
      - derivation from S(Gamma)=0 of the symmetry conditions: electron-selectron mass identity (eqs. (64)-(67)), photino vertex/SUSY algebra (eqs. (70), (73)), photon-photino self-energy relation (eqs. (89)-(93)), gauge constraints (eqs. (94)-(98)), Thomson-limit conditions incl. photino coupling = gauge coupling (eqs. (99)-(103)), four-scalar couplings (eqs. (104)-(109)); collected in Sec. 3.4, eqs. (110)-(134)
      - explicit one-loop DREG breaking: mass counterterms delta m_phi = (alpha/4pi) m [-2B0 - 2/3], delta m_Psi = (alpha/4pi) m [-2B0 + 1], so the SUSY-restoring difference is (5/3)(alpha/4pi) m; photino coupling needs an extra non-symmetric shift -(1/6)(alpha/4pi) to restore charge universality; photon vertices need none (Sec. 4.3)
      - one-loop SUSY transformations of quantum fields are non-local (B0 terms in vertex functions with Y insertions)
  Assumed:
      - absence of SUSY anomalies beyond the SUSY Adler-Bardeen anomaly (taken from the literature)
      - statements on DRED at one loop (invariant counterterms suffice) are quoted from literature, not recomputed here
  Checked partially:
      - one-loop application covers self energies and selected vertices only
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.2: inductive construction Gamma_eff = Gamma_cl + sum of counterterms (eqs. (9)-(10))
      - Sec. 2: eqs. (12)-(24) BRS transformations, (30) ST operator, (37)-(39) linearized operator and nilpotency, (44) ghost normalization
      - Sec. 4.2: Pi^gamma = Pi^photino = (alpha/4pi) 2 B0(m^2,m^2,p^2) (eq. (142)); delta Z_gamma = -Pi^gamma(0) (eq. (144))
      - Sec. 4.3: mass and photino-coupling non-invariant counterterms (values above); conclusions
  Red flags:
      - DREG with BMHV-type gamma5 used for a SUSY theory: breaks SUSY at one loop by finite local terms that must be added by hand - the prototype of the BMHV+SUSY problem
      - the comparison with DRED relies on literature and on DRED being 'invariant but inconsistent'; behaviour beyond one loop not controlled
  Cross-checks:
      - extended to SQCD in S4.06_HollikStoeckinger2001 and to soft/spontaneous breaking in S4.08_FischerHollikRothStoeckinger2004; framework S4.03_MaggiorePiguetWolf1996

  Notes: Concrete one-loop example of a DREG ST-identity breaking in a SUSY gauge theory: (5/3)(alpha/4pi) m mass split and -(1/6)(alpha/4pi) photino-coupling shift. Useful benchmark for any BV/QME computation of DREG/BMHV breaking terms.
