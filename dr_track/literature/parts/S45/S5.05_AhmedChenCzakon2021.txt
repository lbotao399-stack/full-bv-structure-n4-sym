[S5.05_AhmedChenCzakon2021] T. Ahmed, L. Chen, M. Czakon -- Renormalization of the flavor-singlet axial-vector current and its anomaly in dimensional regularization (2021)
  arXiv: 2101.09479   Journal: JHEP 05 (2021) 087
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: With a non-anticommuting (Larin-type) gamma5 in DR, the singlet axial current renormalization constant Z_J is determined to O(alpha_s^3) from 4-loop matrix elements of both sides of the anomaly equation between the vacuum and two off-shell gluons; the conjectured equality of the MS-bar constant of [F F~]_R with the alpha_s renormalization constant is verified explicitly at 4 loops; the resulting relation gamma_J = (alpha_s/4pi) n_f T_F gamma_FJ at O(alpha_s^4) is used, assuming the anomaly equation holds, to fix the non-MS-bar (finite) part of Z_J.
  Loop order / theory: 4-loop matrix elements; Z_J to alpha_s^3 / massless QCD
  Scheme: DREG + MS-bar, non-anticommuting gamma5 (Larin prescription) with finite Z5^s
  Symmetry argument: renormalized ABJ equation [d J5]_R = (alpha_s/4pi) n_f T_F [F F~]_R imposed; Z_FF~ = Z_alpha_s verified
  Proved:
