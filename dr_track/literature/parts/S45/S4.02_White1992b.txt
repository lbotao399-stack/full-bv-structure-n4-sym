[S4.02_White1992b] P.L. White -- Analysis of the superconformal cohomology structure of N=4 super Yang-Mills (1992)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Class. Quant. Grav. 9 (1992) 413-444
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: Builds, with the Batalin-Vilkovisky method, a nilpotent extended BRST operator that includes the full (on-shell) superconformal symmetry of N=4 SYM through constant ghosts; argues with spectral sequences that its cohomology relevant for anomalies is trivial, so that no symmetry is anomalous, hence no conformal anomaly, hence finiteness to all orders.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (claimed) / N=4 SYM in components, Wess-Zumino-type gauge, superconformal algebra realized with constant ghosts (details not verified)
  Scheme: no regulator; algebraic (cohomological) argument
  Symmetry argument: triviality of the superconformal BV cohomology at ghost number one => all symmetries, including dilatations/special conformal transformations, survive renormalization => vanishing beta function
  Proved:
      - (claimed in the abstract) triviality of the anomaly cohomology of the superconformal BV operator; not verified at equation level
  Assumed:
      - that cohomological triviality suffices to impose the full superconformal Slavnov-Taylor identity order by order, which requires a quantum action principle for x-dependent, nonlinearly realized, on-shell-closing symmetries and normalization conditions compatible with dilatations in a massless theory (how this is handled was not visible)
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:
      - INSPIRE abstract of CQG 9 (1992) 413 (INSPIRE recid 31722): BV construction of the superconformal nilpotent operator, spectral sequences, cohomology trivial, no conformal anomaly, all-order finiteness
      - cited as ref. [9] in hep-th/9507045 and as ref. [3] in hep-th/0605164 (only for conformal invariance / superconformal cohomology)
  Red flags:
      - all-order finiteness from a cohomology statement: in the standard algebraic treatment (Callan-Symanzik equation, supercurrent trace identities; cf. S4.04_Piguet1996 Secs. 7-8) the beta function multiplies the breaking of dilatations by the coupling-constant insertion and the renormalization scale; why this term is excluded by the superconformal consistency conditions is the crux and could not be checked
      - no later paper found that re-derives or audits the result; S4.12_BaulieuBossardSorella2006 cites it without assessment and gives its own (different) algebraic proof
      - not on arXiv; only the abstract was accessible
  Cross-checks:
