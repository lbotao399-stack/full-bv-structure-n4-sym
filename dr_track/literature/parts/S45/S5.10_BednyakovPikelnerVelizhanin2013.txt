[S5.10_BednyakovPikelnerVelizhanin2013] A.V. Bednyakov, A.F. Pikelner, V.N. Velizhanin -- Anomalous dimensions of gauge fields and gauge coupling beta-functions in the SM at three loops; Yukawa coupling beta-functions in the SM at three loops; Higgs self-coupling beta-function in the SM at three loops (2013)
  arXiv: 1210.6873; 1212.6829; 1303.4364   Journal: JHEP 01 (2013) 017; Phys. Lett. B722 (2013) 336; Nucl. Phys. B875 (2013) 552
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Complete three-loop SM beta functions (gauge, Yukawa, Higgs self-coupling) in MS-bar with full gauge couplings. For gamma5 (1212.6829): semi-naive approach - anticommute and gamma5^2 = 1; even traces in D dims; odd traces evaluated in 4d via tr(gamma^mu gamma^nu gamma^rho gamma^sigma gamma5) = -4i epsilon (eq. (9)); cyclicity dropped, starting point fixed by the FeynArts generic diagram structure with gamma5 moved to the rightmost position consistently; products of epsilon tensors from different traces contracted before renormalization with the 4d identity (eq. (10)), differing from the D-dim result by O(D-4), harmless for single-pole diagrams. Diagrams with higher poles and fermion-triangle subloops with external gauge fields (Figs. 1a, 1b) cancel only after summing all SM fermions, i.e. by gauge-anomaly cancellation, which needs N_c = 3.
  Loop order / theory: 3 / SM, general R_xi gauge
  Scheme: DREG, MS-bar, semi-naive gamma5 with fixed reading point from the generic diagram structure; LanHEP/FeynArts, FORM, MINCER, COLOR; IR rearrangement
  Symmetry argument: gauge-anomaly cancellation for the multi-pole gamma5-sensitive diagrams; O(D-4) ambiguity irrelevant for single-pole diagrams
  Proved:
      - explicit three-loop results; gauge-parameter independence; Z_chi = Z_h and Z_ffh = Z_ffchi verified at three loops
  Assumed:
      - that formal gamma5 manipulations in higher-pole diagrams are harmless because these diagrams cancel by anomaly cancellation; the authors note (citing Larin) that otherwise finite counterterms would be needed, and none are applied
      - result valid only for SU(3) colour (anomaly cancellation)
  Checked partially:
      - agreement with Chetyrkin-Zoller in the overlapping limit (a1 = a2 = a_b = a_tau = 0)
  Evidence:
      - 1212.6829: gamma5 section before eq. (9), eqs. (9)-(10), Figs. 1a-1c, eq. (13)
  Red flags:
      - the reading point is fixed by a software convention (generic FeynArts structure) rather than a derived rule
      - the 4d contraction of epsilon pairs before renormalization is exact only up to O(D-4)
  Cross-checks:
      - S5.08 (gauge betas), S5.09 (Yukawa/lambda in the gaugeless limit)
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv (1212.6829); abstracts/INSPIRE for 1210.6873 and 1303.4364 / high
  Notes: The 3-loop Yukawa gamma5-odd coefficients computed this way are the 'unambiguous' input that the Weyl-consistency papers (S5.17, S5.18) later use.
