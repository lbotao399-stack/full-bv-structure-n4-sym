[S4.07_HollikKrausRothRuppSiboldStoeckinger2002] W. Hollik, E. Kraus, M. Roth, C. Rupp, K. Sibold, D. Stoeckinger -- Renormalization of the minimal supersymmetric standard model (2002)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0204350   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B639 (2002) 3-65
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts (+ SUSY-restoring counterterms)
  Claim: All-order algebraic renormalization of the electroweak MSSM: ST identity with constant SUSY and translation ghosts, soft breaking via a chiral spurion combined with BRST doublets, spontaneous breaking via external doublets, local U(1)_Y Ward identity with a functional Gell-Mann-Nishijima relation, IR finiteness in BPHZL, and a complete set of on-shell normalization conditions. Breakings in a given order are local (quantum action principle) and can be removed by uniquely determined non-invariant counterterms, given that the only possible anomaly (supersymmetric Adler-Bardeen) cancels.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (algebraic) / electroweak MSSM (SU(2)xU(1), soft breaking, SSB), components, WZ gauge, on-shell scheme
  Scheme: no specific regulator for UV; quantum action principle (ref. [28] there) for locality of breakings; IR via Lowenstein's BPHZL with auxiliary mass, argued scheme independent; authors state that invariant regularizations of the MSSM probably do not exist (parity violation + SUSY); DRED not discussed in the parts read
  Symmetry argument: S(Gamma)=0 (eqs. (3.1)-(3.3)), constant ghosts epsilon^alpha, xi^mu in the BRS transformations (eq. (2.2)); soft terms coupled to spurion A-hat with shifted theta^2 component and BRST doublet (A, a^alpha = sqrt(2) epsilon^alpha A1, F_A = A2) (eqs. (2.11), (2.15), (2.17)); bilinear external-field terms included in Gamma_ext after eliminating auxiliary fields (Sec. 2.3); local U(1) Ward identity (eq. (3.17)) and Gell-Mann-Nishijima relation (eq. (3.16)); linearized ST operator nilpotent only on a subspace (eq. (3.12))
  Proved:
      - breakings of the ST identity of the form (3.33) can be absorbed by counterterms order by order, given the anomaly assumption
      - invariant counterterms: coupling/mass renormalizations, combined field-parameter renormalizations (eqs. (3.40)-(3.45)); g' not independent (local U(1) Ward identity (3.25)); finite field reparametrizations via demixing matrices (eqs. (3.46)-(3.48)); multiplicative renormalizability
      - IR finiteness of the vertex functional in BPHZL with IR dimensions (Tables 3.3-3.4; conditions (3.70)-(3.71))
  Assumed:
      - explicitly assumed (Sec. 3.3): the only possible anomaly is the supersymmetric Adler-Bardeen anomaly; its coefficient vanishes by the hypercharge assignment (all orders if the local U(1) Ward identity holds)
      - existence of a UV scheme satisfying the quantum action principle; which concrete scheme and which counterterms it needs are left to explicit calculations
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.2-2.8: soft breaking, external fields, parametrization, gauge fixing, free parameters (eqs. (2.26a-g), (2.90)-(2.95))
      - Sec. 3.1, 3.3, 3.4, 3.6: ST and Ward identities, anomaly assumption, invariant counterterms, IR finiteness; Sec. 4: on-shell normalization conditions, open question on M_A0 pole vs mass definition (Sec. 4.3.1)
  Red flags:
      - absence of SUSY anomalies is an assumption in this paper (cohomology not computed here; covered for semisimple groups by S4.03, S4.11 but the MSSM has an abelian factor, for which S4.11 lists extra candidate cohomology)
      - nilpotency of the linearized ST operator only on a subspace (abelian factor)
      - statement 'symmetry can be restored in the given order' is conditional; no explicit regulator
  Cross-checks:
      - explicit one-loop counterterms: S4.05, S4.06, S4.08; DRED 2-loop MSSM Higgs-mass checks in section 2 records (Hollik-Stoeckinger hep-ph/0509298, Stoeckinger hep-ph/0503129)

  Notes: This is the constant-ghost ST identity (= BV master equation with constant ghosts and spurion BRST doublets) for a realistic chiral SUSY model; its anomaly input for the abelian sector is assumed.
