[S5.19_DaviesHerrenPooleSteinhauserThomsen2020] J. Davies, F. Herren, C. Poole, M. Steinhauser, A.E. Thomsen -- Gauge coupling beta functions to four-loop order in the Standard Model (2020)
  arXiv: 1912.07624   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 124 (2020) 071803
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Four-loop MS-bar beta functions of all three SM gauge couplings (first/second generation Yukawas set to zero). In the direct computation gamma5 is anticommuting and traces with an odd number of gamma5 are set to zero; the gamma5-odd contributions (sensitive class: two fermion loops connected by one vector and two scalar bosons; a second class cancels by anomaly cancellation) are not computed directly but reconstructed from the WCC relations to 3-loop Yukawa coefficients (Poole-Thomsen). Ghost-gauge vertices are gamma5-unambiguous, which allows reconstructing the gamma5 parts of the gauge-boson wave-function constants. The authors state they see no practical prescription that works at fourth order.
  Loop order / theory: 4 / SM
  Scheme: DREG, MS-bar; anticommuting gamma5 with odd traces dropped; gamma5-odd pieces fixed via WCC
  Symmetry argument: Weyl consistency conditions; SM anomaly cancellation
  Proved:
      - explicit gamma5-even 4-loop results (eqs. (9)-(11) for alpha5 = alpha6 = 0); gauge-parameter cancellation; agreement with van Ritbergen et al./Czakon for gauge-fermion parts and with S5.11/S5.12 for g_s, y_t, lambda parts
      - 80 general coefficients fixed by matching, 28 more by 261 WCC, all consistent
  Assumed:
      - the gamma5-odd 4-loop contributions are fixed only by the WCC (not by direct computation); validity of the WCC in the scheme is assumed
  Checked partially:
      - 198 other coefficients of the general 4-loop gauge beta not determined in this paper
  Evidence:
      - Fig. 1 (gamma5-sensitive classes); eqs. (1)-(2), (9)-(11)
  Red flags:
      - the gamma5-odd part of a published 4-loop SM result rests on a consistency condition rather than on a regularized computation
      - lead corrected: arXiv id is 1912.07624, not 1910.07383
