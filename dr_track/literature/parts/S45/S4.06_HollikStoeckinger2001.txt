[S4.06_HollikStoeckinger2001] W. Hollik, D. Stoeckinger -- Regularization and supersymmetry-restoring counterterms in supersymmetric QCD (2001)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0103009   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C20 (2001) 105-119
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts (+ SUSY-restoring counterterms)
  Claim: One-loop symmetry-restoring counterterms of SQCD (with soft breaking) from the ST identity with constant SUSY and translation ghosts: loop corrections to the SUSY and gauge transformations and to trilinear interactions are fixed so that the ST identity and the symmetry algebra hold. In DREG non-symmetric counterterms are needed (SUSY transformations of gluon/gluino and quark/squark, gluino-quark-squark Yukawa-type vertex); in DRED none are needed for the cases considered.
  Loop order / theory: 1 / SQCD (SU(3), quarks/squarks, gluino), WZ gauge, soft breaking (neglected in the high-momentum limit used)
  Scheme: DREG (parameter theta_DReg = 1) vs DRED (theta_DReg = 0), MS-like; footnote 9: both as used are inconsistent at all orders, difference to a consistent (BM) scheme is local
  Symmetry argument: ST identity S(Gamma) = S0(Gamma) + S_soft(Gamma) = 0 (eq. (7)); epsilon a commuting Majorana spinor ghost, omega^mu translation ghost, both constant; WZ gauge fixing with epsilon-dependent compensating terms (eqs. (2)-(3)); derivative identities wrt c_a (eq. (11)), epsilon (eq. (12)) and epsilon, epsilon-bar (eqs. (13)-(15)) give gauge transformations, SUSY transformations and the algebra; non-symmetric counterterms Gamma_non-sym = sum delta_i O_i (eqs. (16)-(20)) fixed in the high-momentum limit and required to solve transformation and algebra identities simultaneously
  Proved:
      - DREG one-loop restoring counterterms: delta_{YG gluino eps} = -delta_{G eps y-bar gluino} = -(alpha_s C(A)/4pi)(1/3) (eqs. (25), (28)); delta_{y-tilde eps q} = -delta_{y q-tilde eps} = -(alpha_s C(F)/4pi)(1/2) (eq. (35)); gluino-quark-squark vertex delta = (alpha_s/4pi)[(2/3)C(A) - (1/2)C(F)] (eq. (42)), a Yukawa-type Lagrangian counterterm (eq. (43))
      - gauge vertices need no non-symmetric counterterms in either scheme: delta_qqG = delta_gluino-gluino-G = delta_GGG = 0 (eq. (46)); gluon-gluino-gluino counterterm vanishes (eqs. (51)-(52))
      - DRED: all identities considered hold at one loop without non-symmetric counterterms (eqs. (22), (26), (30), (33), (36), (44), (48))
  Assumed:
      - masses and soft terms neglected in the high-momentum limit; the q-tilde q-tilde G vertex is not constrained (it defines g)
  Checked partially:
      - not a complete check: conclusions restrict to 'the considered cases' and state that vertex functions not considered might need additional counterterms
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.1-2.4: BRS, gauge fixing, ST identity, special cases; Sec. 3.1-3.6: parametrization and results; App. C-D one-loop results
  Red flags:
      - the DRED one-loop statement is for a subset of identities (transformations, algebra, trilinear vertices)
      - authors note DRED (and DREG with their gamma5 use) cannot work at all orders; validity rests on differences being local
  Cross-checks:
      - consistent with S4.05_HollikKrausStoeckinger1999 (SQED) pattern: DREG needs a non-symmetric Yukawa-type shift, gauge vertices fine
      - a comparison of eq. (42) with the standard one-loop DRED/DREG translation of the gaugino Yukawa coupling (section 2 records) was not made here

  Notes: Non-abelian one-loop benchmark: DRED passes the ST identities considered; DREG needs restoring counterterms in SUSY transformations and in the Yukawa-type gaugino vertex.
