[S5.22_JackOsbornSteudtner2024] I. Jack, H. Osborn, T. Steudtner -- Explorations in scalar fermion theories: beta-functions, supersymmetry and fixed points (2024)
  arXiv: 2301.10903   Journal: JHEP 02 (2024) 038
  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: General scalar-fermion beta functions and anomalous dimensions to three loops (MS-bar form, no new diagrammatic computation), constrained diagram by diagram by N=1 (4d Wess-Zumino, four supercharges) and formal N=1/2 (two supercharges, 3d-type) SUSY relations and by a-function consistency conditions; applied to epsilon-expansion fixed points. States that DR breaks SUSY but for N=1 the anomalous contributions should be removable by a scheme change, and that problems with extending SUSY away from integer dimensions do not arise up to three loops; in general d various SUSY Ward identities fail because they rely on Fierz identities relating graphs with different numbers of fermion loops.
  Loop order / theory: 3 / general scalar-Yukawa theories; Wess-Zumino-type SUSY subcases
  Scheme: MS-bar (coefficients as in DREG, no explicit computation); two-component trace normalization; Majorana loops with (1 + (-1)^p) factors (eq. (2)); gamma5 not discussed
  Symmetry argument: SUSY constraints on individual diagram coefficients (Sec. 7, (7.1) N=1, (7.2) N=1/2) + Weyl/a-function consistency conditions
  Proved:
      - consistency of the three-loop coefficients with the SUSY and a-function constraints (no inconsistency found)
  Assumed:
      - that DREG's SUSY breaking for N=1 can be absorbed into a scheme change (stated, discussion deferred to the conclusion, not read)
      - that trace normalization alone suffices to three loops
  Checked partially:

  Evidence:

  Red flags:
