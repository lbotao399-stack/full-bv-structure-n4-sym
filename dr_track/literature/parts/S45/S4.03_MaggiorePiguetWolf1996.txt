[S4.03_MaggiorePiguetWolf1996] N. Maggiore, O. Piguet, S. Wolf -- Algebraic renormalization of N=1 supersymmetric gauge theories (1996)
  arXiv: hep-th/9507045   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B458 (1996) 403-429; Erratum ibid. B469 (1996) 513-514 (arXiv journal-ref field wrongly says Phys. Rev. D52 (1995) 6988)
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: Regulator-free algebraic renormalization of a general N=1 SUSY gauge theory with chiral matter in Wess-Zumino gauge. Gauge BRST, SUSY, translations and R-symmetry are merged into one generalized BRST operator with constant ghosts; the resulting Slavnov-Taylor (SST) identity can be restored order by order, the only possible obstruction being a supersymmetric extension of the non-abelian chiral gauge anomaly, whose coefficient vanishes to all orders if it vanishes at one loop. The physical counterterms are the gauge and Yukawa coupling renormalizations; the rest are field renormalizations. The SUSY generators extracted from the BRST operator close on the Wess-Zumino superalgebra on gauge-invariant operators.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (algebraic induction in hbar) / N=1 SYM + chiral matter in arbitrary representation, components, Wess-Zumino gauge, no auxiliary fields, Landau gauge fixing
  Scheme: no regulator; power counting + locality + quantum action principle (Sec. 3.2, eqs. (3.32)-(3.33)); the authors state that no regularization preserving both BRST and SUSY is known; dimensional schemes are not discussed
  Symmetry argument: generalized BRST operator s with constant SUSY ghost epsilon (commuting spinor), translation ghost xi^mu and R-ghost eta; s^2 = 0 only on-shell, so the classical action Sigma = S_SYM + S_gf + S_ext contains terms quadratic in the external fields (antifields), e.g. -(g^2/2)(epsilon lambda* - ...)^2 and 2 epsilon psi* epsilon-bar psi-bar* (eq. (2.7)); SST identity S(Sigma)=0 (eq. (2.8)); linearized operator B_Sigma nilpotent off-shell as a consequence of S(Sigma)=0 (eq. (2.9)). This is exactly the BV master equation (Sigma,Sigma)=0 of an open algebra with constant ghosts for global symmetries.
  Proved:
      - anomaly cohomology (ghost number 1, B_Sigma modulo d) in the power-counting-renormalizable sector is one-dimensional: the supersymmetric extension Delta_SAB of the non-abelian gauge anomaly, built from d^{ijk} (eq. (3.38)) and an invariant rank-4 tensor D^{ijmk} (eq. (3.39)); filtration computation in App. B, other candidates Delta_I..Delta_IV shown B_Sigma-trivial (eqs. (B.40)-(B.43)); no independent SUSY anomaly
      - quantum SST identity S(Gamma) = r Delta_SAB + O(hbar r) (eq. (3.31)); nonrenormalization: r = 0 to all orders if r vanishes at one loop (Sec. 5, by reduction to ordinary gauge theory at zero global ghosts)
      - stability: invariant counterterms = Z_g dSigma/dg + Yukawa renormalizations (eq. (3.25)) plus B_Sigma-trivial field renormalizations (eq. (3.26))
      - Callan-Symanzik equation with beta_g, beta_abc and anomalous dimensions (eq. (6.4), replaced by (E.2) in the erratum)
  Assumed:
      - existence of a scheme satisfying the quantum action principle for the constant-ghost SST identity (no specific regularization is constructed)
      - the one-loop value of the anomaly coefficient r is taken from the known gauge-anomaly computation, not computed
      - infrared: Landau gauge with massless fields; IR safety of the inductive argument is not addressed in the parts read
  Checked partially:
      (none)
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2: field content, generalized BRST s, Table 1 (quantum numbers), eqs. (2.1) on-shell closure, (2.6) s^2 = field equations, (2.7) quadratic external-field terms, (2.8) SST identity, (2.9) B_Sigma
      - Sec. 3: gauge condition (3.2), ghost equation (3.3)-(3.4), global ghost equations for xi and eta (3.5)-(3.6), rigid/translation/R Ward operators (3.16)-(3.18), counterterms (3.25)-(3.26), anomaly (3.31), (3.36)-(3.39)

  Red flags:
      - the theorem is conditional on a regularization with a quantum action principle; it says nothing about which concrete scheme (DREG, DRED, BMHV) realizes it or which finite counterterms that scheme needs
      - the erratum shows that WZ-gauge field renormalizations of multiplet partners are independent (no 'supersymmetric' relation among wave-function renormalizations) - relevant for any claim that SUSY relates anomalous dimensions of component fields
      - arXiv journal-ref field is incorrect (PRD 52 (1995) 6988); the correct reference is NPB 458 (1996) 403
