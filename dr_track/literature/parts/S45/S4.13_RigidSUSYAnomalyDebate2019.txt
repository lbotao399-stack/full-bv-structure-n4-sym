[S4.13_RigidSUSYAnomalyDebate2019] G. Katsianis, I. Papadimitriou, K. Skenderis, M. Taylor; I. Papadimitriou; S.M. Kuzenko, A. Schwimmer, S. Theisen; A. Bzowski, G. Festuccia, V. Prochazka -- Debate on rigid supersymmetry anomalies in N=1 theories with anomalous R-symmetry (Anomalous supersymmetry; Supersymmetry anomalies in N=1 conformal supergravity; ... new minimal supergravity; Comments on anomalies in supersymmetric theories; Consistency of supersymmetric 't Hooft anomalies) (2019)
  arXiv: 1902.06715; 1902.06717; 1904.00347; 1909.07084; 2011.09978 (also 2011.09506)   Journal: PRL (2019) [1902.06715]; JHEP 04 (2019) 040 [1902.06717]; JHEP 09 (2019) 039 [1904.00347]; J. Phys. A (2020), DOI 10.1088/1751-8121/ab64a8 [1909.07084]; JHEP 02 (2021) 225 [2011.09978]
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: Katsianis-Papadimitriou-Skenderis-Taylor and Papadimitriou: Wess-Zumino consistency conditions in the presence of background (conformal or new-minimal) supergravity imply that rigid Q-supersymmetry is anomalous whenever the R-symmetry (or a flavour symmetry) is anomalous; supported by a one-loop four-point function of two supercurrents and two R-currents in the free Wess-Zumino model. Kuzenko-Schwimmer-Theisen: superspace and WZ-gauge anomaly cohomologies are in one-to-one correspondence; apparent Q-SUSY violations come from an explicit superspace counterterm (a scheme choice) and are removable. Bzowski-Festuccia-Prochazka: give an explicit local counterterm removing the SUSY anomaly at the price of enlarging the current multiplet, and a manifestly SUSY Pauli-Villars treatment of supercurrent correlators.
  Loop order / theory: 1 (anomaly cocycles; free-field one-loop correlators) / 4d N=1 (super)conformal and R-multiplet theories coupled to background supergravity
  Scheme: background-field cohomology (Wess-Zumino consistency conditions); explicit one-loop in free WZ model; Pauli-Villars (BFP)
  Symmetry argument: consistency conditions of the combined Weyl/R/SUSY/diffeo transformations of background fields vs choice of local counterterms and current multiplets
  Proved:
      - (KPST/Papadimitriou) a solution of the WZ consistency conditions with nonzero SUSY variation tied to the R-anomaly in the chosen background multiplet
      - (KST) correspondence between superspace and WZ-gauge anomaly cohomologies; removability by counterterm (per abstract)
      - (BFP) explicit counterterm removing the SUSY anomaly with an enlarged current multiplet (per abstract)
  Assumed:
      - which background supergravity multiplet (and hence which current multiplet) the field theory must couple to
  Checked partially:

  Red flags:

      - the dispute shows that 'SUSY anomaly' depends on which current multiplet and which local counterterms are allowed - the same ambiguity a BV/QME treatment with background fields must fix
