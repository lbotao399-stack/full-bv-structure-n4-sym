[S4.04_Piguet1996] O. Piguet -- Supersymmetry, supercurrent, and scale invariance (lectures) (1996)
  arXiv: hep-th/9611003   Journal: lecture notes (conference record only)
  Section: 4 Algebraic renormalization of SUSY gauge theories with constant ghosts
  Claim: Review of the algebraic renormalization of N=1 SUSY gauge theories in superspace (supersymmetric Landau gauge, not WZ gauge): ST identity, anomaly analysis, supercurrent trace identities, and the algebraic all-order finiteness criterion: if the axial (R) anomaly coefficients vanish and the one-loop beta functions vanish with an isolated solution of the coupling reduction equations, then beta_g = 0 to all orders, only field-amplitude renormalizations remaining infinite.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (algebraic) / N=1 SYM + chiral matter in superspace
  Scheme: no regulator; quantum action principle, BPHZ-type induction, point-split Wick products; DRED not mentioned; IR problem of the massless dimensionless superfield C handled by WZ gauge or a SUSY-breaking regulator mass that is a gauge parameter (Secs. 5.1, 6.1)
  Symmetry argument: manifest (superspace) SUSY + ST identity for gauge BRST; scale breaking expressed through the supercurrent trace identity whose anomaly insertion is expanded in beta functions and anomalous dimensions
  Proved:
      - renormalized supercurrent trace identity with a chiral anomaly insertion S (eqs. (7.18), (7.20)); broken dilatation Ward identity = Callan-Symanzik equation (eqs. (7.21)-(7.22), (6.39))
      - finiteness criterion (Sec. 8, eqs. (8.15)-(8.20)): r = r_0a = 0 and one-loop beta = 0 with isolated reduced solution imply beta_g = 0 to all orders; relation (8.14) ties the anomaly coefficient r to beta functions and anomalous dimensions
      - superpotential nonrenormalization at zero momentum (Sec. 5.3, eqs. (5.22)-(5.24))
  Assumed:
      - absence of SUSY anomalies taken from the literature (Sec. 6.2 remark, refs. [48]-[50] there)
      - the one-loop-to-all-orders vanishing of the supersymmetric Adler-Bardeen gauge anomaly relies on a supersymmetric Bardeen-type theorem that the text says has not been explicitly checked (Sec. 6.3, Remark 1)
      - a scheme satisfying the quantum action principle with manifest SUSY
