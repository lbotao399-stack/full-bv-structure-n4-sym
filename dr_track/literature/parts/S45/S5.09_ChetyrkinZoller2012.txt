  Section: 5 Standard Model >= 3 loops and gamma5
  Claim: Three-loop beta functions of y_t and lambda (and anomalous dimensions) in the SM in the gaugeless limit g1 = g2 = 0 with only y_t non-zero among Yukawas (1205.2892); full lambda beta at three loops in 1303.2890. gamma5 is naive on open lines; in closed loops a non-naive contribution needs at least four free indices/momenta on the minimal subgraph containing the loop; the only problematic class (Fig. 1(f)) gives only a 1/epsilon pole and is treated with 't Hooft-Veltman-like rules (anticommute in 4d, gamma5^2 = 1, one gamma5 per line replaced by the epsilon form, two epsilons into metrics), with an O(epsilon) error that does not affect the pole part.
