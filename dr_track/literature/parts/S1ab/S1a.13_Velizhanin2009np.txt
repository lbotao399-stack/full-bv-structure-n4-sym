[S1a.13_Velizhanin2009np] V. N. Velizhanin -- The non-planar contribution to the four-loop universal anomalous dimension in N=4 Supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory (2009)
  arXiv: 0902.4646   Journal: JETP Lett. 89 (2009) 593 (journal from secondary listing; not verified on the abstract page)
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: First non-planar (colour-subleading) four-loop anomalous dimension of the Konishi operator: full result eq. (2), gamma_K = 12g^2 - 48g^4 + 336g^6 + (-2496 + 576 zeta(3) - 1440 (1 + 12/N_c^2) zeta(5)) g^8 with g^2 = g_YM^2 N_c/(4 pi)^2, i.e. the non-planar Konishi part is only -17280 zeta(5) g^8/N_c^2 (enters through the quartic Casimir d44 = N_c^2(N_c^2+36)/24, eq. (3)); eq. (4) gives the non-planar part of the (pseudo)scalar FIELD anomalous dimension, (-42 - 177 zeta(3) + 555 zeta(5)) g^8/N_c^2; eq. (5) conjectures the non-planar twist-two universal term -640 S_1^2(j-2)(1 + 12/N_c^2) zeta(5) from moments j = 2, 4, 6.
