[S1a.20_ChakrabortyMoch2026] M. Chakraborty, S.-O. Moch -- Dimensional reduction is supersymmetric at three loops (2026)
  arXiv: 2603.02892   Journal: preprint (PUBDB-2026-00811)
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: The long-standing claim that DR breaks SUSY at three loops (mismatch of Yukawa and ghost-gluon beta functions in N=2 SYM; Avdeev-Tarasov 1982, Velizhanin 2008) stems from the treatment of Levi-Civita terms in flavour and spinor traces, not from DR itself; with these terms kept, beta_ffs = beta_ccg for N=1, 2, 4 at three loops. A non-zero flavour Levi-Civita trace requires six flavour matrices, which for N=4 first occurs at five loops; four-loop results are consistent. New four-loop N=1 Yukawa beta function.
  Loop order / theory: 3 (N=1,2,4); 4 (N=1 gauge and Yukawa, N=4; QCD check) / N=1, N=2, N=4 SYM from a generalized Lagrangian with n_s scalars and n_f fermions
  Scheme: DR realised as analytic continuation n_s -> n_s + 2 eps of a Lagrangian with Euclidean flavour Clifford algebra Z^I Zbar^J + Z^J Zbar^I = 2 delta^{IJ} (eq. (4)); Levi-Civita (chiral) parts regularised by a 'simplified regularisation' with integer-dimensional indices and an O(eps) remainder (eq. (10)); spinor traces with Wick-rotated Pauli matrices and a four-index epsilon tensor (eq. (11)); MS-bar, IRR for vertices, general covariant gauge; Qgraf, FORM, FORCER.
  Symmetry argument: a posteriori equality of beta functions from different vertices; gauge-parameter independence; the 'five-loop' statement for N=4 is a diagram-structure argument (first appearance of a non-vanishing flavour epsilon-trace).
  Proved:
    - the N=2 three-loop mismatch is removed once the Levi-Civita contribution of the fermion-scalar Yukawa triangle is kept (eq. (15)); beta_ffs = beta_ccg for N=1, 2, 4 at three loops
    - Table 1 of the earlier literature reproduced by dropping Levi-Civita terms (diagnosis)
  Assumed:
    - the 'simplified regularisation' of Levi-Civita terms is consistent (it is a prescription, not a theorem)
    - that no Levi-Civita ambiguity enters N=4 below five loops (argued from flavour-trace structure)
  Checked partially:
    - four-loop QCD (N=0) reproduced; four-loop N=1 beta_ccg agrees with literature; four-loop N=4 matches Velizhanin (S1a.11); N=2 four loops deferred
    - Weyl consistency conditions proposed for future checks
  Evidence:
    - eq. (4): flavour Clifford algebra
    - eq. (9): Tr[Z^I Zbar^J] for n_s = 2 contains i eps^{IJ}
    - eq. (10)-(11): regularisation of Levi-Civita terms and Pauli traces
    - eq. (13): Levi-Civita terms survive only with chiral parts in both traces
    - eq. (15): Yukawa-triangle Levi-Civita contribution cancelling the N=2 error
    - Fig. 3: five-loop N=4 topology with six flavour matrices
    - Table 1: earlier beta_ccg vs beta_ffs values
  Red flags:
    - the claimed SUSY preservation depends on a specific prescription for Levi-Civita tensors in d dimensions - structurally the same issue as gamma5 in the SM (KKS reading point / Larin / BMHV)
    - five-loop N=4 statement is a prediction of where ambiguity can enter, not a demonstration of consistency or of failure
    - evanescent couplings are dismissed by citation ('SYM has no evanescent interaction terms')
  Cross-checks:
    - resolves the N=2 discrepancy of Velizhanin 0809.2509 (S1a.19) and Avdeev-Tarasov 1982
    - consistent with four-loop N=4 beta = 0 (S1a.11)
