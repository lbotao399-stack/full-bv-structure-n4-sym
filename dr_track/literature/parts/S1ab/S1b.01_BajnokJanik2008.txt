[S1b.01_BajnokJanik2008] Z. Bajnok, R. A. Janik -- Four-loop perturbative Konishi from strings and finite size effects for multiparticle states (2008)
  arXiv: 0807.0399   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B (DOI 10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2008.08.020; volume/page not verified)
  Section: 1b integrability-based high-loop spectrum
  Claim: Four-loop planar Konishi anomalous dimension from the string sigma model: ABA plus leading finite-size (wrapping) correction from Luscher-type formulas generalized to multi-magnon states at weak coupling, summed over all mirror bound states; agrees with the gauge-theory perturbative results.
  Loop order / theory: 4 (planar, leading wrapping) / planar N=4 SYM (Konishi, two-magnon sl(2)/su(2) state)
  Scheme: No regularization: not a DR (or any Feynman-graph) computation. Planar spectrum obtained from the integrable AdS5 x S5 worldsheet S-matrix (asymptotic Bethe ansatz with BES/BHL dressing phase) plus finite-size corrections. Leading wrapping from a multi-particle Luscher F-term formula (derived by analogy with solvable relativistic integrable QFTs).
  Symmetry argument: psu(2,2|4)-based worldsheet S-matrix; no gauge-theory regulator involved
  Proved: -

    - identification of gauge-theory anomalous dimensions with string energies (AdS/CFT) in the planar limit
    - multi-particle generalization of Luscher formulas (validated on relativistic integrable models, not derived for AdS5 x S5)
