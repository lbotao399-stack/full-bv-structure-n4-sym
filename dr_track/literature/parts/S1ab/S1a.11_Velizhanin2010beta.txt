[S1a.11_Velizhanin2010beta] V. N. Velizhanin -- Vanishing of the four-loop charge renormalization function in N=4 SYM theory (2010)
  arXiv: 1008.2198   Journal: Phys. Lett. B696 (2011) 560
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Four-loop renormalization constants of N=4 SYM in dimensional reduction: field anomalous dimensions of gluon, ghost, scalar, fermion and the ghost-ghost-gluon and fermion-fermion-scalar vertex anomalous dimensions (eqs. (5)-(10)); the beta function extracted from either vertex vanishes at four loops (eq. (11)), i.e. gauge and Yukawa couplings renormalize identically through four loops.
  Loop order / theory: 4 (full colour as far as SU(N) Casimirs; planarity not restricted in the summary) / N=4 SYM, component fields
  Scheme: dimensional reduction: Dirac algebra in four dimensions, 4 - 2 eps gauge-field components and 3 + eps scalars plus 3 + eps pseudoscalars (6 + 2 eps real scalar components including the eps-scalars) so that the field count matches SUSY; IRR to massless propagator integrals; Laporta reduction (BAMBA); FORM, COLOR, DIANA/QGRAF; about 726,000 four-loop diagrams (Table 1).
  Symmetry argument: SUSY of DR tested a posteriori: beta extracted from the gauge vertex (ghost-ghost-gluon) and from the Yukawa vertex (fermion-fermion-scalar) must coincide (and vanish) if SUSY holds; eq. (4): beta_jjk = g^2 (2 gamma_jjk - 2 gamma_j - gamma_k).
  Proved:
    - beta = 0 at four loops from both vertices in this DR implementation (eq. (11)) - an explicit four-loop check of the gauge/Yukawa coupling equality required by SUSY
  Assumed:
    - absence of evanescent eps-scalar coupling splitting (the eps-scalar couplings are set equal to the gauge coupling)
  Checked partially:
    - only two vertex functions; no check of quartic scalar vertex, of composite operators or of full SUSY Ward identities
    - gauge-parameter independence of the printed results not discussed
  Evidence:
    - eq. (3): coupling renormalization from vertex and field constants
    - eq. (4): beta from a vertex
    - eqs. (5)-(10): four-loop gamma functions
    - eq. (11): beta^(4-loop) = 0
    - Table 1: diagram counts (about 726,000)
    - Introduction: cites Siegel PLB 94 (1980) 37 for internal contradictions; Avdeev PLB 117 (1982) 317 for gauge and Yukawa beta functions that 'do not coincide starting from three loops' in N=1 and N=2 SYM; Avdeev-Chochia-Vladimirov PLB 105 (1981) 272 and Avdeev-Vladimirov NPB 219 (1983) 262 for the estimate that DR should work for N=4 up to five loops for propagator-type diagrams (Table 1 of Avdeev 1982)
  Red flags:
    - the author's own summary of the earlier literature says DRED fails for the gauge/Yukawa equality at three loops in N=1/N=2 SYM (Avdeev 1982) - this should be cross-checked against later N=1 three-loop DRED results (Jack-Jones et al., covered in S1e/S2)
    - the five-loop 'safe' estimate for N=4 is a counting estimate of where Levi-Civita/Fierz ambiguities can first enter propagator-type graphs, not a proof
    - a vanishing beta function is necessary, not sufficient, for SUSY of the scheme
  Cross-checks:
    - consistent with the classic three-loop results: Avdeev-Tarasov-Vladimirov 1980 (S1a.15, components DR) and Grisaru-Rocek-Siegel 1980 (S1a.16, superspace)
