  arXiv: 1209.0791   Journal: JHEP 1211 (2012) 002
  Section: 1b integrability-based high-loop spectrum
  Claim: Six- and seven-loop planar Konishi from Luscher corrections with the worldsheet S-matrix; integrand derived analytically, integral and infinite sum evaluated by high-precision numerics and asymptotic expansion, integer coefficients of zeta values fitted; seven loops is the limit of the approach because double wrapping enters at eight loops.

Single-wrapping Luscher corrections; analytic result reconstructed by fitting integer coefficients of zeta values to numerics.
