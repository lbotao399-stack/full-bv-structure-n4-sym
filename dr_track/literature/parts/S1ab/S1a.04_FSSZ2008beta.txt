[S1a.04_FSSZ2008beta] F. Fiamberti, A. Santambrogio, C. Sieg, D. Zanon -- Single impurity operators at critical wrapping order in the beta-deformed N=4 SYM (2008)
  arXiv: 0811.4594   Journal: JHEP 0908 (2009) 034
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Anomalous dimensions of the one-impurity operators O_{1,L} = tr(phi Z^{L-1}) in the planar superconformal (real beta, h hbar = g^2) beta-deformed theory, including wrapping at the critical order K = L, obtained explicitly up to L = 11 loops (v2). Wrapping plus range-(L+1) subtraction give purely transcendental terms; the highest is zeta(2L-3) (Table 1).
  Loop order / theory: L loops for length L, explicit for L <= 11 (this is the highest loop order reached by a DR-type Feynman-graph spectral computation in this family) / planar beta-deformed N=4 SYM (N=1 superconformal, real beta, h hbar = g^2), SU(N), SU(2) sector, single-impurity operators
  Scheme: N=1 superspace, Fermi-Feynman gauge, D-algebra in 4d, integrals in D = 4 - 2 eps (dimensional regularization of the scalar integrals), gamma from the 1/eps pole of log Z (eq. (1.3)). The surviving L-loop integrals P_L and I_L^(j) are free of subdivergences, so only simple poles enter (eq. (2.15)); they are evaluated with D-dimensional IBP triangle rules generalized to numerators (Appendix A.1, eqs. (A.1)-(A.5)) and GPXT for L up to 11 (Appendix A.3).
  Symmetry argument: Manifest N=1 superspace; cancellations among wrapping supergraphs derived from the D-algebra lemma; asymptotic (non-wrapping) part taken from an all-loop formula for long single-impurity states derived earlier in superspace (eq. (1.5)); conformality of the deformed theory taken from the literature (real beta, h hbar = g^2).
  Proved:
    - for this operator class only a few supergraph classes survive (Sec. 2, Fig. 10), reducing the L-loop computation to P_L and I_L^(j) (eqs. (2.12)-(2.15))
  Assumed:
    - asymptotic all-loop dimension gamma_as = -1 + sqrt(1 + 16 lambda sin^2(pi beta)) for long single-impurity states (eq. (1.5)), taken from Mauri-Penati-Santambrogio-Zanon
    - planar superconformal invariance of the real-beta deformed theory with h hbar = g^2 to all orders (eq. (1.2), cited)
    - supersymmetric DR (4d D-algebra + D-dim integrals) is adequate; no discussion of regulator-induced SUSY breaking (here harmless at the level of simple poles of primitive integrals)
  Checked partially:
    - closed-form guesses for I_L^(1), I_L^(2) (eqs. (3.2)-(3.3)) verified up to L = 11
    - Luscher/twisted-S-matrix integrability computations reproduce critical-wrapping single-impurity results (reported in the integrability review 1012.3998; individual comparison not opened here)
  Evidence:
    - eq. (1.1)-(1.2): deformed superpotential and conformality condition h hbar = g^2
    - eq. (1.3): gamma = lim eps g d/dg log Z (dimensional regularization)
    - eq. (1.5) and (2.6): asymptotic all-loop result gamma_L^as = alpha_L lambda^L sin^{2L}(pi beta)
    - eqs. (2.7)-(2.8): range-(L+1) subtraction diagram S_L
    - eq. (2.15): delta gamma_L in terms of simple poles of P_L and I_L^(j)
    - eq. (3.1): P_L pole ~ (2/L) binom(2L-3, L-1) zeta(2L-3)
    - Sec. 4 and Table 1: all I_L^(j) computed up to L = 11; transcendental content up to zeta(19) at L = 11
    - arXiv comment: v2 completed results up to 11 loops
  Red flags:
    - gauge group SU(N) used in eq. (2.2); later work (Fokken-Sieg-Wilhelm, S1a.08/S1a.09) showed that in deformed theories the U(N) vs SU(N) choice and induced double-trace couplings matter for short operators (L = 2), though L >= 3 single-trace results in the planar limit are not affected in the same way
    - hybrid: non-wrapping part from an all-loop asymptotic formula, not recomputed
    - the 11-loop statement concerns only the critical-wrapping correction of a one-magnon state, built from subdivergence-free integrals; it is not a test of DR beyond simple poles
  Cross-checks:
    - integrability (twisted S-matrix Luscher formula) reproductions reported in the integrability review arXiv:1012.3998; Bajnok-el Deeb 1010.5606 extend to six loops for L=... beyond critical order (not opened)
