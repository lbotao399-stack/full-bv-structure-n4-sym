[S1a.05_FSS2009] F. Fiamberti, A. Santambrogio, C. Sieg -- Five-loop anomalous dimension at critical wrapping order in N=4 SYM (2009)

  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Five-loop anomalous dimension of a length-five operator in the planar SU(2) sector (critical wrapping order) computed with N=1 superspace perturbation theory; confirms the twist-three formula conjectured in 0901.4864 (Beccaria-Forini-Lukowski-Zieme) and is reproduced from the Y-system.

  Symmetry argument: manifest N=1 superspace; integrability input for non-wrapping part
