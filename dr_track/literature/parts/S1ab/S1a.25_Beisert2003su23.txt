[S1a.25_Beisert2003su23] N. Beisert -- The su(2|3) dynamic spin chain (2003)
  arXiv: hep-th/0310252   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B682 (2004) 487
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: In the su(2|3) subsector the requirement that the perturbatively deformed su(2|3) symmetry algebra be respected fixes the planar energy shifts uniquely through three loops (confirming the BKS conjecture); the higher-loop Hamiltonian changes the number of sites ('dynamic' chain) and still appears integrable.
  Loop order / theory: 3 (planar) / N=4 SYM su(2|3) subsector
  Scheme: no regularization: algebraic construction of the deformed generators (interacting supercharges at O(g)) constrained by closure of the symmetry algebra; remaining freedom fixed by additional physical input (per EJS: a two-parameter freedom at three loops fixed by BMN scaling)
  Symmetry argument: Assumes that the quantum (renormalized) theory realizes su(2|3) on composite operators through coupling-dependent generators whose algebra closes order by order; this replaces a scheme-level derivation of the dilatation operator.
  Proved:
      - Within the algebraic ansatz: the deformed generators are constructed order by order by solving the closure constraints (eq. 1.5), and the planar energy shifts are fixed through three loops (abstract; Secs. 3-5).
  Assumed:
      - Identification of the deformed su(2|3) representation with the action on renormalized N=4 operators, H = D (eq. 2.30), up to similarity transformations (eq. 3.18) and a coupling redefinition (footnote 3); argued from field-theory structure and BMN scaling, not derived from a regulated theory.
      - Parity (eq. 2.12), planarity, Feynman-diagram leg counting (eq. 3.7) and iterated-one-loop structure of maximal-leg diagrams (Sec. 5).
      - BMN scaling (eqs. 6.3-6.4) for the coupling redefinition; integrability is not assumed (stated in Sec. 1).
  Checked partially:
      - agreement with three-loop field theory (S1a.23, S1a.07)
  Evidence:
      - Eq. (3.1): J(g) = sum_k g^k J_k, J_0 free representation; eq. (3.7): leg count n + m = k + 2 (planar interaction symbols).
      - Eq. (3.32): O(g) length-changing supercharges (Q_1)^a_alpha = c_9 eps_{alpha beta} eps^{abc} {beta; bc} (one fermion -> two bosons) and (S_1)^alpha_a = c_10 eps_{abc} eps^{alpha beta} {bc; beta}.
      - Eqs. (3.29)-(3.30): [Q_0, H_2] = 0 fixes the form of the one-loop Hamiltonian H_2 (c_1 = c_2 = c_3, c_4 = c_5 = -c_6); eq. (3.35): O(g^3) closure gives c_1 = -c_4.
      - Eq. (3.36) and text after (3.37): closure of {Q, S} at O(g^2) relates normalizations, c_9 c_10 = (1/2) c_1 with c_1 = alpha_1^2; eqs. (3.41)-(3.42) classical anticommutator and second-order relation.
      - Sec. 5, eqs. (5.3)-(5.4): maximal-leg diagrams = iterated one-loop diagrams used to set sigma_3 = sigma_4 = 0, zeta_1 = xi_1 = 0; eqs. (4.13)-(4.15): BPS energy integrality gives eps = 6 alpha_1^4; eqs. (6.3)-(6.4): BMN scaling fixes the coupling redefinition.
  Red flags:
      - the existence of a perturbatively deformed, closing su(2|3) algebra acting on renormalized operators is exactly what a regularization (DR/DRED) must deliver and is not derived here
  Cross-checks:
      - S1a.23 (EJS three-loop SSDR test), S1a.07
