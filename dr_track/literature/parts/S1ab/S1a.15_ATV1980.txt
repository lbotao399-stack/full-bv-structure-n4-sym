[S1a.15_ATV1980] L. V. Avdeev, O. V. Tarasov, A. A. Vladimirov (detailed version: O. V. Tarasov, A. A. Vladimirov, JINR E2-80-483) -- Vanishing of the three-loop charge renormalization function in a supersymmetric gauge theory (1980)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv); detailed JINR preprint E2-80-483 posted as 1301.5645   Journal: Phys. Lett. B96 (1980) 94
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Three-loop beta function of the SU(4)-supersymmetric (N=4) gauge theory vanishes, as at one and two loops; the standard (non-supersymmetric) dimensional regularization would instead give a non-zero three-loop value (8 N^3 h^4 in their normalization).
  Loop order / theory: 3 / N=4 SYM, component fields
  Scheme: 'supersymmetric dimensional regularization' in components (preprint 1301.5645): Dirac algebra written in 4 - 2 eps with defining relations kept in four-dimensional form; numbers of scalars and pseudoscalars continued to 3 + eps each so that bosonic and fermionic components match (counting argument: 8 spinor components = (2 - 2 eps) vector + (3 + eps) + (3 + eps)); internal alpha/beta matrices adjusted accordingly; beta from ghost-ghost-gluon vertex and propagators, beta = h[2 gamma~_1 - gamma_3 - 2 gamma~_3].
  Symmetry argument: a posteriori: at two loops two methods of computing beta agree only in the supersymmetric scheme, the standard scheme gives a different value by one method and is judged non-invariant; authors state there is no rigorous proof of validity of the regularization and call for a symmetry-based argument.
  Proved:
    - beta = 0 at three loops in this scheme (preprint eq. (57))
  Assumed:
    - the component supersymmetric dimensional regularization preserves SUSY at three loops
  Checked partially:
    - two-loop comparison of two ways of computing beta (scheme consistency check)
  Evidence:
    - 1301.5645 Appendix: Dirac algebra in 4 - 2 eps with 4d defining relations
    - 1301.5645: delta^{rr} = 3 + eps for scalars and pseudoscalars
    - 1301.5645 eq. (57): beta(h) = 0 at three loops; standard regularization gives 8 N^3 h^4
  Red flags:
    - explicitly no proof that the regularization is supersymmetric (stated by the authors)
    - only vertex-based beta; Yukawa-vertex check came later (Velizhanin, S1a.19)
  Cross-checks:
    - Grisaru-Rocek-Siegel PRL 45 (1980) 1063 and Caswell-Zanon NPB 182 (1981) 125 in superspace (S1a.16)
    - Velizhanin 0809.2509 (S1a.19) recomputed three loops in arbitrary gauge
