[S1a.08_FSW2013nonconf] J. Fokken, C. Sieg, M. Wilhelm -- Non-conformality of gamma_i-deformed N=4 SYM theory (2013)
  arXiv: 1308.4420   Journal: J. Phys. A47 (2014) 455401
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: The non-supersymmetric three-parameter gamma_i-deformation of N=4 SYM is not conformal: a double-trace coupling is induced and runs, also in the 't Hooft limit; adding tree-level multi-trace couplings with minimal consistency requirements does not restore conformality. Suggested link to a divergence of integrability-based two-loop finite-size corrections for tr(Z^2).
  Loop order / theory: one-loop running of induced double-trace coupling (from abstract; loop order of the explicit computation not verified) / gamma_i-deformed N=4 SYM (non-supersymmetric), planar limit
  Scheme: Feynman diagrams with dimensional regularization (details not verified from full text)
  Symmetry argument: Shows that conformal invariance assumed in integrability-based treatments of deformed theories fails because of double-trace running; a DR-based computation is used to detect symmetry breaking rather than to assume protection.
  Proved:
      - running of an induced double-trace coupling in the gamma_i-deformed theory, surviving the planar limit (per abstract)
  Assumed: -
  Checked partially:
      - proposed (not completed in this paper) test of the link to the divergent two-loop finite-size integrability result
  Evidence:
      - Abstract (paraphrased): running double-trace coupling breaks conformal invariance even at large N; multi-trace completion does not cure it
  Red flags:
      - shows that 'planar = single-trace only' reasoning can miss double-trace couplings that contribute to planar two-point functions of short operators (L=2)
      - integrability (Y-system/TBA) predictions for deformed theories rest on conformality assumed, not derived
  Cross-checks:
      - continued in Fokken-Sieg-Wilhelm 1405.6712 (S1a.10) and Sieg-Wilhelm 1602.05817 (fishnet-type limit also non-conformal; complex double-trace fixed points at one loop)
  Access / confidence: abstract only / medium
  Notes: Sieg-Wilhelm, PLB 756 (2016) 118, arXiv:1602.05817 (abstract read): the integrable non-unitary limit of the gamma_i-deformation is incomplete and not conformal even in the planar limit; completing it with double-trace couplings gives conformal one-loop fixed points only for complex couplings. Parent check (abstract, arXiv v3 31 Mar 2015): non-conformality holds even in the t Hooft limit; tree-level multi-trace couplings satisfying minimal consistency requirements do not cure it; abstract suggests a link to the two-loop finite-size divergence seen in integrability for a two-site chiral operator.
