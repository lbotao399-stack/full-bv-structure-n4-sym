[S1b.02_BHJL2009] Z. Bajnok, A. Hegedus, R. A. Janik, T. Lukowski -- Five loop Konishi from AdS/CFT (2009)
  arXiv: 0906.4062   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B827 (2010) 426
  Section: 1b integrability-based high-loop spectrum
  Claim: Five-loop planar Konishi anomalous dimension from generalized Luscher formulas (multi-magnon, weak coupling); new features: modification of ABA quantization and sensitivity to infinitely many dressing-phase coefficients; also the five-loop wrapping for the L=2 single-impurity state of the beta-deformed theory.
  Loop order / theory: 5 (planar) / planar N=4 SYM; beta-deformed L=2 single impurity
  Scheme: No regularization: not a DR (or any Feynman-graph) computation. Planar spectrum obtained from the integrable AdS5 x S5 worldsheet S-matrix (asymptotic Bethe ansatz with BES/BHL dressing phase) plus finite-size corrections.
  Symmetry argument: integrability; internal consistency (simple transcendentality structure, cancellation of mu-term poles)
  Proved: -
  Assumed:
    - all-loop integrability of planar N=4 SYM / AdS5 x S5 superstring
    - asymptotic Bethe ansatz with the BES/BHL dressing phase (exact worldsheet S-matrix)
    - identification of gauge-theory anomalous dimensions with string energies (AdS/CFT) in the planar limit
    - generalized Luscher formula including modified ABA quantization at next order
  Checked partially:
    - internal: transcendentality structure and cancellation of mu-term poles
    - later confirmed by mirror TBA (S1b.03, S1b.04) and by the correlator-based five-loop computation (Eden-Heslop-Korchemsky-Smirnov-Sokatchev 1202.5733, covered in S1c)
  Evidence:
    - Abstract (paraphrased)
  Red flags:
    - the beta-deformed L=2 single-impurity prediction concerns exactly the short operator for which double-trace running/prewrapping issues were later found in deformed theories (S1a.08-S1a.10) - the L=2 deformed prediction is not a clean CFT datum without addressing those
