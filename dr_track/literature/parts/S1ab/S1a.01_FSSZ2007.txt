[S1a.01_FSSZ2007] F. Fiamberti, A. Santambrogio, C. Sieg, D. Zanon -- Wrapping at four loops in N=4 SYM (2007)
  arXiv: 0712.3522   Journal: Phys. Lett. B666 (2008) 100
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: First field-theory computation of the planar four-loop anomalous dimension of the length-4 Konishi descendant tr(phi[Z,phi]Z) in the SU(2) sector, the first order at which wrapping interactions contribute; wrapping produces zeta(5). Letter version; details in 0806.2095 (S1a.02).
  Loop order / theory: 4 (planar) / planar N=4 SYM, SU(N), SU(2) scalar sector, length L=4 operator
  Scheme: N=1 superspace Feynman supergraphs; D-algebra completed in 4 dimensions, remaining momentum integrals regulated dimensionally (1/eps poles), MS subtraction; non-wrapping part not computed from graphs but taken from the asymptotic four-loop dilatation operator (see S1a.02).
  Symmetry argument: Manifest N=1 superspace (only N=1 of the N=4 SUSY is manifest); SUSY of the regulator is implicit in the use of supersymmetric dimensional regularization/reduction after D-algebra; no explicit SUSY Ward identity check.
  Proved: -
  Assumed:
    - supersymmetric DR (D-algebra in 4d, integrals in D dims) preserves the N=4 structure at four loops for the operator renormalization constant
    - the asymptotic four-loop SU(2) dilatation operator (integrability-based, dressing coefficient beta=4 zeta(3)) is correct for the range-1..4 part
  Checked partially:
    - final number later agreed with the Luscher/integrability prediction (S1b.01) after corrections; see notes
  Evidence:
    - Abstract: wrapping first contributes at four loops for this operator and gives zeta(5)
    - arXiv comments field: v2 corrected the sign of one diagram, which changed the zeta(3) coefficient of the final result
  Red flags:
    - v1 result was wrong (sign of one diagram); corrected value only in v2/v3
    - the non-wrapping part is imported from an integrability-derived asymptotic dilatation operator rather than computed, so this is a hybrid field-theory/integrability result
    - only N=1 of the N=4 supersymmetry is manifest
  Cross-checks:
    - agrees after correction with Bajnok-Janik 0807.0399 (S1b.01) and with Velizhanin 0808.3832 (S1a.06)
    - disagrees with component computation of Keeler-Mann 0801.1661 (S1a.03)
  Access / confidence: abstract only (details verified in companion paper S1a.02, full text via PDF) / high
  Notes: Final number: gamma_4 = -2496 + 576 zeta(3) - 1440 zeta(5) in units lambda^4, lambda = g^2 N/(16 pi^2); see S1a.02 eq. (5.3)-(5.4).
