[S1b.07_LeurentVolin2013] S. Leurent, D. Volin -- Multiple zeta functions and double wrapping in planar N=4 SYM (2013)
  arXiv: 1302.1135   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B875 (2013) 757
  Section: 1b integrability-based high-loop spectrum
  Claim: Planar Konishi anomalous dimension up to eight loops (leading double-wrapping order) from the FiNLIE solution of the Y-system; first appearance of the irreducible Euler-Zagier sum zeta(1,2,8); spectral quantities expressible via multiple Hurwitz zeta functions to all orders; conjecture that only Euler-Zagier sums appear; resummation of leading transcendental terms in Bessel functions.
  Loop order / theory: 8 (planar) / planar N=4 SYM
  Scheme: No regularization: not a DR (or any Feynman-graph) computation. Planar spectrum obtained from the integrable AdS5 x S5 worldsheet S-matrix (asymptotic Bethe ansatz with BES/BHL dressing phase) plus finite-size corrections. FiNLIE weak-coupling expansion.
  Symmetry argument: integrability; absence-of-poles condition equivalent to exact Bethe equations
  Proved:
    - multiple Hurwitz zeta representation of spectral-dependent Y-system quantities (within the Y-system framework)
  Assumed:
    - all-loop integrability of planar N=4 SYM / AdS5 x S5 superstring
    - asymptotic Bethe ansatz with the BES/BHL dressing phase (exact worldsheet S-matrix)
    - identification of gauge-theory anomalous dimensions with string energies (AdS/CFT) in the planar limit
    - Y-system/FiNLIE describes the exact planar spectrum including double wrapping
