[S1a.22_EJSS2005] B. Eden, C. Jarczak, E. Sokatchev, Y. S. Stanev -- Operator mixing in N=4 SYM: the Konishi anomaly revisited (2005)
  arXiv: hep-th/0501077   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B722 (2005) 119
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: In N=4 SYM the Konishi anomaly is the superconformal descendant K_10 of the Konishi multiplet; it is a mixture of a bare trilinear operator B and a bilinear 'quantum' operator F whose renormalized combination must carry the Konishi anomalous dimension; superconformal covariance truncates the anomaly equation to K_10 = a(g)[B]_R (eq. (3)); the protected combination is O_10 = -1/2 (F - 4B); the coefficient one would call the anomaly becomes divergent beyond one loop, so 'one-loop exactness of the Konishi anomaly' is not a meaningful question in a superconformal scheme. Explicit two-loop SSDR computation.
  Loop order / theory: 2 (explicit), structure of Z_O through g^6 (eq. (65)) / N=4 SYM in N=1 superspace
  Scheme: N=1 superspace Feynman rules with supersymmetric dimensional reduction (SSDR), coupling g-hat = g mu^eps (Sects. 1, 3.1); minimal subtraction (Sect. 3.2, eq. (45))
  Symmetry argument: superconformal symmetry of the renormalized operators fixes which operator can appear on the right-hand side of the anomaly equation; the anomalous dimension is scheme independent in a conformal theory, while mixing coefficients (finite part of Z_O) are not (Sect. 3.3)
  Proved:
    - explicit two-loop SSDR computation: the protected combination O_10 is not renormalized; the Konishi mixing coefficient becomes divergent (Sect. 3.3)
    - one-loop anomaly coefficient N/(32 pi^2) reproduced as first term of Z_O
  Assumed:
    - SSDR preserves the superconformal structure needed for the truncation argument
    - conformal covariance of renormalized operators (used to exclude F from the right-hand side)
  Checked partially:
    - protected two-point functions (O_20' and descendants) checked to O(g^4) (Sects. 2.2, 4.1)
  Evidence:
    - Introduction / eq. (3): truncated anomaly equation
    - eq. (1), (8): N=1 form of Dbar^2 K with one-loop coefficient
    - Sect. 3.2, eq. (45): MS renormalization in SSDR
    - Sect. 3.3: scheme (in)dependence statements; eq. (65): structure of Z_O to g^6
    - footnote 13: corrections to classical equations may be scheme dependent
  Red flags:
    - explicitly states that mixing coefficients and whether classical equations of motion receive quantum corrections are scheme dependent - a DR-type scheme does not by itself define the 'anomaly' beyond one loop
    - superconformal covariance of the renormalized operators in SSDR is assumed, not derived from a Ward identity of the regulated theory
  Cross-checks:
    - consistent with BKRS hep-th/0104016 (S1a.21) at one loop
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv (first 100k characters, through Sect. 3.4) / medium
  Notes: Direct precedent for a one/two-loop N=4 composite-operator test of how a DR-type scheme realises superconformal symmetry on operators (Konishi descendant mixing).
