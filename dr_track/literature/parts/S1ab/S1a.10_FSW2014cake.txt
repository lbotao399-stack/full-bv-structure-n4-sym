[S1a.10_FSW2014cake] J. Fokken, C. Sieg, M. Wilhelm -- A piece of cake: the ground-state energies in gamma_i-deformed N=4 SYM theory at leading wrapping order (2014)
  arXiv: 1405.6712   Journal: JHEP 1409 (2014) 078
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Leading-wrapping-order anomalous dimensions of tr(Z^L) in the gamma_i-deformed theory from Feynman diagrams: for L >= 3 proportional to the maximally transcendental 'cake' integral and equal to the integrability (Luscher/TBA/Y-system) prediction; for L = 2 integrability diverges while the diagrammatic result is finite, rational and scheme dependent because an induced quartic double-trace coupling has non-zero beta function - conformal invariance broken even in the 't Hooft limit.

  Symmetry argument: explicit; shows that the conformality assumption underlying the integrability prediction fails for L = 2
  Proved:
    - L >= 3: field theory agrees with integrability at leading wrapping order (per abstract)
    - L = 2: result scheme dependent due to running double-trace coupling (per abstract)
