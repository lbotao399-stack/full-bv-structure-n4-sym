[S1a.17_KLV2003] A. V. Kotikov, L. N. Lipatov, V. N. Velizhanin -- Anomalous dimensions of Wilson operators in N=4 SYM theory (2003)
  arXiv: hep-ph/0301021   Journal: Phys. Lett. B557 (2003) 114
  Section: 1a N=4 spectrum via Feynman/supergraphs
  Claim: Two-loop anomalous-dimension matrix of twist-two operators (unpolarized and polarized) in N=4 SYM, triangularized by the leading-order similarity transformation; eigenvalues are a universal function with shifted arguments (eq. (18) with Q-hat of eq. (8)).
  Loop order / theory: 2 / N=4 SYM, twist-two operators (quark/gluino, gluon, scalar)
  Scheme: Diagrams computed in MS-bar (conventional DREG) with DIANA/QGRAF, MINCER, FORM, following the QCD calculations of Hamberg-van Neerven and Mertig-van Neerven; polarized case with a 'reading point' gamma5 prescription plus a Larin-type finite renormalization restoring anticommutativity; then converted to DR-bar (Siegel) using the one-loop finite-part differences (Antoniadis-Floratos), plus an additional coupling redefinition alpha_s -> alpha_s(1 - alpha_s N_c/(12 pi)) to reach the universal form.
  Symmetry argument: MS-bar is stated to violate SUSY; DR-bar is described as preserving SUSY at least at two loops (cited, not proved); superconformal invariance is said to break at two loops, requiring a slightly modified operator basis for diagonalization.
  Proved:
    - two-loop matrix triangular in the LO similarity basis; eigenvalue relations eqs. (19)-(27) (stated to hold to all orders after replacing the two-loop function by the exact gamma)
  Assumed:
    - DR-bar preserves SUSY at two loops
    - MS-bar -> DR-bar conversion fully captured by one-loop finite differences (Antoniadis-Floratos argument)
    - reading-point gamma5 + Larin-type finite renormalization is consistent for the polarized operators
  Checked partially:
    - agreement with earlier Born-level and partial results
  Evidence:
    - eq. (8): definition of Q-hat(j) in harmonic sums
    - eq. (13)-(17): eigenvalues Q-hat(j-2), Q-hat(j), Q-hat(j+2) (unpolarized), Q-hat(j-1), Q-hat(j+1) (polarized)
    - eq. (18): gamma(j) = -(alpha_s N_c/pi) S_1(j-2) + (alpha_s N_c/4 pi)^2 Q-hat(j-2)
    - text: MS-bar to DR-bar conversion via one-loop finite parts; coupling redefinition removing the S_1 term
  Red flags:
    - the N=4 result is obtained in a non-supersymmetric scheme (MS-bar) and converted; this conversion assumes that the scheme difference is a finite renormalization of coupling/operators determined at one loop
    - the polarized case relies on a reading-point gamma5 prescription (KKS-type) - a gamma5 subtlety inside an N=4 computation
    - statement that superconformal invariance breaks down at two loops in the computation means the conformal (similarity) basis has to be corrected - an instance of DR-induced conformal-symmetry breaking (beta-like term from d != 4)
  Cross-checks:
    - used as the two-loop check for KLOV (S1a.18) and for the ABA predictions
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv (summarised) / medium
  Notes: Relevant for the gamma5 synthesis: even inside an N=4 two-loop computation a reading-point prescription was used for the polarized operators.
