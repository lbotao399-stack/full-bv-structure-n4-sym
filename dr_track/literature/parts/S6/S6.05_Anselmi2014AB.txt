[S6.05_Anselmi2014AB] D. Anselmi -- Adler-Bardeen theorem and manifest anomaly cancellation to all orders in gauge theories (2014)
  arXiv: 1402.6453   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C74 (2014) 3083
  Section: 6a BV master equation / quantum action principle in DR and regularization-free BV
  Claim: In renormalizable, perturbatively unitary gauge theories with matter there is a subtraction scheme in which gauge anomalies cancel manifestly to all orders when they vanish at one loop (the scheme with no finite local subtractions from two loops on). The proof uses the BV formalism with a combined dimensional + higher-derivative regularization and no renormalization-group argument, hence covers conformal and finite theories.
  Loop order / theory: all orders / non-abelian gauge theory with left-handed fermions (worked model); right-handed fermions, scalars, photons in Sec. 8 (not read)
  Scheme: 'DHD' regularization: spacetime R^4 x R^(-eps), hatted evanescent components (Sec. 2); right-handed partners psi_R coupled through a matrix varsigma_IJ to regulate chiral propagators in all directions (eqs. 2.5-2.7); gauge-invariant higher-derivative factors (D^2/Lambda^2)^8 on F^2 and (D^2/Lambda^2)^3 on fermion kinetic terms, modified gauge fixing (eqs. 3.1-3.3); limits: eps -> 0 at fixed Lambda, then Lambda -> infinity (Sec. 3.1); gamma5 = i gamma0 gamma1 gamma2 gamma3 with continued Dirac algebra, no named gamma5 scheme.
  Symmetry argument: BV antibracket, master equation with gauge fermion (eqs. 2.2-2.4); anomaly functional A = (Gamma,Gamma) = <(S,S)>_S (eq. 2.11); regularized violation (S_r0,S_r0) = O(eps) (eq. 2.9) and DHD version (eqs. 3.5, 3.10, 4.2); one-loop anomaly split into non-evanescent, divergent-evanescent and evanescent parts (eqs. 4.3-4.4, 5.1); higher-derivative theory has only one-loop divergences (eqs. 3.11, 4.1).
  Proved:
      - Counterterms confined to one loop in the HD-regularized theory; the subtracted anomaly functional is convergent (Secs. 3-5).
  Assumed:
      - No analytically evanescent coefficients in the regularized action (Sec. 2.2).
      - No products of two or more fermion bilinears in the relevant functionals, to avoid Fierz ambiguities (Secs. 2.2, 2.6).
      - Perturbative unitarity and renormalizability.
  Checked partially:
      - The higher-loop cancellation step (Sec. 6) and the Lambda -> infinity limit (Sec. 7) were not read here.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2: eqs. (2.2)-(2.19) BV setup, chiral regularization, evanescent terms S_ev.
      - Sec. 3: eqs. (3.1)-(3.11) DHD regularization and limit prescription.
      - Secs. 4-5: eqs. (4.1)-(5.1) one-loop anomaly decomposition.
  Red flags:
      - The Fierz restriction excludes exactly the structures on which supersymmetry invariance depends (Yukawa and gaugino Fierz identities); supersymmetry is not treated.
      - Existence statement: the scheme is characterized implicitly (no finite subtractions beyond one loop), not constructed explicitly for practical computations.
  Cross-checks:
      - S6.06_Anselmi2014chiralDR
      - S3.08_BIMS2020 and later BMHV works (explicit finite restoring counterterms instead of an existence proof)
