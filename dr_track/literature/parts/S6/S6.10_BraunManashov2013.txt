[S6.10_BraunManashov2013] V. M. Braun, A. N. Manashov -- Evolution equations beyond one loop from conformal symmetry (2013)
  arXiv: 1306.5644   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C73 (2013) 2544
  Section: 6b conformal symmetry in d != 4 (breaking and restoration in DR)
  Claim: At the critical coupling of a theory in non-integer dimension (O(n) phi^4 in d=4-2eps, su(n) phi^3 in d=6-2eps) the theory is taken to be exactly conformal; the collinear conformal generators acquire coupling-dependent corrections, and commutation relations with the evolution kernel determine the non-conformal part of the l-loop kernel from the (l-1)-loop correction to the special conformal generator; MS kernels are eps-independent, so results carry over to integer dimension.
  Loop order / theory: method to all orders; explicit checks at two/three loops in scalar theories / O(n) phi^4 (d=4-2eps), su(n) phi^3 (d=6-2eps); gauge theories announced for later work
  Scheme: Dimensional regularization at the Wilson-Fisher critical point u*, MS(-bar); kernels eps-independent in minimal subtraction (Sec. 2.2).
  Symmetry argument: Exact conformal invariance of the critical theory in d dimensions; special conformal Ward identity derived from the finite renormalized generating functional.
  Proved:
      - Generator corrections: S_- uncorrected (eqs. 44-46); Delta S_0 = -eps + (1/2) H(u*) (eqs. 39, 51); Delta S_+ = (z1+z2)(-eps + (1/2) u* H^(1)) + O(eps^2) at one loop (eqs. 39, 52-62, 80).
      - Consistency relations [S_+^(0), H(u*)] = [H(u*), Delta S_+] and [Delta S_+, S_-] = 2 Delta S_0 (eqs. 88-91).
      - A naive all-order guess for S_+ (eq. 81) fails; explicit extra term (1/4) u*^2 [H^(2), z1+z2] (eq. 83).
  Assumed:
      - The critical theory in non-integer d is exactly conformally invariant (Secs. 1, 2.3).
      - eps-singular terms of the Ward identity cancel because the renormalized generating functional is finite (Sec. 3).
      - Transfer to integer d via eps-independence of MS kernels (Secs. 1, 2.2).
  Checked partially:
      - Only scalar theories in this paper.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2.1 eqs. (2)-(4), (12)-(13): models and critical points.
      - Sec. 2.3 eq. (39); Sec. 3 eqs. (44)-(62), (80)-(83); Sec. 4 eqs. (88)-(91); eq. (103) eps-u* relation.
  Red flags:
      - Non-integer-d theories are non-unitary, so 'scale implies conformal' is not available; conformality is assumed (motivates S6.11).
      - Does not apply directly to N=4 SYM: with beta_4 = 0 the d-dimensional beta function -2eps a has only the trivial zero, so no interacting critical point exists in d = 4-2eps.
