[S6.17_LPS1988] C. Lucchesi, O. Piguet, K. Sibold -- Vanishing beta functions in N=1 supersymmetric gauge theories (1988)
  arXiv: none (pre-arXiv)   Journal: Helv. Phys. Acta 61 (1988) 321
  Section: 6c all-order protection results with manifestly supersymmetric or algebraic schemes; BPS protection checks in DR
  Claim: For N=1 SYM with simple gauge group: if the gauge anomaly vanishes, the one-loop gauge beta function and one-loop matter anomalous dimensions vanish, and the one-loop Yukawa solution lambda = rho g is isolated and non-degenerate, then the gauge and Yukawa beta functions vanish to all orders (one-coupling theory via reduction of couplings).
  Loop order / theory: all orders / N=1 SYM with chiral matter, simple gauge group
  Scheme: Algebraic renormalization with renormalized Ward identities for R, SUSY, BRS (Slavnov) and chiral symmetries (eqs. 3.5-3.10 of hep-ph/9604216), assuming a supersymmetric superspace renormalization scheme; not DR.
  Symmetry argument: Supercurrent anomaly expansion relates the R-axial anomaly coefficient r to beta functions and anomalous dimensions (eq. 3.31 of hep-ph/9604216); non-renormalization of the axial anomaly coefficients (eq. 3.32) then forces beta_g = 0 to all orders (eq. 3.46).
  Proved:
      - All-order vanishing of beta functions under one-loop hypotheses, within the stated scheme (as summarized in hep-ph/9604216 Sec. 3).
  Assumed:
      - A renormalization scheme preserving supersymmetry (superspace scheme of the cited ref. [13]).
      - Non-renormalization of the R-axial/chiral anomaly coefficients.
  Checked partially: -
  Evidence:
      - Secondary: Lucchesi-Zoupanos hep-ph/9604216, Sec. 3, criterion after eq. (3.32); eqs. (3.5)-(3.10), (3.14)-(3.17), (3.31)-(3.37), (3.43)-(3.46).
  Red flags:
      - Statement is about vanishing beta functions in a specific scheme; scheme requirements stated explicitly for phases (hep-ph/9604216 Sec. 3).
      - Relies on a SUSY-preserving scheme that is not DRED.
