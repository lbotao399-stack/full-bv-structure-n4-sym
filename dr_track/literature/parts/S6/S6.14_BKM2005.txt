[S6.14_BKM2005] A. V. Belitsky, G. P. Korchemsky, D. Mueller -- Integrability of two-loop dilatation operator in gauge theories (2005)
  arXiv: hep-th/0509121   Journal: Nucl. Phys. B735 (2006) 17
  Section: 6b conformal symmetry in d != 4 (breaking and restoration in DR)
  Claim: Computes one- and two-loop dilatation operators in the non-compact SL(2) sector (three quark or gaugino fields of equal helicity with light-cone derivatives) of QCD and of N=1,2,4 SYM; two-loop spectra are integrable in planar SYM, and with extended supersymmetry the N-dependence factorizes into a c-number; conformal symmetry is violated from two loops on.
  Loop order / theory: 2 / QCD (SU(3)) and N=1,2,4 SYM (SU(Nc)), SL(2) sector
  Scheme: Computed in parallel in DREG (MS-bar) and DRED (DR-bar) (Sec. 4.1), light-like axial gauge with principal-value prescription (eq. 4.3); conversion eqs. (5.48)-(5.53).
  Symmetry argument: In d = 4-2eps the coupling runs with beta_eps(g^2) = -2eps + beta(g^2) (eq. 4.6), so conformal symmetry is broken for eps != 0 even for N=4; the breaking enters the dilatation operator from two loops; at L=3, V_DR^(1) = V^(1) - (1/6) V^(0) - (3/4)(4-N) 1l (eq. 5.54), the c-number term vanishing for N=4.
  Proved:
      - Two-loop dilatation operators in both schemes; DR-bar and MS-bar operators differ by a c-number/normalization at two loops (Sec. 5.4.1, eqs. 5.48-5.54).
  Assumed:
      - DRED preserves supersymmetry at two loops (stated as the reason DRED is mandatory, not verified; Sec. 4.1).
      - Principal-value prescription for light-like gauge poles (eq. 4.3).
  Checked partially:
      - Sec. 6.1.3 (conformal Ward identity discussion for N=4) only partly read.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2 eqs. (2.3)-(2.9): field content, beta = 0 for N=4.
      - Sec. 4.1, eq. (4.6).
      - Sec. 5.4.1, eqs. (5.48)-(5.54).
  Red flags:
      - SUSY preservation of DRED is an assumption here.
      - Light-cone gauge with PV prescription is a non-covariant ingredient.
  Cross-checks:
      - S6.13_BelitskyMueller1998
      - S1a.25_Beisert2003su23 (closure assumptions in the compact sectors)
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv (up to part of Sec. 6.1.3) / medium
  Notes: For N=4 the only conformal-breaking parameter in d dimensions is the explicit -2eps (no critical point), exactly the term a d-dimensional BV conformal Ward identity has to carry.
