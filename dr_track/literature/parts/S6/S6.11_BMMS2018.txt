[S6.11_BMMS2018] V. M. Braun, A. N. Manashov, S. Moch, M. Strohmaier -- Conformal symmetry of QCD in d dimensions (2018)
  arXiv: 1810.04993   Journal: journal not verified (DESY 18-175)
  Section: 6b conformal symmetry in d != 4 (breaking and restoration in DR)
  Claim: Gives a perturbative argument that QCD in d = 4-2eps at the critical coupling a* is conformal in the gauge-invariant sector: the inhomogeneous term in the conformal variation of a renormalized gauge-invariant operator is itself gauge-invariant up to BRST-exact and EOM terms that drop out of correlators.
  Loop order / theory: all orders (perturbative argument) / QCD in d = 4-2eps at the critical point
  Scheme: MS in d = 4-2eps (eq. 5); multiplicative renormalizability assumed (Sec. 2); Landau gauge used for scale invariance of all Green functions (Sec. 5).
  Symmetry argument: Scale and conformal Ward identities: the bulk term carries beta(a) and vanishes at a* (eq. 24); products of the Lagrangian with renormalized operators decompose into gauge-invariant, BRST and EOM pieces (eqs. 26-28, 35; Joglekar-Lee); renormalized transformation law delta_K O_j = (delta_jj' K_Dj + 2 x^mu gamma_jj') O_j' + O^mu_j (eq. 44).
  Proved:
      - Vanishing of bulk contributions at beta(a*)=0 (eq. 24); dilatation identity F_j = - sum gamma_jj' O_j' (eq. 42); gauge invariance of the inhomogeneous term up to BRST/EOM terms (Secs. 4-5).
  Assumed:
      - Multiplicative renormalizability; existence of a*; diagonalizability of the anomalous-dimension matrix (fn. 2 warns it can fail with fermions).
      - Joglekar-Lee structure of gauge-invariant operator mixing.
  Checked partially: -
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 1 eqs. (1)-(3); Sec. 2 eqs. (4), (9)-(14), (21)-(23); Sec. 3 eqs. (16)-(25); Sec. 4 eqs. (26)-(35); Sec. 5 eqs. (38)-(44).
  Red flags:
      - Gauge fixing and ghosts break conformal symmetry already in d=4; only gauge-invariant correlators transform covariantly.
      - The authors flag that F Ftilde = dK shows inhomogeneous terms that are not gauge invariant can arise.
      - Evanescent operators, the d-dimensional energy-momentum tensor and gamma5 are not discussed.
