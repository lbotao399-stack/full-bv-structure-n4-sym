[S6.16_BPS2015] I. L. Buchbinder, N. G. Pletnev, K. V. Stepanyantz -- Manifestly N=2 supersymmetric regularization for N=2 supersymmetric field theories (2015)
  arXiv: 1509.08055   Journal: Phys. Lett. B (2015) (DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2015.10.071; volume/page not verified)
  Section: 6c all-order protection results with manifestly supersymmetric or algebraic schemes; BPS protection checks in DR
  Claim: Constructs a higher covariant derivative regularization in N=2 harmonic superspace plus N=2 Pauli-Villars superfields, preserving N=2 supersymmetry and background gauge invariance at every step; proves the N=2 non-renormalization theorem: divergences only at one loop, hypermultiplets not renormalized, beta function one-loop exact; N=4 SYM finite to all orders.
  Loop order / theory: all orders / N=2 gauge theories with hypermultiplets; N=4 SYM as special case
  Scheme: N=2 higher-derivative term S_Lambda (eq. 13), background field split and gauge fixing (eqs. 14-25), Nielsen-Kallosh sector (eqs. 29-31), commuting analytic PV superfields with masses proportional to Lambda (eqs. 34-35); generating functional eq. (36).
  Symmetry argument: Manifest N=2 supersymmetry and background gauge invariance of the regularized theory; power counting omega_Lambda = omega - 2(P-V) (eq. 33); analyticity gives gamma_phi = 0 (eq. 40); beta one-loop exact (eqs. 45-46); second proof via NSVZ (eqs. 37-38).
  Proved:
      - Finiteness beyond one loop and one-loop exactness of beta in the HD+PV scheme (Secs. 3-5).
  Assumed:
      - N_D > 0 beyond one loop and manifest N=2 SUSY of the regularized effective action taken from ref. [5] (fn. 6).
      - NSVZ relation in the second proof supported by evidence, not derived (Sec. 4).
      - One-loop computation done at m0 = 0, xi = 1 (Sec. 5).
  Checked partially: -
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 3 eqs. (13)-(36); Sec. 3.3 eqs. (32)-(33); Sec. 4 eqs. (37)-(38), (40); Sec. 5 eqs. (45)-(46); Introduction: earlier proofs (Howe-Stelle-Townsend, Howe-Stelle-West) implicitly assumed an N=2 supersymmetric gauge-invariant regularization.
