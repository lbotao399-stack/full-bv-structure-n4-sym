[S1d.11_BCCDDN2015] Z. Bern, C. Cheung, H.-H. Chi, S. Davies, L.J. Dixon, J. Nohle -- Evanescent effects can alter ultraviolet divergences in quantum gravity without physical consequences (2015)
  arXiv: 1507.06118   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 115 (2015) 211301
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: Evanescent operators (Gauss-Bonnet) and evanescent fields (3-form, dual in 4d) change the coefficient of the two-loop R^3 (Goroff-Sagnotti) divergence of pure gravity and its change under p-form duality, while the renormalization-scale dependence and physical renormalized amplitudes are unaffected and can respect duality.
  Loop order / theory: 2 loops / pure gravity with/without p-form fields (non-supersymmetric)
  Scheme: Dimensional regularization implied (evanescent operators vanish only in exactly 4d); not named in the abstract.
  Symmetry argument: Duality between theories equivalent in 4d; one-loop trace anomaly changes under duality (Duff-van Nieuwenhuizen).
  Proved:
      - Explicit two-loop identical-helicity four-graviton computation shows the divergence coefficient depends on evanescent content.
  Assumed: (none)
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence:
      - Abstract.
  Red flags:
      - Generic lesson for DR-based claims: pole coefficients can depend on evanescent operators/fields; only scale dependence (log coefficients) is robust.
  Cross-checks:
      - S1d.10_BPR2017.
  Access / confidence: abstract only / medium
  Notes: Non-SUSY; included as a methodological red flag for DR pole coefficients.
