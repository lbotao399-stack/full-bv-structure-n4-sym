[S1d.03_BCJK2007] Z. Bern, J.J.M. Carrasco, H. Johansson, D.A. Kosower -- Maximally supersymmetric planar Yang-Mills amplitudes at five loops (2007)
  arXiv: 0705.1864   Journal: Phys. Rev. D76 (2007) 125020 (from memory)
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: The planar five-loop four-point integrand of N=4 SYM is written as 34 nonvanishing pseudo-conformal integrals out of a 59-element basis (193 terms in eq. 19 counting permutations), with coefficients 0, +-1 fixed mainly by four-dimensional maximal cuts, plus partial D-dimensional cut checks.
  Loop order / theory: 5 loops (planar integrand only; no integrals evaluated) / planar N=4 SYM four-gluon amplitude
  Scheme: FDH (relative of Siegel's dimensional reduction) chosen to preserve SUSY (Sec. II); DR for IR divergences, eps=(4-D)/2.
  Symmetry argument: SUSY: FDH assumed SUSY-preserving. Dual conformal invariance assumed (only pseudo-conformal integrals appear; no proof, Sec. III); D-dimensional validity holds only if the pseudo-conformal basis with dimension-independent coefficients is correct.
  Proved:
      - Coefficients of the pseudo-conformal ansatz fixed by 4d maximal and near-maximal generalized cuts (Secs. VI-VII).
  Assumed:
      - Only pseudo-conformal integrals contribute (Sec. III, explicitly unproven).
      - Dimension-independence of coefficients (needed for D-dim validity).
      - Equal contribution from different maximal-cut solutions (checked in many but not all cases).
  Checked partially:
      - D-dimensional cut checks described as partial (Sec. I).
      - UV bound D < 6/L + 4 (eq. 22; D < 26/5 at L=5) argued for the included integrals, not computed.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. II: FDH chosen to preserve SUSY.
      - Sec. III: assumption of pseudo-conformal integrals.
      - Sec. V.1: basis of 59 pseudo-conformal integrals; eq. (19): result.
      - Eq. (22): UV finiteness bound D < 6/L + 4.
  Red flags:
      - Integrand fixed largely by 4d cuts; mu-term (extra-dimensional) contributions not treated explicitly; D-dim validity conditional on the dual-conformal ansatz.
      - Some signs (I31, I32, I34) unexplained.
  Cross-checks:
      - Later reproduced from the correlator side (S1c.02_EHKS2012, five-loop integrand); UV in D=26/5 computed later (S1d.05_BCDJR2012).
