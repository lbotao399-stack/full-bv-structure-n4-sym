[S1e.04_JJP1998] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, A. Pickering -- The connection between DRED and NSVZ (1998)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9805482   Journal: Phys. Lett. B435 (1998) 61 (from memory)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED
  Claim: The remaining coefficient rho_1 of the three-loop DRED->NSVZ coupling redefinition is fixed (rho_1 = 16/3) through a three-loop computation of the epsilon-scalar mass beta-function in the softly broken theory, completing the NSVZ/DRED connection through O(alpha^4) and thereby determining the four-loop DRED gauge beta-function; agreement with an earlier Pade-approximant prediction. Also introduces DRED' (soft scalar mass beta independent of the epsilon-scalar mass).
  Loop order / theory: redefinition to three loops; DRED gauge beta to four loops (inferred) / general N=1 gauge theories, including soft SUSY breaking
  Scheme: DRED / DRED' with epsilon-scalars acquiring radiative masses (missed by naive spurion formalism).
  Symmetry argument: Relations between soft-breaking beta-functions and the coupling redefinition; consistency of tensor structures T2, T3 used as checks.
  Proved:
      - rho_1 = 16/3 from the epsilon-scalar mass beta (three-loop computation in the broken theory).
  Assumed:
      - Four-loop DRED gauge beta follows from NSVZ + redefinition (i.e. assumes NSVZ holds in some scheme and DRED is a SUSY scheme related to it).
  Checked partially:
      - Consistency of the redefinition with soft scalar mass beta for alpha m^2 Y^2 Y*^2 C(R) terms; T2 and T3 structures give same rho_1.
  Evidence:
      - Main text: determination of rho_1 = 16/3; DRED' definition; Pade comparison.
  Red flags:
      - The four-loop DRED gauge beta is not computed diagrammatically here; it is inferred assuming the NSVZ-DRED redefinition picture (direct check later: S1e.05_HJKMS2006, S1e.06_JJKM2007).
      - Epsilon-scalar masses are a genuine DRED artefact that must be tracked (DRED vs DRED').
  Cross-checks:
      - Completes S1e.03_JJN1996c.
