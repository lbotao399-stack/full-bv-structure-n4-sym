[S1d.02_BCDKS2006] Z. Bern, M. Czakon, L.J. Dixon, D.A. Kosower, V.A. Smirnov -- The four-loop planar amplitude and cusp anomalous dimension in maximally supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory (2006)
  arXiv: hep-th/0610248   Journal: Phys. Rev. D75 (2007) 085010 (from memory)
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: The planar four-loop four-gluon amplitude integrand (eight integrals, eq. 39) is determined with D-dimensional generalized unitarity; its IR poles evaluated in DR give the four-loop cusp anomalous dimension numerically, ruling out the Eden-Staudacher conjecture and supporting the BES dressing phase (beta = zeta3).
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops (planar) / planar N=4 SYM, four-gluon amplitude
  Scheme: DR d=4-2eps with the four-dimensional helicity (FDH) scheme (state counting kept at 4d values, a variation of dimensional reduction) to preserve SUSY; loop momenta in D dims; tree amplitudes in cuts taken in D dims with intermediate states summed via the 10d N=1 SYM multiplet (Sec. III).
  Symmetry argument: FDH/DRED-like regulator assumed SUSY-preserving; D-dimensional cuts computed using 10d SYM states (which dimensionally reduce to the 16-supercharge theory); conformal (dual conformal) integrals used as a guide, not a requirement (Sec. V.2).
  Proved:
      - Generalized D-dimensional cuts (i)-(vi) of Fig. 3 reproduced by the eight-integral integrand (Sec. III, V.1, Table 1); cuts (v),(vi) show no additional integrals.
  Assumed:
      - No-triangle property assumed (Sec. V.1); SUSY Ward identity step lacks a general proof.
      - No theorem that four-dimensional cuts suffice beyond one loop; therefore D-dim cuts are used, but full D-dimensional completeness for all cuts is not demonstrated in the visible text.
      - Only conformal integrals appear: used as guide, no proof (Sec. V.2).
  Checked partially:
      - 1/eps^8..1/eps^4 analytic, 1/eps^3 and 1/eps^2 numerical (MB) at four kinematic points.
      - Exponentiation (eq. 32) checked only for divergent terms; finite part left to future work.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. III: FDH scheme to preserve SUSY; D-dim unitarity with 10d N=1 multiplet state sum.
      - Sec. V.1: generalized cuts (v),(vi); no-triangle hypothesis assumed.
      - Eq. (39): four-loop planar integrand; eq. (7): modified ES form with beta.
      - Intro: numerical f^(4) consistent with sign-flipped zeta3^2 term (BES).
  Red flags:
      - The SUSY of FDH beyond one/two loops is an assumption; here it enters the IR-pole coefficients used to fix f^(4).
      - Numerical precision limited for the 1/eps^3, 1/eps^2 coefficients.
      - Mu-term absence not explicitly proved; D-dim computation used because no proof that (-2eps) parts are unimportant.
  Cross-checks:
      - Agrees with BES (hep-th/0610251) dressing-phase prediction; disagrees with Eden-Staudacher.
