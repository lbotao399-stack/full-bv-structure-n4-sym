[S1c.06_BHT2016] J.L. Bourjaily, P. Heslop, V.-V. Tran -- Amplitudes and correlators to ten loops using simple, graphical bootstraps (2016)
  arXiv: 1609.00007   Journal: JHEP 11 (2016) 125 (from memory; not shown on the fetched page)
  Section: 1c correlator integrands
  Claim: Three graph-level rules acting on S_{4+l}-symmetric planar f-graphs (square rule = generalized rung rule from the four-point light-like/amplitude limit; triangle rule from the Euclidean short-distance/OPE limit; pentagon rule from the five-point light-like limit) fix the planar four-point correlator, hence the four-point amplitude (and five-point parity-even/odd pieces), through ten loops; nine loops is fixed by the triangle rule applied at ten loops.
  Loop order / theory: 10 loops (planar); 900,145 f-graphs at ten loops; partial statistics at 11 loops / planar N=4 SYM; four-point stress-tensor correlator; four- and five-point amplitudes by duality
  Scheme: No regulator: rational 4d integrand in dual coordinates; parity-odd parts via a six-dimensional (Klein quadric) epsilon tensor; integrated correlator stated finite, light-like limit divergent; no DR discussed in the text.
  Symmetry argument: Hidden S_{4+l} symmetry + dual/ordinary 4d conformal weights + planarity; the rules follow from required behaviour in OPE and light-like limits and from the amplitude/correlator duality.
  Proved:
      - Triangle rule derived from the OPE/short-distance argument (Sec. 3.2, eqs. 25-33); square rule derived from the four-point light-like limit (Sec. 3.1, eqs. 21-24); pentagon rule derived from the five-point light-like limit / correlator-amplitude duality (Sec. 3.3, eqs. 34-39).
      - Combined rules fix all 900,145 ten-loop coefficients uniquely (Sec. 4.1, Table 3).
  Assumed:
      - The planar f-graph ansatz with hidden symmetry (from S1c.01_EHKS2011, S1c.02_EHKS2012).
      - Correlator/amplitude duality (input for the square and pentagon rules).
      - Authors: rules are necessary conditions, not obviously sufficient to all orders (Sec. 5); correctness supported by consistency with known results through eight loops.
  Checked partially:
      - Square + triangle alone leave 3 unknowns at nine loops and 1,570 at ten loops (Table 3); square + pentagon leave 64 at nine loops (Table 4).
      - Eleven loops: square rule fixes about 95% of 22,097,035 coefficients (Sec. 4); not completed.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 3.1, eqs. (21)-(24): square rule; eq. (9): four-point light-like limit.
      - Sec. 3.2, eqs. (25)-(33): triangle rule from Euclidean coincidence limit.
      - Sec. 3.3, eqs. (34)-(39): pentagon rule and its proof via eq. (17) five-point light-like limit.
      - Sec. 4, Tables 3-4: counts of undetermined coefficients per rule combination.
      - Sec. 5: rules are necessary, not obviously sufficient; push to 11-12 loops anticipated.
  Red flags:
      - All high-loop 'results' here are integrands, not integrated quantities; no regularization, no DR, no Feynman-graph evaluation; correctness rests on the hidden symmetry, the duality and the OPE/light-cone structure.
      - Sufficiency of the rules is empirical; pentagon rule necessary from 9-10 loops.
  Cross-checks:
      - Consistent with the 8-loop result S1c.05_BHT2015 and earlier lower-loop integrands.
