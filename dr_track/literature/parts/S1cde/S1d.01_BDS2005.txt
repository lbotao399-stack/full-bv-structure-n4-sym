[S1d.01_BDS2005] Z. Bern, L.J. Dixon, V.A. Smirnov -- Iteration of planar amplitudes in maximally supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory at three loops and beyond (2005)
  arXiv: hep-th/0505205   Journal: Phys. Rev. D72 (2005) 085001 (from memory)
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: The planar three-loop four-gluon amplitude of N=4 SYM, built from the Bern-Rozowsky-Yan rung-rule integrand and evaluated in DR through O(eps^0), satisfies an iterative relation in terms of one- and two-loop amplitudes; this motivates the all-loop exponentiation ansatz (BDS) for planar MHV amplitudes.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops computed (planar, four-point); all-loop conjecture / planar N=4 SYM, four-gluon MHV amplitude
  Scheme: Dimensional regularization d=4-2eps for IR divergences; the unitarity method implicitly assumes a supersymmetric regulator such as the four-dimensional helicity (FDH) scheme, described as a variant of dimensional reduction (Sec. II); coupling carries eps-dependent factors and is dimensionful in d dims.
  Symmetry argument: Supersymmetry assumed preserved by FDH/DRED-type regulator (no proof given); integrand from rung rule / iterated two-particle cuts in 4d.
  Proved:
      - Two-loop iterative relation (eq. 18) verified through O(eps^0); three-loop relation (eq. 21) obtained by explicit computation through O(eps^0).
  Assumed:
      - SUSY-preserving regulator (FDH) implicit in unitarity method (Sec. II).
      - Rung-rule (BRY) integrand called an ansatz; terms from (-2eps)-dimensional loop-momentum components (mu-terms) dropped in three-particle and MHV cut analysis; a rigorous proof would need them (Sec. III).
      - All-loop exponentiation (eq. 24) is a conjecture.
  Checked partially:
      - Three-loop relation has unchecked O(eps) remainder; tennis-court integral checked only via leading s/t -> 0 behaviour.
      - Constants c1, c2 and f_2^(l) not fixed by four-point data.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. II: statement that a supersymmetric regulator such as FDH is implicit in the unitarity approach.
      - Sec. III: rung-rule integrand; mu-term contributions dropped in cut analysis.
      - Eq. (18): two-loop iteration; eq. (21): three-loop iteration with O(eps) error term; eq. (24): all-loop ansatz.
      - Text: DR explicitly breaks conformal invariance although MSYM is conformal in 4d.
  Red flags:
      - Integrand validity in d dims not checked by D-dimensional cuts in this paper (mu-terms dropped).
      - SUSY of the regulator assumed, never tested here; O(eps) pieces of lower loops enter higher-loop iteration so evanescent errors could feed in.
      - DR breaks conformal symmetry explicitly (dimensionful coupling); conformal/iterative structure only up to O(eps).
  Cross-checks:
      - Two-loop iteration (Anastasiou-Bern-Dixon-Kosower 2003) reproduced; later 4-loop test S1d.02_BCDKS2006; violation of BDS for n>=6 found later (outside this section).
