[S1e.10_AGKS2016] S.S. Aleshin, I.O. Goriachuk, A.L. Kataev, K.V. Stepanyantz -- The NSVZ scheme for N=1 SQED with N_f flavors, regularized by the dimensional reduction, in the three-loop approximation (2016)
  arXiv: 1610.08034   Journal: Phys. Lett. B764 (2017) 222 (DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2016.11.041)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED
  Claim: With DRED, the NSVZ relation does not hold for RG functions defined in terms of the bare coupling; for RG functions in terms of the renormalized coupling, boundary conditions on the renormalization constants (more complicated than in the higher-derivative case) define an NSVZ scheme at three loops; the DRED-based and HD-based NSVZ schemes are related by a finite renormalization at this order.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops (beta), 2 loops (anomalous dimension) / N=1 SQED with N_f flavours
  Scheme: DRED (superspace), renormalized via boundary conditions chosen to give NSVZ; contrasted with higher covariant derivative regularization.
  Symmetry argument: NSVZ as a scheme property: existence of finite renormalizations mapping DR-bar to an NSVZ scheme; DRED loop integrals are not double total derivatives, so the HD mechanism is mimicked only approximately.
  Proved:
      - NSVZ fails for bare-coupling RG functions in DRED at this order; NSVZ scheme for renormalized-coupling RG functions constructed at three loops (abstract).
  Assumed: (none)
  Checked partially:
      - Only three-loop SQED.
  Evidence:
      - Abstract; S1e.11_Stepanyantz2020 Sec. 1 summarizes: with DR-bar NSVZ fails starting at O(alpha^2, alpha lambda^2, lambda^4) (three-loop beta, two-loop anomalous dimension); a tuned finite renormalization restores it at least through three and four loops.
  Red flags:
      - Failure of NSVZ in DR-bar is a scheme statement, not evidence of SUSY violation by DRED; conversely, restoring NSVZ by finite renormalization is not a SUSY test.
  Cross-checks:
      - S1e.03_JJN1996c (DRED->NSVZ redefinition), S1e.11_Stepanyantz2020.
  Access / confidence: abstract only (plus summary in 2007.11935) / medium
  Notes: Lead correct.
