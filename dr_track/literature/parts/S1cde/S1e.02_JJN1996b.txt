[S1e.02_JJN1996b] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, C.G. North -- N=1 supersymmetry and the three loop gauge beta function (1996)
  arXiv: hep-ph/9606323   Journal: Phys. Lett. B386 (1996) 138
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED
  Claim: The three-loop gauge beta-function of an abelian N=1 gauge theory is computed in DRED; a coupling redefinition relating it to the NSVZ form is constructed and generalized to the non-abelian case, yielding the non-abelian three-loop DRED gauge beta-function.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops (gauge beta function) / N=1 SQED-type abelian theory with Yukawa couplings; inferred non-abelian result
  Scheme: DRED (superspace), minimal subtraction.
  Symmetry argument: SUSY-covariant superspace DRED; the non-abelian result follows from the redefinition ansatz, not from direct non-abelian graphs.
  Proved:
      - Abelian three-loop DRED gauge beta (direct computation).
  Assumed:
      - Non-abelian three-loop DRED beta inferred by extending the redefinition (not computed directly).
      - SUSY of DRED not discussed in the abstract.
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence:
      - Abstract.
  Red flags:
      - The non-abelian three-loop DRED result is an inference based on the existence of a DRED->NSVZ redefinition, i.e. it presupposes that DRED and NSVZ are SUSY schemes related by a coupling redefinition.
  Cross-checks:
      - Detailed in S1e.03_JJN1996c; later direct checks S1e.05_HJKMS2006, S1e.06_JJKM2007.
  Access / confidence: abstract only / medium
  Notes: Lead correct.
