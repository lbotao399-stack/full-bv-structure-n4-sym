[S1d.06_BCJR2012] Z. Bern, J.J.M. Carrasco, H. Johansson, R. Roiban -- The five-loop four-point amplitude of N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory (2012)
  arXiv: 1207.6666   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 109 (2012) 241602
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: The complete (full-colour) five-loop four-point N=4 SYM integrand (416 cubic graphs) is constructed with the method of maximal cuts; its UV divergence in the critical dimension D_c=26/5 is computed: for SU(Nc) it is single-trace with leading-colour and 1/Nc^2-suppressed pieces only, no double-trace terms, expressed via three positive vacuum integrals (subleading/leading ratio 45.0/Nc^2).
  Loop order / theory: 5 loops (full colour); UV pole in D=26/5 / N=4 SYM continued to D dims (16-supercharge SYM)
  Scheme: DR with eps=(26/5-D)/2; small-external-momentum expansion to about 185 vacuum diagrams reduced by consistency relations (eq. 4); vacuum integrals evaluated numerically (FIESTA).
  Symmetry argument: Maximal-cut method in D dims (cuts evaluated in D dims via auxiliary cuts); finiteness bound D < 4 + 6/L cited (eq. 1); no regulated-action SUSY argument.
  Proved:
      - Integrand matches maximal and next-to-maximal cuts down to k=3 (410, 2473, 7917, 15156 nonvanishing cuts for k=0..3).
      - UV pole in D=26/5 computed numerically (eq. 5).
  Assumed:
      - D-dimensional cut agreement not separately verified numerically like the 4d supersum checks; completeness of cuts below k=3 not shown on page.
      - Supergravity sharing the same critical dimension is a conjecture.
  Checked partially:
      - Numerical evaluation of vacuum integrals; cut checks numerical in 4d.
  Evidence:
      - Eqs. (2)-(3): 416 cubic graphs, generalized gauge freedom of numerators.
      - Eq. (4): vacuum expansion and reduction; eq. (5): divergence in terms of V(a), V(b), V(c).
      - Eq. (1): N=4 finiteness bound D < 4 + 6/L.
  Red flags:
      - UV coefficient in non-integer D=26/5 depends on the D-dimensional completeness of the integrand, which is checked on cuts but not derived from a D-dimensional regulated SUSY action.
      - Numerical integrals only.
  Cross-checks:
      - Planar part consistent with S1d.03_BCJK2007; integrand reused in N=8 five-loop analysis S1d.07_BCCJPRZ2018.
