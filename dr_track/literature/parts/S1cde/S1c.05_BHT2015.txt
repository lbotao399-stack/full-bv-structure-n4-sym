[S1c.05_BHT2015] J.L. Bourjaily, P. Heslop, V.-V. Tran -- Perturbation theory at eight loops: novel structures and the breakdown of manifest conformality in N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory (2015)
  arXiv: 1512.07912   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 116 (2016) 191602
  Section: 1c correlator integrands
  Claim: The planar 8-loop integrand of the four-point amplitude and of the four stress-tensor correlator is constructed with the soft-collinear bootstrap applied to S_{4+l}-symmetric planar f-graphs. New at eight loops: integrals finite even on-shell, and 'pseudoconformal' integrals that are conformal as integrands but divergent even off-shell, so manifest finiteness/conformality of individual terms is lost; their divergences cancel only in combination.
  Loop order / theory: 8 loops (planar); f-graph census to 10 loops (Table 1) / planar N=4 SYM; four-point amplitude and four-point 20' correlator
  Scheme: No regulator: integrand-level construction in 4d (coordinates in R^{3,1}); the correlator is finite, the amplitude IR divergent; pseudoconformal integrals must be regularized but no scheme is specified; the Higgs-branch (Alday-Henn-Plefka-Schuster) mass regulator cannot be used for them because they diverge on the Higgs branch.
  Symmetry argument: Hidden S_{4+l} symmetry and dual conformal invariance of f^(l), planarity, locality, weight -4, simple poles; dynamics from the soft-collinear bootstrap (vanishing residue of log A in the collinear limit, eq. 6).
  Proved:
      - Within the planar f-graph ansatz, the collinear residues are linearly independent enough that the soft-collinear criterion fixes all coefficients through 8 loops (Sec. III).
  Assumed:
      - Hidden symmetry, amplitude/correlator duality, locality, planarity, simple poles (Sec. II).
      - Expected but unproven graph property: every f-graph has a triangle adjacent to a square face (Sec. III).
      - The authors state it is unclear why the collinear criterion should suffice (Sec. III).
  Checked partially:
      - Using only finite integrals the bootstrap would have failed beyond 7 loops; correlator input is needed (Sec. I).
      - Alternative coincident-limit + rung-rule route claimed to fix 8 loops, left for future work (Sec. IV).
  Evidence:
      - Sec. I, eq. (1): finite conformal integrals with coefficients {-1, 1/2, 1/2, 1}; eq. (2): pseudoconformal divergent integrals.
      - Sec. II: f-graph enumeration, Table 1 through 10 loops.
      - Sec. III, eq. (6): soft-collinear bootstrap criterion; sufficiency through 8 loops by linear independence of residues.
      - Sec. IV, Table 2: new coefficients -2, -5, half-integers.
  Red flags:
      - From 8 loops on, individual integrand terms are divergent even off-shell; no regulator compatible with the symmetry used is specified (Higgs-branch regulator excluded), so the integrated correlator's finiteness relies on cancellations argued at integrand level.
      - Uniqueness of the bootstrap is empirical, with no explanation of why the criterion suffices.
      - Planar only.
  Cross-checks:
      - Reproduces the known 7-loop integrands (S1c.02_EHKS2012 lineage and amplitude constructions).
  Access / confidence: full text via ar5iv / high
  Notes: Lead correct. The brief's guesses (double poles / non-planar numerators at 8 loops) were wrong: the novelty is pseudoconformal (off-shell divergent) and on-shell finite integrals within planar graphs.
