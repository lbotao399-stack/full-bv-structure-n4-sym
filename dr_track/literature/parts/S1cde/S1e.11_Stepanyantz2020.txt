[S1e.11_Stepanyantz2020] K.V. Stepanyantz -- The all-loop perturbative derivation of the NSVZ beta-function and the NSVZ scheme in the non-Abelian case by summing singular contributions (2020)
  arXiv: 2007.11935   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C80 (2020) 911 (DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-020-8416-6; page from memory)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions (contrast: manifestly supersymmetric higher-derivative regulator)
  Claim: For general renormalizable N=1 gauge theories regularized by higher covariant derivatives in N=1 superspace (with Pauli-Villars superfields for one-loop divergences), the NSVZ relation holds to all orders for RG functions defined in terms of bare couplings, for any renormalization prescription; for RG functions in renormalized couplings, the HD+MSL prescription (higher derivatives + minimal subtraction of logarithms) is an NSVZ scheme to all orders. The proof sums singular contributions of double-total-derivative integrals (matter, ghost and gauge propagator cuts).
  Loop order / theory: all loops (perturbative proof) / general N=1 supersymmetric gauge theories (non-abelian; abelian case earlier)
  Scheme: Higher covariant derivative regularization: regulator functions R, F of (nablabar^2 nabla^2)/(16 Lambda^2) in background-field N=1 superspace (Sec. 2, eqs. 6-18); PV chiral superfields (three adjoint with mass a_phi Lambda; matter-sector PV in R_PV) with coupling-independent mass ratios.
  Symmetry argument: SUSY manifest (superspace); background gauge invariance manifest (eq. 17); quantum gauge invariance broken by gauge fixing and controlled by Slavnov-Taylor identities (Sec. 3). No continuation of dimension, so no evanescent / Fierz issues.
  Proved:
      - All-loop NSVZ for bare-coupling RG functions (eqs. 5/28, 33) given the stated inputs; HD+MSL is an NSVZ scheme.
  Assumed:
      - All-loop finiteness of triple gauge-ghost vertices, Z_alpha^{-1/2} Z_c Z_V = 1 (eq. 27; proved in earlier work of the programme).
      - No finite constants in Yukawa renormalization (eq. 26).
      - Cancellation among terms with 1/Q^{2n}, n>=2 (argued from well-definedness).
      - One-loop beta taken from earlier calculation.
  Checked partially:
      - Explicit multi-loop checks in the programme (e.g. three-loop Yukawa-dependent terms in 1908.04108, JHEP 10 (2019) 011).
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2, eqs. (6)-(18): regulator and PV fields.
      - Sec. 3: Slavnov-Taylor identities, transversality.
      - Eqs. (26)-(27): scheme conditions; Sec. 5: summation of singularities.
      - Intro: with DR-bar NSVZ fails from O(alpha^2, alpha lambda^2, lambda^4); tuned finite renormalization restores it through three and four loops; bare-coupling NSVZ never holds with DRED.
  Red flags:
      - Higher-derivative + PV regularization is manifestly SUSY and background-gauge invariant but breaks quantum BRST invariance through gauge fixing only; the all-loop proof relies on finiteness of the gauge-ghost vertex proved separately.
      - Different object from DRED: shows that a genuinely SUSY-preserving regulator exists for N=1 beta-functions, and that NSVZ-vs-DRED discrepancies are scheme artefacts.
  Cross-checks:
      - S1e.10_AGKS2016 (DRED vs HD NSVZ schemes related by finite renormalization at three loops); 1908.04108 (double total derivatives, all orders).
