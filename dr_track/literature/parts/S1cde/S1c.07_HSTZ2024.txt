[S1c.07_HSTZ2024] S. He, C. Shi, Y. Tang, Y.-Q. Zhang -- The cusp limit of correlators and a new graphical bootstrap for correlators/amplitudes to eleven loops (2024)
  arXiv: 2410.09859   Journal: JHEP 03 (2025) 192
  Section: 1c correlator integrands
  Claim: A Lagrangian-insertion argument in the double light-like (cusp) limit relates the Sudakov double-log divergence of an n-point correlator to an (n+1)-point correlator whose inserted Lagrangian pinches to the soft-collinear region; on f-graphs this becomes a 'double-triangle' pinching rule, the most constraining known. With it the planar four-point integrand is fixed through ten loops and all but one of 22,024,902 eleven-loop coefficients; the last is fixed by the triangle rule.
  Loop order / theory: 11 loops (planar), plus the 12-loop anti-prism coefficient (-42) / planar N=4 SYM; n-point stress-tensor correlators, four-point amplitude by duality
  Scheme: No regulator named: integrand-level argument; the light-like limits are the divergence-producing limits; the double log is extracted from a one-loop integral (Sec. 2, eqs. 12-16; App. A).
  Symmetry argument: Lagrangian insertion + S_n permutation symmetry, simple poles and conformal weight 4 of the n-point integrand F_n; the one-loop cusp coefficient Gamma_cusp at order a is input (taken from an earlier paper / five-point result).
  Proved:
      - Given the insertion procedure and the f-graph ansatz: the double-triangle rule follows from matching Sudakov double logs (Sec. 2, eq. 16); complete fixing through ten loops and eleven loops up to one coefficient (Secs. 1, 4.1).
  Assumed:
      - Lagrangian insertion and the superconformal / permutation structure of F_n.
      - Cusp coefficient Gamma_cusp at one loop taken as input, not derived.
      - All-loop characterization of F_n open (Sec. 5); some steps physical arguments checked up to n=14.
  Checked partially:
      - Catalan conjecture for anti-prism f-graphs checked; 12-loop anti-prism coefficient -42 from local analysis.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2, eqs. (12)-(16): cusp limit, pinching of the inserted Lagrangian, double-triangle constraint.
      - App. A: one-loop extraction of the double log.
      - Secs. 1, 4.1: ten loops complete; eleven loops all but one coefficient; triangle rule fixes the last.
      - Sec. 5: open problems including all-loop characterization.
  Red flags:
      - Sudakov divergences are used as constraints without specifying a regulator; the argument is integrand-level and relies on the Lagrangian insertion procedure with unregulated integrated insertions.
      - No DR or Feynman-graph computation; eleven-loop statement is about the integrand only.
  Cross-checks:
      - Reproduces ten-loop result of S1c.06_BHT2016.
