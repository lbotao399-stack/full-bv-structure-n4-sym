[S1d.09_BDD2014] Z. Bern, S. Davies, T. Dennen -- Enhanced ultraviolet cancellations in N=5 supergravity at four loops (2014)
  arXiv: 1409.3089   Journal: Phys. Rev. D90 (2014) 105011
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: Four-loop four-point N=5 supergravity amplitudes are UV finite in D=4, contrary to expectations from supersymmetry and duality-symmetry counterterm arguments; the cancellations ('enhanced cancellations') cannot be manifest diagram by diagram in any covariant local formalism. Three-loop N=4 and N=5 amplitudes show the same.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops (also 3 loops), D=4 / N=5 (and N=4) supergravity
  Scheme: Integrand from colour-kinematics duality; standard UV extraction with FIRE5 integral reduction; regularization not specified in the abstract.
  Symmetry argument: None beyond the double-copy construction; the result contradicts symmetry-based counterterm expectations.
  Proved:
      - UV finiteness of the computed four-loop four-point N=5 integrand in D=4.
  Assumed:
      - Correctness of the double-copy integrand.
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence:
      - Abstract: four-loop finiteness; enhanced cancellations; FIRE5.
  Red flags:
      - Shows that symmetry-based (counterterm) reasoning about divergences can fail: the vanishing is not explained by known symmetries; relevant as a caution when inferring protection from symmetry alone.
  Cross-checks:
      - Contrast with S1d.08_BDDSS2013 (N=4 diverges at four loops) and S1d.07_BCCEJPRZ2018.
  Access / confidence: abstract only / medium
  Notes: Lead correct.
