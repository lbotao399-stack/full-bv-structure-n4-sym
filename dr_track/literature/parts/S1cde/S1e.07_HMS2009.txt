[S1e.07_HMS2009] R.V. Harlander, L. Mihaila, M. Steinhauser -- The SUSY-QCD beta function to three loops (2009)
  arXiv: 0905.4807   Journal: Eur. Phys. J. C63 (2009) 383 (from memory; not shown on fetched page)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED
  Claim: A direct three-loop component-field DRED computation in SUSY-QCD gives the same beta-function from every alpha_s-type three-point vertex, including the SUSY-fixed quark-squark-gluino vertex; epsilon-scalar couplings track the gauge coupling (Z_s = Z_e through two loops by counting, explicit three-loop checks for several epsilon-scalar vertices); the gluino-mass anomalous dimension obeys gamma_n = (n+1) beta_n through three loops; the result agrees with the NSVZ-based three-loop DRED result of Jack-Jones-North. Conclusion: DRED is consistent with SUSY through three loops for these quantities.
  Loop order / theory: 3 loops / SUSY-QCD (N=1 SQCD in components) with soft gluino mass
  Scheme: Component DRED with epsilon-scalars (4-d gluon components), minimal subtraction (DR-bar); coupling renormalization from Slavnov-Taylor identities (eq. 11); single-gamma5 traces with anticommuting gamma5 and a formal trace rule (eq. 8), cancelling in the final result; Majorana fermions via flipped-fermion rules; massless propagators with small-mass cross-check.
  Symmetry argument: SUSY Ward identity tests realized as equality of renormalization constants of couplings that SUSY identifies (gauge vs quark-squark-gluino Yukawa; gauge vs epsilon-scalar couplings) and the exact gluino-mass relation; no SUSY-restoring counterterms needed.
  Proved:
      - Equality of beta-functions from all alpha_s-type vertices through three loops, including the SUSY-fixed Yukawa vertex (Intro, Sect. 3, Conclusions).
      - gamma^gluino_n = (n+1) beta_n through three loops (eq. 19) for vanishing other soft terms.
      - Agreement of eq. (16) with JJN/NSVZ-based result.
  Assumed:
      - Anticommuting gamma5 with formal trace rule (eq. 8): justified only by the observed cancellation of single-gamma5 traces and absence of higher than simple poles (Sect. 2.1).
      - Z_s = Z_e through two loops from a counting argument (three loops checked explicitly only for several vertices).
  Checked partially:
      - Only SUSY-QCD; general soft-breaking parameters not covered (Sect. 4).
      - Quartic epsilon-scalar couplings other than the four-gluon colour structure set to zero (sufficient at this order).
  Evidence:
      - Sect. 2.1, eq. (8): gamma5 trace prescription; Sect. 2.2, Table 1: Green's functions computed.
      - Sect. 3, eq. (11): ST identities for coupling renormalization; eq. (16): three-loop beta; eq. (17): quark mass anomalous dimension vs superfield result; eq. (19): gluino-mass relation.
      - Sect. 4: conclusion of SUSY consistency of DRED through three loops.
  Red flags:
      - Explicit statement of DRED-SUSY consistency is restricted to three loops and to the vertices computed.
      - Uses an anticommuting gamma5 within DRED; the possible inconsistency is evaded because single-gamma5 traces cancel, not because the prescription is consistent.
  Cross-checks:
      - Agrees with S1e.02_JJN1996b / S1e.03_JJN1996c and Pickering et al.; quark mass AD agrees with superfield result (ref. [23]).
