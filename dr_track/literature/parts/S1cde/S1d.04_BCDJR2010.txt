[S1d.04_BCDJR2010] Z. Bern, J.J.M. Carrasco, L.J. Dixon, H. Johansson, R. Roiban -- The complete four-loop four-point amplitude in N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory (2010)
  arXiv: 1008.3327   Journal: Phys. Rev. D82 (2010) 125040 (from memory)
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: The full-colour (planar + non-planar) four-loop four-point N=4 SYM integrand is given as 50 parent graphs with numerators, fixed by the method of maximal cuts; its UV divergence in the critical dimension D_c=11/2 is computed via vacuum integrals: for SU(Nc) it is single-trace, with only Nc^4 and Nc^2 terms; non-planar terms are as UV-well-behaved as planar ones; double-trace terms are better behaved.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops (full colour); UV pole in D=11/2 / N=4 SYM, general gauge group / SU(Nc), four-point amplitude, also used in D>4 as the 16-supercharge SYM
  Scheme: Integrand in D dims assumed obtained by replacing the 4d loop measure by the D-dim one (eq. 20); UV analysis in DR around D = 11/2 - 2eps (Sec. V.4); IR divergences of the small-momentum expansion handled by an off-shell momentum injection / R*-type rearrangement (eqs. 72-85).
  Symmetry argument: States organized as 10d N=1 SYM (gluon + gluino), so sewing relations hold in any D<=10 (Sec. III.1); Alday-Maldacena state-counting for intermediate supermultiplets in any D (eq. 21); BCJ numerator identities as an independent check in any dimension (Sec. II.2.2).
  Proved:
      - Integrand satisfies a spanning set of 13 basis cuts plus permutations (Sec. II.2), all two-particle and box cuts in D dims (Fig. 10).
      - UV pole at D=11/2: single-trace for SU(Nc), Nc^0 term vanishes (eq. 73); non-cancellation argued from irrational values (eq. 107).
  Assumed:
      - Not all cuts checked in D dims; D-dimensional validity assumed via measure replacement (Sec. II.2.2, eq. 20).
      - Factorized form (eq. 21) assumed in D dims from Alday-Maldacena state counting.
  Checked partially:
      - V8 evaluated numerically (Gegenbauer x-space, eqs. 97-103).
      - Four-loop double-trace divergence in D=6 not evaluated (end Sec. VI.2).
  Evidence:
      - Sec. II.1, eq. (16): 50 parent graphs; Sec. II.2.2, eq. (20): D-dim measure replacement assumption.
      - Sec. III.1, eq. (27)-(28): two-particle sewing valid in D<=10 via 10d N=1 multiplet.
      - Sec. V.4, eqs. (72)-(81): reduction to 11 vacuum integrals, colour structure of pole.
      - Eq. (107): subleading/leading ratio 44.40538395605(2)/Nc^2.
  Red flags:
      - D-dim integrand not fully verified; missing pieces that vanish in 4d but not in D=11/2 would change the UV coefficient.
      - UV computation is in a non-integer critical dimension where N=4 is the dimensionally continued 16-supercharge SYM; SUSY of that continuation is argued from 10d state sums, not from a regulated action.
  Cross-checks:
