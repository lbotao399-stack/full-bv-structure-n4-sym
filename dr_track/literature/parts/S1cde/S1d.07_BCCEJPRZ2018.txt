[S1d.07_BCCEJPRZ2018] Z. Bern, J.J.M. Carrasco, W.-M. Chen, A. Edison, H. Johansson, J. Parra-Martinez, R. Roiban, M. Zeng -- Ultraviolet properties of N=8 supergravity at five loops (2018)
  arXiv: 1804.09311   Journal: Phys. Rev. D98 (2018) 086021
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: With an integrand from the generalized double copy (built on the five-loop N=4 SYM integrand), the five-loop four-point N=8 supergravity amplitude first diverges in D_c=24/5 (D^8 R^4 counterterm); the divergence is a combination of two positive-definite vacuum-like integrals, excluding additional UV cancellations at this order.
  Loop order / theory: 5 loops; UV in D=24/5 / N=8 supergravity (dimensionally continued)
  Scheme: Dimensional regularization around the critical dimension (vacuum-integral expansion and reduction); the abstract does not specify the scheme further.
  Symmetry argument: Generalized double copy from 16-supercharge SYM numerators; no regulated-action SUSY argument.
  Proved:
      - Leading UV behaviour of the constructed integrand in D=24/5 computed (abstract).
  Assumed:
      - Correctness of the generalized-double-copy integrand in D dims (built on S1d.06_BCJR2012).
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence:
      - Abstract: D_c=24/5, D^8R^4 counterterm, two positive-definite integrals, relations to lower-loop integrals.
  Red flags:
      - Non-integer critical dimension; SUSY of the continuation inherited from the gauge-theory integrand, not from a regulator.
  Cross-checks:
      - Contrasted with enhanced cancellations in N=4 (three loops) and N=5 (four loops) supergravity (S1d.09_BDD2014).
