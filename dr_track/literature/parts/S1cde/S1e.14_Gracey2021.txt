[S1e.14_Gracey2021] J.A. Gracey -- Five loop renormalization of the Wess-Zumino model (2021)
  arXiv: 2108.13133   Journal: Phys. Rev. D105 (2022) (article number not verified)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED (Wess-Zumino model)
  Claim: Five-loop MS-bar and MOM renormalization of the Wess-Zumino model (and O(N) version) in superspace: five-loop gamma_Phi and beta (eqs. 5.1-5.4); MOM five-loop result also obtained by scheme conversion from four-loop data; four-loop beta re-verified with MINCER/FORCER; relevance for the emergent-SUSY fixed point of the Gross-Neveu-Yukawa model (eta = eps/3 exact from the Ward identity, eq. 6.10).
  Loop order / theory: 5 loops / Wess-Zumino model, O(N) Wess-Zumino model
  Scheme: N=1 superspace supergraphs with D-algebra applied graph by graph, then scalar momentum integrals in DR d=4-2eps ('used throughout'); traces from 2x2 sigma matrices mapped to a gamma-trace routine with normalization 2; MS-bar and MOM. The component Lagrangian (eq. 2.2) is not used: Sec. 2 states component calculations would need dimensional reduction because dimensional regularization does not preserve SUSY.
  Symmetry argument: SUSY Ward identity / non-renormalization Z_g Z_Phi^{3/2} = 1 (eq. 2.4), hence beta = 3 a gamma_Phi (eq. 2.5); imposed also in MOM so the scheme respects the symmetry (Sec. 5); superspace said to avoid the gamma5 issue (Sec. 1).
  Proved:
      - Five-loop gamma_Phi computed; beta follows from the Ward identity (eqs. 2.5, 5.1-5.2); RG consistency of higher poles of Z_Phi (Sec. 5).
  Assumed:
      - That superspace D-algebra followed by DR of scalar integrals preserves the non-renormalization theorem to five loops (not examined; the dimension in which D-algebra is done is not stated).
      - Single coupling: Yukawa/quartic SUSY relations are not separately testable.
  Checked partially:
      - Four-loop beta re-verified; MOM result cross-checked via conversion (eqs. 5.5-5.9); T_2^L coefficients agree with Hopf-algebra analysis (Sec. 6).
  Evidence:
      - Eq. (2.1)-(2.2): superspace and component actions; eq. (2.4)-(2.5): Ward identity.
      - Sec. 2: component calculations would require dimensional reduction since DREG does not preserve SUSY; superspace graphs evaluated in DR throughout.
      - Eqs. (5.1)-(5.9): five-loop MS-bar and MOM results; eq. (6.10): eta = eps/3.
  Red flags:
      - Highest loop order of an explicit supersymmetric DR-type computation found in this section, but in a theory with no gauge fields and a single coupling, where SUSY consistency reduces to Z_g Z_Phi^{3/2} = 1 imposed by superspace structure; no independent SUSY test is possible inside the computation.
      - D-algebra dimension unspecified; ambiguities of 4d sigma-algebra in d dims not discussed (for chiral-only theories they are believed absent).
  Cross-checks:
      - Four-loop result (ref. [6] of the paper, presumably S1e.13_AGKL1982); emergent SUSY in GNY at four loops (ref. [15] of the paper).
