[S1c.08_BHST2025] J.L. Bourjaily, S. He, C. Shi, Y. Tang -- The four-point correlator of planar sYM at twelve loops (2025)

  Section: 1c correlator integrands
  Claim: The double-triangle rule fully determines the 11-loop planar correlator and fixes all but 3 of the 619,981,403 twelve-loop f-graph coefficients (35,503,735 nonzero); the triangle rule fixes the remaining three. The double-triangle rule is argued to imply the square and pentagon rules.

  Scheme: No regularization discussed: integrand-level bootstrap using OPE and light-like divergence structures as constraints.

      - Computational result: unique solution of the double-triangle + triangle constraints on the 12-loop planar f-graph ansatz (Sec. III, Table 1).
