[S1d.05_BCDJR2012] Z. Bern, J.J.M. Carrasco, L.J. Dixon, H. Johansson, R. Roiban -- Simplifying multiloop integrands and ultraviolet divergences of gauge theory and gravity amplitudes (2012)
  arXiv: 1201.5366   Journal: Phys. Rev. D85 (2012) 105014
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: Four-loop (not five-loop) result: the complete four-loop four-point N=4 SYM integrand in colour-kinematics (BCJ) dual form (85 cubic graphs, determined by two planar master numerators); its N=8 supergravity double copy has the same manifest UV power counting; both diverge first in D=11/2, and the N=8 coefficient equals the subleading-colour single-trace N=4 coefficient up to a rational factor; the N=4 double-trace divergence appears in D=6 and cancels in D=11/2.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops (full colour); UV in D=11/2 (and double trace in D=6) / N=4 SYM (16-supercharge SYM in D dims) and N=8 supergravity
  Scheme: DR around the critical dimension D=11/2 (vacuum integrals); integrand built in D dims from BCJ duality and checked on D-dimensional unitarity cuts against the earlier representation (S1d.04_BCDJR2010).
  Symmetry argument: Colour-kinematics duality determining the integrand; the earlier four-loop representation has demonstrated validity for D<=6; three-loop numerators argued valid in D<=10 (Sec. II.3); double copy for gravity.
  Proved:
      - Exact agreement of new BCJ four-loop integrand with earlier result on D-dimensional unitarity cuts (Intro); maximal cut of graph 12, next-to-maximal of graph 14 checked (Sec. III.2).
  Assumed:
      - Validity of the D-dimensional integrand demonstrated only for D<=6 (Intro) - adequate for D=11/2.
  Checked partially:
      - UV coefficients from vacuum integrals (some numerical, inherited from S1d.04_BCDJR2010).
  Evidence:
      - Sec. III, eq. (20): 85-graph BCJ representation; Sec. III.2: masters N18, N28.
      - Sec. III.1, eq. (21): N=8 supergravity integrand via double copy.
      - Intro: four-loop N=4 representation validity for D<=6; UV in D=11/2 for both theories.
  Red flags:

      - UV statements concern the dimensionally continued 16-supercharge theory at non-integer D; SUSY of the continuation is not established by a regulated action argument.
