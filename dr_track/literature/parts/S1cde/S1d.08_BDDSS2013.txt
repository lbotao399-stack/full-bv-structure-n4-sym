[S1d.08_BDDSS2013] Z. Bern, S. Davies, T. Dennen, A.V. Smirnov, V.A. Smirnov -- Ultraviolet properties of N=4 supergravity at four loops (2013)
  arXiv: 1309.2498   Journal: Phys. Rev. Lett. 111 (2013) 231302
  Section: 1d amplitudes / UV divergences
  Claim: Pure N=4 supergravity diverges at four loops in D=4 (four-point amplitude); the form of the divergence suggests it is due to the rigid U(1) duality-symmetry anomaly.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops, D=4 / pure N=4 supergravity
  Scheme: Integrand from colour-kinematics duality (double copy of N=4 SYM with pure YM); UV divergence extracted with standard methods (DR implied; scheme not named in the abstract).
  Symmetry argument: Double copy; no statement on regulator symmetry. Divergence linked (as a suggestion) to the U(1) anomaly.
  Proved:
      - Explicit nonzero four-loop UV divergence in this computational setup.
  Assumed:
      - Interpretation via the U(1) anomaly is a suggestion, not derived.
  Checked partially: (none)
  Evidence:
      - Abstract: first UV divergence in a pure ungauged 4d supergravity; anomaly interpretation suggested.
  Red flags:
      - Later work (S1d.10_BPR2017) showed anomalous one-loop amplitudes and evanescent terms can be removed by a finite local counterterm and that the four-loop divergence was computed without it; the physical status of the divergence depends on this DR/evanescent/anomaly interplay.
  Cross-checks:
      - Re-examination called for in 1712.03928 (S1d.10_BPR2017); evanescent/anomaly link 1706.01486.
  Access / confidence: abstract only / medium
  Notes: Included because it is the cleanest example where a DR UV result is entangled with an anomaly and evanescent operators.
