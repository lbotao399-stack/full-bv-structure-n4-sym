[S1e.06_JJKM2007] I. Jack, D.R.T. Jones, P. Kant, L. Mihaila -- The four-loop DRED gauge beta-function and fermion mass anomalous dimension for general gauge groups (2007)
  arXiv: 0707.3055   Journal: JHEP 0709 (2007) 058 (from memory; not shown on fetched page)
  Section: 1e N=1 beta functions in DRED
  Claim: Four-loop DRED gauge beta-function and fermion mass anomalous dimension for a general gauge group with two-component fermions in an arbitrary representation (no physical scalars), including the evanescent epsilon-scalar Yukawa and quartic couplings (one-loop betas for SU(N), SO(N), Sp(N)); specialized to N=1 SYM (adjoint fermion, no superpotential) the four-loop NSVZ-derived DRED gauge beta is verified, and the gaugino-mass beta obeys the exact spurion relation through the same order.
  Loop order / theory: 4 loops (beta_s, gamma_m); beta_e to 3 loops; quartic evanescent betas to 1 loop / general gauge group with fermions in representation R; N=1 SYM special case
  Scheme: DRED (DR-bar) with epsilon-scalars W_sigma as internal-only evanescent components of the gauge field carrying independent couplings g_e, lambda_r (Sec. 2, eq. 12); four-loop DRED from four-loop MS-bar via the indirect method (eq. 62) with three-loop conversion relations (eqs. 63-65); three-loop beta_e computed with QGRAF/q2e/exp/MINCER (eq. 61).
  Symmetry argument: N=1 SYM limit: setting evanescent couplings to SUSY values (v3 = alpha_e = alpha_s, v1=v2=v4=0) the epsilon-scalar quartic beta equals the SUSY gauge beta at one loop (eq. 42); four-loop gauge beta and gaugino-mass beta compared with exact SUSY relations.
  Proved:
      - Four-loop DRED gauge beta for N=1 SYM without superpotential agrees with the NSVZ-derived prediction (Intro/Sec. 5, refs. [6,7]); gaugino-mass beta consistent with the exact spurion relation (ref. [8]) at the same order.
  Assumed:
      - DRED <-> DREG equivalence via coupling reparametrization including evanescent couplings (indirect method).
  Checked partially:
      - Only the case without superpotential (pure SYM) is checked at four loops; general-representation fermion box only explicit for adjoint and fundamental (Sec. 3.1.1).
      - Evanescent quartic betas only at one loop.
  Evidence:
      - Sec. 2, eqs. (1)-(4), (12): Lagrangian and epsilon-scalars.
      - Sec. 3.1.1, eq. (42): SUSY limit of quartic evanescent beta.
      - Eq. (54): one-loop evanescent betas for classical groups.
      - Sec. 4.1, eqs. (61)-(67): three-loop beta_e, indirect method, four-loop results.
  Red flags:
      - As in S1e.05_HJKMS2006, the four-loop DRED result is derived from MS-bar plus three-loop conversion, not from four-loop DRED diagrams; agreement with NSVZ+redefinition is a non-trivial consistency test for SYM without superpotential only.
  Cross-checks:
      - S1e.04_JJP1998 (four-loop prediction), S1e.05_HJKMS2006.
