Track A: IP0/IP1 — the one-loop two-point functions of the scalar bilinears, the radial Gram matrices, and the Hermiticity of the one-loop radial form

Scope (milestone 1 of Track A, CONTRIBUTING §4): the first two stages of the inner-product engine plan (IP0, IP1), the gap items IPC2-01 (beta_2 carried symbolically), IPC2-10 (Hermiticity) and IPC2-22 (the two constants rested on one derivation), and the Track A task "prove or refute Hermiticity of the one-loop radial form".

What was established
- The one-loop two-point function of the scalar bilinears O_ab = ∫ω(ι_a A, ι_b A) at separated points, in the minimal scheme and at T = ∞, by two independent engines that agree entry by entry on all 48 per-class coefficients (qa/comparison.json):
  <O1,O2>(x) = (4ħ dim g S12/(16π⁴|x|⁴)) [1 + unit (27/2 + 4 log 3/2 − 8β₂)] + (32ħ dim g T12/(16π⁴|x|⁴)) unit (log(|x|²/ℓ²) + γ_E − 3 log 2),
  with S12 = (a1,a2)(b1,b2) + (a1,b2)(b1,a2), T12 = (a1,b1)(a2,b2), unit = ħ tr_g(ad ad)/(16π²(X,Y)) = −g²N/(32π²), and β₂ the coefficient (in units) of the Q-closed direction ∫(dA_⊥, dA_⊥) in the arity-zero finite counterterm. The minimal-scheme radial constants of the 20' and of Konishi, 27/2 + 4 log 3/2 and 27/2 + 4 log 3/2 + 24(γ_E − 3 log 2), are confirmed; each now shifts by −8β₂. Per class (relative to the tree, in units): SE = 43/2 + 8γ_E − 24 log 2 + 8 log x², B = −8β₂, EX = −8 − 4γ_E + 8 log 2 + 4 log 3 − 4 log x² + 4 log ε, QU = (2τ − 1)(4 log x² − 4 log ε + 4γ_E − 12 log 2), OC = −8(1 − τ) log ε, τ = T12/S12; log ε cancels, the log x² coefficient is −½ D₁ (0 on the 20', 24 on Konishi), γ_E cancels on the 20', no other flavour structure.
- Engine A (collab/smilezzm/A/engine_a, exact, sympy): dimensionally reduced 10d Feynman rules, Schwinger masters with the heat-kernel cutoff, the numerator identity (k+k')·(2q−k−k') = 2q² − k² − … with |k|²D̂(k) = e^{−εk²}, a localisation lemma for the heat-kernel contacts (constant log(2/3)/(16π²)), and the Fourier lemma; 94 s, 87 MB. Engine B (engine_b, built by a separate agent with no access to engine A): canonical su(N) rules, explicit Wick enumeration with su(2)/su(3) structure constants and random flavour vectors, explicit 16×16 chiral gamma matrices for the Majorana–Weyl loop, position-space Schwinger representation with exact Gaussian vertex integration and Hepp-sector extraction of the ε → 0 asymptotics; the exchange constant is identified by PSLQ at 12 digits (closed form not derived there) and checked by finite-ε extrapolation to 6 digits; 210 s, 632 MB. Both pass the gates G1–G8 (self-energy singular parts graph by graph against the Chapter 4 table, operator counterterm against Chapter 5, log ε cancellation, Konishi coefficient 24, γ_E cancellation, flavour structures, Gram Hermiticity/positivity, colour).
- IP0 on the scalar family: the inversion acts as (O_ab(x))^{Θ_I} = (x,x)^{−2} O_{āb̄}(I(x)); the radial Gram matrix of the twenty-one bilinears at the origin is the conjugated two-point coefficient at unit separation (Chapter 20, Proposition c20-gram); Gram_tree is Hermitian positive definite (eigenvalues 1 (×20) and 2 (×1) in units of 4ħ dim g/(16π⁴)), Gram_1 is Hermitian for real β₂, and the 20' and Konishi blocks are orthogonal.
- Hermiticity (Chapter 20, Proposition c20-herm and Theorem c20-hermrad): in a reflection-compatible scheme the reflection form ⟨a^{Θ_n}, b⟩ is Hermitian at every order at which the regularised theory is reflection invariant (proof from thm:c8-fields (ii), Θ_n² = 1 and the graded symmetry of the two-point function); with the two-point Ward identity along P_n − K_n the one-loop radial form is Hermitian, so Gram_tree and Gram_1 are Hermitian and in cor:c8-gram the relation for X^† is the adjoint of that for X. Corollary: in the scalar sector Gram_1 is Hermitian if and only if β₂ is real (anti-Hermitian part −8i Im β₂ · unit · Gram_tree); the Hermiticity gate of IPC2-10 tests exactly the reflection compatibility of the two-leg finite counterterm.
